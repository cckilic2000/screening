## Executive Summary

GGGI Jordan Office is seeking proposals from eligible firms to facilitate the Water Sector Pipeline Development Training Program under the Enhancing Jordan's Water Sector Access to Climate Finance project. The assignment focuses on operationalizing a capacity building training roadmap, preparing bilingual curriculum materials, developing a Concept Note Guidance Toolkit, and co-facilitating workshops and drafting clinics. The tender submission deadline is 29 July 2026 16:00 KST.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The project responds to Jordan's water scarcity and climate-finance mobilization challenges. The source page states that the training roadmap addresses strategy, knowledge, pipeline, and private sector engagement gaps. The selected consulting firm will prepare bilingual curriculum materials for nine modules, develop a Concept Note Guidance Toolkit, deliver workshops and drafting clinics, provide on-the-job training recommendations for MWI and WAJ focal points, and produce a final reporting and handover package.

## Key Deliverables

- Bilingual English and Arabic comprehensive curriculum materials for nine modules
- Concept Note Guidance Toolkit
- In-person workshops and drafting clinics based on the nine-module curriculum
- Pre- and post-tests for each training session
- One final training report following completion
- On-the-job training recommendations for Ministry of Water and Irrigation and Water Authority of Jordan focal points
- Final reporting and handover package

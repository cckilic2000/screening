## Executive Summary

HaDEA has published Prior Information Notice HADEA/2026/OP/0041-PIN for a future call for tenders under the Better Training for Safer Food initiative. The call will concern organization and implementation of training activities in food, feed, animal by-products, and food processing technologies. The notice was published on 8 July 2026 and invites interested parties to check the Funding and Tenders Portal for the upcoming call.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The page explains that Better Training for Safer Food is a European Commission initiative to improve knowledge and implementation of EU rules covering food safety, plant, animal, and One Health. The basic training principles include information delivery, knowledge sharing, networking, and a train-the-trainer approach. Training may be through face-to-face courses, virtual classrooms, or eLearning.

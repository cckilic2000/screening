## Executive Summary

ILO has issued a call for implementing partners under reference ILO/SYR/IA/2026-04 for a Syria-focused MSME and start-up support assignment. The page states that the selected NGO/INGO will deliver SIYB-based entrepreneurship support, financial education, tailored advisory services, bank-account support, and access-to-finance referrals. The intervention focuses on Aleppo, Rural Damascus, Idleb, and Hama, with proposals due by 27-Jul-2026 23:59 GMT+3.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment aims to strengthen sustainable and market-oriented Business Development Service provision in Syria. It targets 1,000 aspiring and existing entrepreneurs, MSMEs, and start-ups, including returnees, IDPs, women, youth, and host communities. Activities include outreach and enrolment, diagnostics, training needs assessment, business management and entrepreneurship support, financial education, coaching, mentoring, formal financial institution engagement, and loan-referral support.

## Key Deliverables

- Inclusive outreach and enrolment of eligible MSMEs and start-ups
- Business diagnostics, training needs assessments, and market segmentation exercises
- Gender-responsive business management and entrepreneurship support activities for 1,000 MSMEs and start-ups
- Financial Education training for 500 selected MSMEs and start-ups
- Post-training coaching, mentoring, and tailored business advisory support
- Support for 500 MSMEs and start-ups to open bank accounts or engage formal financial institutions
- Investment readiness assessment and referral of at least 350 eligible entrepreneurs to partner financial institutions

## Capacity Building Components

- Start and Improve Your Business programme
- Financial Education training
- Training needs assessments
- Post-training coaching and mentoring
- Tailored business advisory support
- Strengthening local service delivery capacities
- Improving coordination among enterprise support actors

## Team Requirements

The project must be implemented by a non-profit organization/entity. The page requires institutional capacity, presence, and operational experience in Syria; experience in BDS, entrepreneurship support, MSME recovery, livelihoods, market systems, or economic inclusion; experience with entrepreneurship coaching, mentoring, market linkages, investment readiness, and access to finance referrals; and a qualified team including a Team Leader, MEAL Officer, and technical experts/coaches.

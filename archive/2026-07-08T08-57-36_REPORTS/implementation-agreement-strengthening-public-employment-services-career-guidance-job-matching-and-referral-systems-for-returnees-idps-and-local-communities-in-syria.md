## Executive Summary

ILO has published call for implementing partners ILO/SYR/IA/2026-03 to strengthen public employment services, career guidance, job matching, and referral systems in Syria. The assignment will support 3,200 job seekers through a structured two-day employment services package and related employer engagement activities. Proposals are due by 27-Jul-2026 23:59 GMT+3 / 27 July 2026, 11:59 pm Damascus time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The objective is to support Syrian returnees, IDPs, and vulnerable local populations into decent employment and self-employment opportunities. The intervention will operate through Public Employment Services and DoSAL centres under MoSAL, TVET centres, UNHCR community centres, NGOs, youth centres, community centres, and partner facilities. Major activities include career guidance, job-search support, rights-at-work awareness, referral systems, employer linkages, and job fairs.

## Key Deliverables

- Employability and career guidance support for target groups
- Career planning, CV preparation, interview skills, job search techniques, and individual action planning
- Awareness on rights at work, decent work principles, occupational safety and health, non-discrimination, and fair working conditions
- Strengthened community-level job matching and referral systems
- Linkages between job seekers and employers
- 16 job fairs, career days, employer networking events, and outreach activities

## Capacity Building Components

- Structured two-day integrated employment services package
- Career guidance support
- Job-search skills support
- Community-level job matching and referral systems
- Coordination with PES/DoSAL centres, TVET institutions, youth centres, community hubs, and local referral mechanisms

## Team Requirements

The project must be implemented by a legally registered national NGO or international NGO/INGO authorized to operate in Syria. The page requires active operational presence and implementation track record in Aleppo, Hama, and Idleb; minimum seven years of experience in employment promotion, labour market interventions, job creation, employability support, livelihoods, or income-generating programmes; experience with vulnerable groups; coordination experience with MoSAL, PES/DoSAL, TVET institutions, chambers of commerce, employers, humanitarian actors, and local stakeholders; financial reliability; and qualified management, technical, administrative, and field personnel.

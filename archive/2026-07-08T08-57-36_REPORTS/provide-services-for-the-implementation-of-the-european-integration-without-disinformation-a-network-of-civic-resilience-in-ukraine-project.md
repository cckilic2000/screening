## Executive Summary

The Delegation of the European Union to Ukraine has launched an expression of interest for services under the project "European Integration Without Disinformation: A Network of Civic Resilience in Ukraine." The contract value is stated as 100,000 EUR - 300,000 EUR, and the place of performance is Ukraine. The project combines media monitoring, capacity building, community-led initiatives, and strategic communication, with expressions of interest due by 21/07/2026.

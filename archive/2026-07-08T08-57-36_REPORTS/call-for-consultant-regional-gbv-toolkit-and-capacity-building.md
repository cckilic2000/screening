## Executive Summary

ABAAD is seeking an external consultant or team for a regional GBV Toolkit and Capacity Building consultancy under a BMZ-funded project implemented with Terre des Hommes Deutschland E.V. The assignment covers five MENA countries and includes assessment, toolkit development, capacity-building plans, training workshops, and national network activation. The duration is 18 months, approximately 60 working days, with proposals due by July 20, 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultancy aims to improve the quality and consistency of GBV shelter services in Lebanon, Jordan, OPT/West Bank, Tunisia, and Morocco. The workstreams cover regional needs and capacity assessment, GBV Resource Toolkit development, capacity-building planning and training delivery, and national network activation. The expected impact stated by the page is improved quality and consistency of services provided in GBV shelters across the five countries.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception Report
- Regional Needs and Capacity Assessment Report
- Draft GBV Resource Toolkit
- Final GBV Resource Toolkit in print-ready format
- Five tailored capacity-building plans
- Training delivery across the five countries
- Training reports for each country
- National Network Mapping and Activation Report
- End-of-assignment report

## Capacity Building Components

- Capacity-building initiative for GBV shelters
- Five country-specific capacity-building plans
- Training curricula, materials, and tools
- Training workshops for approximately 75 staff members
- Pre- and post-training assessments
- Post-training recommendations
- National-level network activation or strengthening

## Team Requirements

The consultant/firm must have an advanced university degree in gender studies, social sciences, international development, public health, human rights, or a related discipline; at least 10 years of GBV programming experience; expertise in GBV shelter management and survivor-centered approaches with direct experience in at least two target countries; experience in institutional and capacity assessments; toolkit/SOP/guidance development; capacity-building programme design and delivery; knowledge of international GBV frameworks; MENA legal and institutional familiarity; multi-country partner coordination; English writing and communication; Arabic fluency; French as a strong advantage; and ability to travel. Firms must indicate team composition, roles, and relevant experience.

## Executive Summary

EBRD has published a General Procurement Notice for Jordan: NEPCO Northern Green Substation, project ID 53334. The fetched page states an estimated total project value of 38,000,000.00 USD, funded by a USD 28,250,000 EBRD loan and USD 6,750,000 EU NIP grant. Procurement includes works and consultancy components, including construction supervision/PIU support, ESAP implementation support, inclusion support, and gender inclusion technical cooperation.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The project introduces a new 400kV substation to Jordan's national grid in an area with existing transmission bottlenecks. The procurement package list includes civil works and switchgears, transformers and reactors, construction supervision consultant and PIU support, ESAP implementation support, inclusion TI implementation support, and gender inclusion technical cooperation.

The notice is for information purposes and cannot be responded to directly. The page states that procurement will be conducted through ECEPP and that registered entities will be notified when procurement opportunities are published.

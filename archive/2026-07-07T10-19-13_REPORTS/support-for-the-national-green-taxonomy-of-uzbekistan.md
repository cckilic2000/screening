## Executive Summary

UNDP Uzbekistan is procuring qualified consulting firms to support the National Green Taxonomy of Uzbekistan. The assignment includes operationalization, refinement, roadmap development, piloting on public investment projects, and capacity building for government stakeholders. The page gives reference number UNDP-UZB-01894 and a deadline of 21-Jul-26 at 07:00 AM New York time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The objective is to provide methodological and advisory support for Uzbekistan's National Green Taxonomy. The scope includes policy and regulatory advisory services, refinement of the taxonomy, and creation of an implementation roadmap.

The page states that the taxonomy will be piloted on public investment projects and that government stakeholders will receive capacity building. It encourages firms with expertise in green finance, sustainable finance, environmental policy, taxonomy development, and advisory services for governments or international organizations.

## Key Deliverables

- Methodological and advisory support for operationalization of the National Green Taxonomy.
- Refinement of the Green Taxonomy.
- Development of an implementation roadmap.
- Piloting the taxonomy on public investment projects.
- Capacity building for government stakeholders.

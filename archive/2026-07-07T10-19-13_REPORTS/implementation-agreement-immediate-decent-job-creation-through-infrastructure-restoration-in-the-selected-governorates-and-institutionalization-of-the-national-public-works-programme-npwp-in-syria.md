## Executive Summary

ILO has issued call for implementing partners ILO/SYR/IA/2026-02 for immediate decent job creation through infrastructure restoration and institutionalization of a National Public Works Programme in Syria. The intervention promotes employment creation, skills development, enterprise recovery, financial inclusion, labour market governance, and capacity building. The deadline is 31-Jul-2026 at 23:59 GMT+2.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The objective is to support the Government of Syria and national stakeholders in designing, institutionalizing, and implementing a future National Public Works Programme that promotes Decent Work, accelerates recovery, and contributes to inclusive economic development. The intervention is described as a pilot and demonstration mechanism that can build foundations for a nationally owned public works programme.

The scope includes support to NPWP design and institutionalization, strengthening national and local capacities, demonstrating employment-intensive public works approaches, creating decent employment opportunities, supporting economic inclusion, rehabilitating priority infrastructure, and generating evidence for scale-up. Major outputs include manuals, guidelines, safeguards, M&E framework, grievance mechanisms, maintenance guidance, and a Training-of-Trainers package.

## Key Deliverables

- Public Works Programme Implementation Manual.
- Beneficiary Selection and Targeting Guidelines.
- Labour-Intensive Works Methodology Guide.
- Gender and Disability Inclusion Guidelines.
- Occupational Safety and Health Procedures.
- Environmental and Social Safeguards Checklist.
- Monitoring and Evaluation Framework.
- Community Engagement and Grievance Mechanism Procedures.
- Asset Maintenance and Sustainability Guidelines.
- Training-of-Trainers Package for Municipalities and Local Communities.

## Capacity Building Components

- Strengthen national and local capacities for public works implementation.
- Training-of-Trainers Package for Municipalities and Local Communities.
- Institutional capacity to address gender dynamics, social mainstreaming, and inclusive participation.
- Knowledge generation for scale-up.
- Implementation tools and pilot initiatives for future public works programming.

## Team Requirements

The page requires a registered non-profit organization/entity authorized to operate in Syria, strong field presence and network footprint in the selected Governorate, documented partnerships with localized community entities, more than 5 years of donor-funded employment and livelihoods programme experience, experience with UN agencies/INGOs as an advantage, verifiable corporate background with international donors, civil engineering capacity, adequate qualified human resources, a project coordinator with at least 5 years of project management experience, legal advisor/experts with at least 10 years in institutional support/governance/PWP framework activities, civil engineers with at least 3 years in infrastructure projects, gender/social mainstreaming capacity, and Arabic/English reporting capacity.

## Executive Summary

ABAAD seeks an external consultant or team for a regional GBV Toolkit and Capacity Building consultancy. The assignment covers five MENA countries and includes needs assessment, operational toolkit development, training design and delivery, and support for national network activation. The page states a closing date of 20 Jul 2026 and an assignment duration of 18 months with approximately 60 working days.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The objective is to improve the quality and consistency of GBV shelter services in Lebanon, Jordan, OPT/West Bank, Tunisia, and Morocco. The work starts with an assessment of regional GBV shelter actors, including service models, staffing, referral mechanisms, technical capacities, and the legal and policy environment.

The consultant or team will create standardized guidance for GBV shelter operations, including case management, risk assessment, confidentiality, safety planning, intake, documentation, staff roles, quality assurance, and practical templates. The assignment also includes country-specific capacity-building plans, training workshops, pre/post assessments, training reports, and support to national network mapping and activation.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report with methodology, analytical framework, data collection tools, work plan, and approach for engaging regional partners.
- Regional needs and capacity assessment report for GBV shelter actors across the five target countries.
- Draft GBV Resource Toolkit with standardized operational and programmatic guidance.
- Final GBV Resource Toolkit in print-ready format.
- Five country-specific capacity-building plans.
- Training delivery across the five countries for approximately 75 staff members.
- Training reports for each country.
- National Network Mapping and Activation Report.
- End-of-assignment report.

## Capacity Building Components

- Development of five tailored capacity-building plans.
- Design of training curricula, materials, and tools aligned with the toolkit.
- Training workshops in Tunisia, Morocco, Jordan, Lebanon, and arrangements for OPT participants.
- Pre- and post-training assessments.
- Support for national-level network activation and coordination mechanisms.
- Recommendations for sustained coordination beyond the project period.

## Team Requirements

The page requires an advanced university degree in a relevant field, at least 10 years of professional GBV programming experience, GBV shelter management expertise, experience in at least two target countries, capacity assessment experience, toolkit/SOP development experience, training curriculum and facilitation experience, strong knowledge of international GBV standards, English writing skills, Arabic fluency, and ability to travel. If applying as a firm, the proposal must state team composition, roles, and relevant experience.

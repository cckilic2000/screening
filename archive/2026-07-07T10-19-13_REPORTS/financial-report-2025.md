## Executive Summary

WHH Iraq has published tender IRQ-2026-RFQ-005 for Financial Report 2025. The assignment seeks an independent certified accountant or accounting company to prepare the annual financial report required by the Directorate of Non-Governmental Organizations in Baghdad. The page states a submission deadline of 2026-07-14 at 23:00 Iraqi time and a two-week delivery period after WHH provides the relevant documents.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The purpose is to prepare the 2025 annual financial report for Welthungerhilfe Iraq in line with generally accepted auditing and accounting standards in Iraq. The report must cover program activities and project financial values, staff salaries, social security and income tax deductions, funds received, expenditure schedules, bank and cash balances, and any annexes or notes required by DNGO instructions.

The supplier must submit expression-of-interest materials, including NGO auditing experience, proposed team composition and qualifications, company profile with previous DNGO reports, proposed number of days, financial proposal, supplier declaration, and signed TOR.

## Key Deliverables

- 2025 annual financial report for WHH.
- Statement of program activities and project financial values.
- Statement of staff salaries, social security, and income taxes.
- Statement of funds received.
- Schedules of expenditures.
- Bank and cash balances.
- Applicable annexes and notes as per DNGO instructions.
- Six copies of the report.
- Modifications required by new federal government instructions after submission, without additional costs on the first accepted offer.

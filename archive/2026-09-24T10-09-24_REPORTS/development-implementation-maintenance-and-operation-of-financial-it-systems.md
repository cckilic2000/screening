## Relevant Sector Analysis

- DIMOS VII was a large-scale IT-services framework for EU financial information systems.
- Lot 1 focused on SAP-based systems, including the SUMMA platform built on SAP S/4HANA and Fiori.
- Lot 2 focused on interoperable financial systems and non-SAP applications, interfaces, middleware, APIs, reporting, analytics, and workflows.
- Services covered design, development, configuration, integration, testing, deployment, maintenance, support, operations, data migration, documentation, security, and performance.
- The systems support accounting, budgeting, treasury, procurement, financial reporting, and regulatory compliance.
- The contract primarily concerns IT delivery rather than financial-sector development or advisory services.

## Executive Summary

The European Commission published DIMOS VII as a EUR 700 million, two-lot framework for development, implementation, maintenance, and operation of financial IT systems. The scope covered SAP S/4HANA and Fiori, interoperable financial applications, custom software, analytics, APIs, data migration, testing, security, and operational support. Bidder requirements included very high turnover, large recent EU delivery references, and extensive expert-profile CV matrices. Both lots were cancelled on 11 September 2026 because of a material compliance issue.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The framework's objective was to provide specialised services for the design, development, configuration, integration, testing, deployment, maintenance, support, and evolution of financial information systems. Lot 1 addressed SAP-based systems, especially SUMMA, the corporate platform supporting financial, accounting, and budgetary processes. Lot 2 addressed financial interoperable systems, including custom applications, web solutions, reporting and analytics, workflow, middleware, APIs, and data-integration services.

Specific contracts could require architecture, business analysis, functional consulting, software development, AI, testing, access management, interoperability, system and database administration, IT operations, security, data migration, technical documentation, and quality assurance. Work could be delivered under time-and-means, quoted time-and-means, or fixed-price arrangements from EU-based locations.

Expected impact was continuity, modernisation, harmonisation, reliability, interoperability, data quality, security, and regulatory compliance across participating authorities' financial IT landscape.

## Key Deliverables

- System and application architecture, designs, and technical analyses.
- Configured, integrated, tested, and deployed SAP and non-SAP solutions.
- Software components, interfaces, APIs, middleware, workflows, reports, and analytics.
- Data-migration, quality-assurance, security, access-control, and testing outputs.
- Corrective, adaptive, and evolutive maintenance and operational-support services.
- Technical and user documentation and task-specific handover materials.
- Deliverables defined in quoted-time-and-means and fixed-price specific contracts.
- Monthly registration and progress reports, service reporting, KPI reporting, and project-completion reports.

## Capacity Building Components

- At least four days of IT training per year for contractor staff working under time-and-means contracts.
- In-house SAP self-training capacity for relevant contractor personnel.
- Customer-provided functional, technical, or internal-procedure training where required.
- Replacement-personnel training, onboarding, handover, and knowledge transfer for service continuity.
- Training of expert users may be required under some functional-consultant profiles.
- A structured beneficiary capacity-building programme: COULDN'T BE FOUND.

## Team Requirements

Lot 1 required average annual turnover above EUR 70 million; Lot 2 required above EUR 110 million, with combined thresholds where both lots were awarded. Each lot required a recent two-year English-language financial-IT contract for an international client with more than 500 staff, involving at least 75 FTEs supplied in the EU.

Lot 1 profiles included SAP architects, project managers, business analysts, functional consultants, developers, BI developers, technical experts, AI consultants, testing and authorisation experts, interoperability specialists, system administrators, database administrators, and SAP operations experts. Lot 2 required a broad interoperable-systems profile pool covering IT operations, AI, architecture, project and programme management, analysis, development, testing, data, security, user experience, and related functions. Detailed education, certification, specialisation, and experience levels reached senior thresholds of approximately 12 to 13 years. English and international working-context experience were required at relevant levels.

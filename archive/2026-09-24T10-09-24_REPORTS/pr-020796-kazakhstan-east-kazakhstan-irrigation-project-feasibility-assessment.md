## Executive Summary

EBRD is procuring a EUR 700,000, 20-week feasibility assessment for irrigation and water infrastructure in Abai and East Kazakhstan. The assignment combines technical and economic due diligence, ESIA, and accelerated procurement support, all to be implemented concurrently. It includes financial modelling, tariff and affordability analysis, institutional and regulatory diagnostics, climate assessment, and IFI/FIDIC procurement. The breadth, bilingual requirements, and accelerated schedule make this a demanding multidisciplinary opportunity.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The proposed project comprises 22 to 24 subprojects involving rehabilitation and modernisation of irrigation, reservoir, dam, canal, and flood-protection infrastructure, including two new reservoirs expected to bring approximately 11,690 hectares into irrigation. The consultant must assess technical, economic, financial, green, environmental, social, and institutional viability and prepare the documentation needed for EBRD approval.

Technical-economic work includes engineering, climate mitigation and adaptation, farm economics, tariffs, subsidies, affordability, cost recovery, financial modelling, water accounting, data governance, institutional diagnostics, and reform programming. The ESIA workstream covers environmental and social due diligence, biodiversity, health and safety, labour, stakeholder engagement, land acquisition, and resettlement instruments. The procurement workstream supports works procurement and selection of implementation-support and technical-supervision consultants under IFI and FIDIC rules.

Expected impact is an investment-ready, environmentally and socially compliant programme with clear financing, procurement, governance, and sector-reform foundations.

## Team Requirements

The technical-economic team includes a senior Project Manager, Irrigation Engineer, Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation Specialist, Irrigation Economist or Tariff and Affordability Specialist or Financial Modeller, Data and Water-Accounting Specialist, Senior Economic Governance or IWRM Institutional Specialist, National Gender Specialist, and other technical experts. Relevant thresholds range from approximately seven to fifteen years, with Central Asia or Kazakhstan experience required or preferred depending on the role.

A separate multidisciplinary ESIA team must include a Project Manager, Environmental Specialist, Biodiversity Specialist, Health and Safety Specialist, Senior Social Specialist, and other E&S personnel. Advanced procurement requires a Project Manager, Contracts and Procurement Manager, Procurement or Contracts Specialist, and procurement engineers with IFI and FIDIC experience. English and Russian fluency are required for all key personnel; Kazakh is advantageous for some procurement roles. The consultant must demonstrate simultaneous resourcing and coordination of all three workstreams.

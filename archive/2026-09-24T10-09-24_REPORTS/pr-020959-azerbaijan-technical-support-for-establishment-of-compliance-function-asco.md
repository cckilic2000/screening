## Executive Summary

EBRD is procuring a EUR 175,000 consultancy to establish an independent compliance function at Azerbaijan Caspian Shipping Company. The 12-month assignment covers ABC and sanctions risk assessment, control review, Chief Compliance Officer role design, policies, procedures, reporting lines, implementation, and training. The required team combines international compliance and corporate-governance expertise with shipping-sector sanctions knowledge and Azerbaijani legal expertise. This is a strong institutional-strengthening and governance opportunity, but the specialist combination is demanding.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

ASCO is Azerbaijan's state-owned shipping company and operates under AZCON, the state holding created to oversee transport and telecommunications SOE transformation. ASCO intends to improve corporate governance and manage anti-bribery, anti-corruption, sanctions, and broader integrity risks through a comprehensive and independent compliance function.

The consultant will assess primary and secondary ABC and sanctions risks, review existing controls, establish the role and reporting lines of a Chief Compliance Officer, support recruitment or appointment, and prepare the policies and procedures needed for an effective function. EBRD expects implementation support and practical training, not solely policy drafting.

The expected impact is a sustainable compliance function with clearer accountability, stronger internal controls, improved sanctions and integrity risk management, and enhanced SOE governance.

## Team Requirements

The consultant must be a reputable international firm or consortium with integrity and sanctions-compliance expertise and experience implementing related policies and procedures in a public-sector context. The proposed team must include a Project Manager or Technical Expert in compliance and corporate governance with essential Caucasus experience; a Compliance Expert with at least ten years' experience and SOE implementation credentials; a Sanctions Compliance Expert with shipping-sector experience; and a Local Legal Expert experienced in Azerbaijani integrity and compliance issues.

All key experts must be fluent in written and spoken English; Azerbaijani is an advantage. One expert may fill more than one role if all requirements and availability are demonstrated. Additional team members may be proposed.

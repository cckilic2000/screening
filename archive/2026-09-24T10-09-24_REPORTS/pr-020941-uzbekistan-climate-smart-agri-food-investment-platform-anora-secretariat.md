## Relevant Sector Analysis

- ANORA is a government-led platform for mobilising climate-smart agrifood investment in Uzbekistan.
- The platform targets EUR 1.5 billion in investment mobilisation by 2030.

## Executive Summary

EBRD is procuring a USD 2.2 million consultancy to operate the ANORA Secretariat for 48 months. ANORA aims to mobilise EUR 1.5 billion for climate-smart agrifood investments in Uzbekistan through project preparation, matchmaking, technical assistance, financing partnerships, carbon-market support, and policy dialogue. The Secretariat will maintain the investment plan and pipeline, coordinate government and development partners, mobilise finance, manage knowledge and M&E, and report results. This is a direct climate- and sustainable-finance opportunity with strong institutional-strengthening and investment-promotion content.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

ANORA responds to Uzbekistan's emissions-intensive and water-stressed agricultural system and supports a transition toward low-carbon, resilient, inclusive, and competitive agrifood value chains. By 2030, the platform targets water-saving technology over 340,000 hectares, EUR 1.5 billion in mobilised investment, substantial GHG reductions and water savings, and increased agricultural output.

The Secretariat will coordinate stakeholders and policy dialogue; develop a pipeline of climate-smart investments; facilitate project preparation; match projects with financiers and TA; update the investment plan annually; maintain knowledge sharing and M&E; report results; promote the platform; and help programme donor-funded TA. Financing and partnership work covers MDBs, IFIs, climate funds, donors, concessional windows, blended-finance instruments, and private-sector finance.

Expected impact includes better-prepared projects, more efficient access to finance and technical assistance, stronger partnerships, improved policy foundations, greater investment mobilisation, and sustainable institutional coordination.

## Team Requirements

The team must combine local and international expertise. Required positions are a Programme Manager or Team Leader with preferably 15 or more years in programme management, financial structuring, and climate-related investment; an Economic or Investment Expert with preferably ten or more years in agrifood finance, sustainable finance, and climate-smart agriculture; Local Technical Expert or Experts with deep Uzbekistan agrifood knowledge and preferably seven or more years' experience; a Finance Mobilisation and Partnerships Expert with preferably ten or more years in climate, blended, donor, development, or investment finance; a Communications and Stakeholder Engagement Officer with preferably seven or more years; and a Monitoring, Reporting and Results Officer with preferably five or more years.

English is essential across the core team. Uzbek or Russian is required or desired depending on the role, including the Programme Manager and communications function. The team needs strong government, MDB, donor, financial-institution, private-sector, and stakeholder-management experience.

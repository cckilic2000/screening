## Executive Summary

EBRD seeks an independent consultant for a four-month due diligence of ASCO's proposed two-vessel Caspian Shipping Project. The EUR 175,000 assignment spans commercial, technical, financial, procurement, operational, and climate-alignment analysis and supports EBRD's financing decision. The required team includes six specialised maritime, financial, procurement, vessel-design, and climate experts. Strong Caspian Sea and Middle Corridor experience will be a major competitive factor.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

ASCO has requested EBRD financing for two RSD30C-design multipurpose dry-cargo vessels of approximately 9,900 dwt and up to 530 TEU each, expected to be constructed by Baku Shipyard Company. The investment supports transport operations in the Caspian basin and broader Middle Corridor connectivity.

The consultant must independently research, analyse, and verify the project's commercial, technical, and financial viability, review alignment with EBRD policies, and assess procurement and broader operational matters. The work must identify risks and opportunities and provide an updated commercial and operational outlook for the Caspian Sea.

The expected result is a robust evidence base for EBRD appraisal and financing decisions.

## Key Deliverables

- Detailed feasibility study.
- Commercial and market due diligence.
- Technical assessment of the proposed RSD30C vessels.
- Financial evaluation, vessel valuation, and financial projections using FAST standards.
- Shipbuilding-contract, procurement, contractor-oversight, and value-for-money assessment.
- Climate or Paris Alignment and environmental-performance assessment.

## Team Requirements

The consultant must demonstrate a proven record in comparable maritime-transport and logistics assignments. Middle Corridor and Caspian Sea experience is critical. The proposal must include methodology, team composition, task allocation, time inputs, and CVs.

Required experts are a Project Manager or Team Leader with maritime and Middle Corridor knowledge; a Maritime Commercial and Market Expert with at least ten years' experience; a Contract or Procurement Specialist with at least ten years in shipbuilding contracts and maritime contractor oversight; a Financial Expert with at least ten years in maritime investment evaluation, vessel valuation, and FAST-model projections; a Technical Expert familiar with RSD30C or comparable vessels; and a Climate or Paris Alignment Specialist. English fluency is essential for all key experts, and one person may cover multiple roles if qualified and available.

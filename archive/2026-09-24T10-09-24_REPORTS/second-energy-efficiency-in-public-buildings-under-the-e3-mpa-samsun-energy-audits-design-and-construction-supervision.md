## Executive Summary

The Ministry of Environment, Urbanization and Climate Change is seeking a consulting firm for a World Bank-financed energy-efficiency assignment covering 45 public-building blocks in Samsun. The work combines investment-grade audits, detailed multidisciplinary designs, tender support, construction supervision, commissioning, defects-period oversight, and 12 months of measurement and verification. The TOR also requires environmental and social management, OHS oversight, staff training, and knowledge transfer. Strong local regulatory, language, certification, and site-delivery capabilities will be important.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

Task I requires preliminary reviews, site assessments, baseline and modelling work, investment-grade energy audits, survey drawings, detailed architectural, civil, mechanical, electrical and structural designs, technical specifications, BOQs, cost estimates, commissioning plans, M&V plans, customised ESMPs and OHS plans, stakeholder consultations, and construction-tender documentation. Proposed measures may include envelope upgrades, electrification of heating, heat pumps, efficient lighting, building controls, and solar PV.

Task II covers construction supervision, contract administration, quality and cost control, environmental and social compliance, OHS, commissioning, defect remediation, final completion documentation, awareness materials, and post-renovation M&V. Beneficiaries are the public institutions and users of the listed university, health, educational, and administrative buildings, as well as the Ministry and PIU responsible for the national programme.

Expected impact includes lower energy consumption and operating costs, increased renewable-energy use, better building performance and user awareness, verified energy savings, and strengthened institutional capacity for scaled public-building renovation.

## Key Deliverables

- Monthly audit progress reports, investment-grade energy audit reports, and survey plans and drawings.
- Detailed architectural, civil, structural, mechanical, and electrical renovation designs.
- Technical specifications, BOQs, cost estimates, design calculations, and construction bidding documents.
- Building-specific ESMPs, OHS plans, stakeholder-consultation documentation, and training reports.
- Commissioning and measurement-and-verification plans.
- Initial inspection report, monthly progress reports, requested technical reports, completion certificates, and defects-notification certification.
- Commissioning report, as-built documentation, Energy Performance Certificates, and final completion reports.
- Visual presentation and awareness materials for each building complex.
- Quarterly and final Defects Notification Period reports.
- Quarterly and final M&V reports for all renovated buildings.

## Capacity Building Components

- Orientation and training of building operation and maintenance personnel on renovated systems and equipment.
- Verification of contractor-delivered O&M manuals and staff training as part of commissioning.
- Knowledge transfer to building administrations through commissioning documentation and handover.
- Environmental, social, grievance-mechanism, code-of-conduct, and OHS training for relevant personnel and workers.
- On-the-job safety and compliance training, including correct use of protective equipment.
- Public-building awareness materials explaining energy-efficiency interventions and benefits.
- Employment of at least one female intern from a relevant engineering or architecture programme.

## Team Requirements

The consulting firm must have at least ten years in consulting, at least five years of similar energy-efficient building supervision experience, sound administrative and financial capacity, and access to the required key experts. The team includes a Team Leader, Civil Engineer, Architect, Mechanical Engineer, Electrical Engineer, Cost and Planning Engineer, QA/QC Engineer, M&V Expert, Commissioning Expert, Environmental Specialist, Social Specialist, and Occupational Health and Safety Specialist, supported by technical and administrative personnel.

Minimum experience ranges from five to fifteen years depending on the role. The M&V expert requires an energy manager or audit-project certification issued by the Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources; the OHS specialist requires an A- or B-Class Turkish certificate or equivalent international certification. Project working language is English, field communication is Turkish or English, and most deliverables must be prepared in Turkish, with specified English translations.

Firms may submit alone, as a joint venture, or with subconsultants. Joint-venture partners must be legal entities and jointly and severally liable; subconsultant experience does not count toward the firm's evaluated experience.

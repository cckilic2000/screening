## Executive Summary

AICS is seeking eligible non-profit civil society organisations as possible consortium partners for a 36-month CERV-DAPHNE proposal focused primarily on Moldova. The concept combines financial support to grassroots organisations with sustained technical and institutional capacity building in GBV/VAW. Selected partners may lead mentoring, coaching, peer learning, governance and financial-management strengthening, networking, and grant-management activities. The procedure is exploratory and non-binding, and selection does not guarantee proposal submission or EU funding.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The initiative is expected to strengthen the technical capacity and organisational sustainability of grassroots and women-led CSOs working on GBV/VAW, particularly outside major urban centres and among underserved or vulnerable groups. A regranting mechanism will provide accessible financial support, while selected partners will help design and deliver participatory capacity-strengthening pathways based on each organisation's priorities and needs.

Support may cover survivor-centred GBV programming, referral mechanisms, accessibility and intersectionality, safeguarding, PSEAH, advocacy, governance, leadership, strategic planning, project-cycle and financial management, fundraising, MEL, HR management, internal controls, data protection, policies, procedures, and SOPs. Networking and coordination will promote peer learning, collective advocacy, communities of practice, and sustained cooperation with Moldovan institutions and relevant European networks.

The precise partnership roles, work allocation, geographic extension, and financial allocations will be negotiated during proposal preparation.

## Key Deliverables

- At EOI stage: completed and signed Annex B Expression of Interest Form.
- Proof of legal registration, most recent annual activity report, and safeguarding or child-protection policy where applicable.
- Evidence of relevant projects, EU-funded experience, organisational capacity, and proposed added value.
- During proposal preparation: contribution to project strategy, partnership design, work allocation, budget, and CERV application.
- If funded: regranting design and management arrangements.
- Participatory organisational assessments and tailored capacity-strengthening roadmaps.
- Mentoring, coaching, peer-learning, technical-guidance, and learning-by-doing activities.
- Technical GBV/VAW guidance, organisational policies, procedures, SOPs, and referral tools.
- Networking, alliance-building, advocacy, knowledge exchange, monitoring, and learning outputs.

## Capacity Building Components

- Long-term side-by-side mentorship and organisational accompaniment.
- Coaching, peer-to-peer learning, communities of practice, study visits, networking, and continuous follow-up.
- Technical support on survivor-centred, rights-based, trauma-informed, inclusive, and accessible GBV/VAW programming.
- Institutional support on governance, leadership, strategy, financial management, MEL, fundraising, HR, risk, internal controls, and data protection.
- Development and contextualisation of policies, procedures, SOPs, referral mechanisms, and operational tools.
- Capacity support for advocacy, communication, safeguarding, PSEAH, accountability, and organisational sustainability.
- Grant-making or regranting systems and support for grassroots organisations.
- Adaptation of international and European standards to the Moldovan legal, institutional, cultural, and operational context.

## Team Requirements

Eligible applicants must be legally established non-profit CSOs in Moldova, an EU Member State, or another CERV-eligible country. They need adequate organisational and operational capacity, EU-funded project experience, English working capability, compliance with legal and exclusion requirements, and at least three recent years of CSO capacity-building experience in GBV or violence against children.

Profile 1 requires international or transnational GBV/VAW and organisational-development expertise, strong European links, and capacity to organise exchanges and networking. Profile 2 is restricted to a CSO registered and operational in Moldova with strong contextual and grassroots experience. Applicants may apply for either profile or both. Specific named expert positions and individual qualification thresholds: COULDN'T BE FOUND.

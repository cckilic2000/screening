## Executive Summary

The European Commission's Directorate-General for Budget published FAST II as a EUR 265 million framework for financial and accounting advisory services across participating EU entities. Services included IPSAS and European Union Accounting Rules, financial-instrument accounting, valuations, accounting manuals, closure tasks, workflow analysis, and financial-information-system support. The tender imposed very high corporate-turnover, reference-project, staffing, and CV requirements. The contracting authority cancelled the procedure on 11 September 2026 and announced its intention to publish a replacement under a different reference.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The framework was intended to support fair presentation of participating entities' annual accounts, implementation and development of European Union Accounting Rules based on IPSAS, and accounting for loans, equity investments, guarantees, borrowings, and other financial instruments. Assignments could include written and oral accounting opinions, auditor presentations, accounting-policy development, implementation guidance, valuation methodologies, asset and liability valuations, expected-credit-loss work, accounting manuals, and supervised closure tasks.

A further workstream covered analysis and optimisation of financial and accounting processes and workflows, including opportunities, risks, compliance, and IT-augmented processes. Specific contracts could be delivered through individual experts, teams, or fixed-price outputs, from approved EU-based far-site or near-site locations and participating-entity premises.

Expected impact was improved accounting compliance, reliable financial reporting, consistent IPSAS-based policies, sound financial-instrument treatment, and more effective financial workflows across EU institutions and bodies.

## Key Deliverables

- Written accounting opinions, white papers, and oral advisory inputs.
- Accounting policies, European Union Accounting Rules implementation guidance, and accounting manuals.
- Financial-instrument classification, measurement, disclosure, valuation-methodology, and expected-credit-loss outputs.
- Valuations of financial assets and liabilities.
- Annual-account closure support and related documentation.
- Financial and accounting process and workflow analyses and optimisation recommendations.
- Fixed-price deliverables and task outputs defined in individual specific contracts.
- Monthly registration and progress reports, KPI reports, meeting minutes, handover documents, and service-management reporting.

## Capacity Building Components

- Continuous accounting and financial-information-system training for contractor personnel.
- At least four days of information-technology training per year for staff working under time-and-means contracts.
- Customer-required functional or technical training where needed for specific assignments.
- Handover documents, replacement training, and knowledge transfer to maintain service continuity.
- On-the-job training for replacement personnel where requested.
- Beneficiary-facing institutional training deliverables: COULDN'T BE FOUND.

## Team Requirements

Tenderers had to demonstrate average annual turnover above EUR 70 million for the preceding two financial years. A qualifying major reference needed at least two years' duration, English execution, an international client active in at least two countries with more than 500 staff, and at least 75 FTEs delivered in the EU. Tenderers also needed 18 to 20 time-and-means references and two to three fixed-price or quoted-time-and-means references across at least three clients.

The required profile pool included accounting consultants, IPSAS specialists, financial-instrument accountants, programme or portfolio advisers, business analysts, financial analysts, financial-information-system project managers, data and information analysts, treasury specialists, accounting auditors, and data-protection experts. Profile levels carried detailed education and experience thresholds, reaching up to roughly 18 years for senior accounting profiles. English proficiency was required; security clearance could be required for specific tasks. Joint tenders, subcontracting, and reliance on other entities were permitted subject to the administrative rules.

## Risks / Concerns

- The procedure was cancelled for a material compliance issue.
- A replacement was announced but its reference and publication details were not provided in the source.
- Minimum EUR 70 million turnover and large FTE/reference requirements sharply restrict the bidder pool.
- The framework does not guarantee specific-contract volume.
- Some assignments may require EU security clearance, EU-based delivery, rapid mobilisation, or on-site attendance.

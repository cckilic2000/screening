## Executive Summary

ILO published a call for an individual consultant to establish an informal Business and Human Rights network in Pakistan. The collaborator is expected to secure voluntary participation, clarify the network's purpose, put simple coordination arrangements in place, assess initial learning needs, and hand over a pathway for continued engagement and future capacity building. The public notice provides no budget, duration, detailed qualifications, or TOR. The four-day submission window and individual-only format triggered exclusion under the specified rules.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment's intended output is a functioning informal network with verified opt-in members, a defined purpose, workable coordination arrangements, and an initial assessment of learning needs. It must also provide a practical path for continued engagement and future capacity building after handover to ILO.

The official notice explicitly limits the network's role: it will not become a new institution or representative body, replace tripartite or WEBCOP structures, require action plans or results reporting, mandate cascade training, or require members to implement project activities. Membership does not guarantee funding, travel, certification, or selection for future training.

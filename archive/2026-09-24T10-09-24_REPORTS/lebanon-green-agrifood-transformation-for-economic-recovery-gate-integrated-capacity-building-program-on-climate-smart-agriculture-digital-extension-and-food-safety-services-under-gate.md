## Executive Summary

CDR is seeking an eligible consulting firm or consortium to deliver an 18-month integrated capacity-building programme under the World Bank-financed GATE project. The assignment will strengthen Ministry of Agriculture extension and regulatory functions while training farmers, cooperatives, and public staff in climate-smart production, market readiness, digital advisory tools, and food-safety systems. It includes certified ToT, farmer field schools, e-extension content, institutional coordination, and a comprehensive monitoring and learning framework. The opportunity is particularly relevant to organisations combining agricultural, training, digital, market-development, and institutional-reform expertise.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The programme will strengthen the Ministry of Agriculture's extension, rural-development, food-safety, and regulatory capacities while helping farmers, farmer groups, and agricultural cooperatives adopt technical, quality, and organisational practices needed by off-takers and target markets. Target groups include 15,000 farmers and farm workers, 100 extension staff, up to 40 food-safety or regulatory staff, and 150 farmer groups, with minimum participation targets of 35% women and 20% youth.

Track A covers climate-smart agriculture, market linkages, extension services, digital advisory, farmer field schools, demonstration plots, cooperative development, financial literacy, and business planning. Track B covers food-safety systems, inspection, sampling, reporting, traceability, GAP, GMP, HACCP, Codex, SPS concepts, e-certification awareness, and coordination among Ministry units, LARI, laboratories, and other institutions. Common work includes inception planning, participant databases, monitoring dashboards, feedback systems, knowledge products, and final evaluation.

Expected impact includes a sustainable pool of certified Ministry trainers, stronger farmer market readiness, wider access to practical climate and production advice, improved institutional processes, and sustained public ownership of digital extension services.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report with methodology, implementation approach, coordination arrangements, work plan, rollout approach, and assessment tools.
- Training Needs Assessment Report covering both technical tracks and beneficiary segmentation.
- Consolidated Training and Rollout Plan with calendar, regional coverage, staffing, participant targeting, modules, and monitoring arrangements.
- Certified Training of Trainers curriculum, package, delivery records, assessments, and certification records.
- Farmer and farm-worker training programme reports, cooperative capacity-building outputs, demonstration-plot documentation, and farmer field-school reports.
- Digital and e-extension content, rollout reports, advisory dissemination records, and beneficiary engagement data.
- Food-safety and regulatory curricula, training packages, delivery reports, participant lists, assessments, and institutional-coordination recommendations.
- Participant and activity database, monitoring dashboard, quarterly progress reports, feedback summaries, learning products, and knowledge-management documentation.
- Complete final training-resource set in the specified languages and formats.
- Draft and final evaluation and closing reports, including lessons, sustainability, and scaling recommendations.

## Capacity Building Components

- Participatory training-needs assessments for extension, farming, cooperative, digital-advisory, food-safety, and regulatory functions.
- Certified ToT for Ministry extension staff, delivered with agricultural vocational schools and extension offices.
- Climate-smart agriculture, GAP, IPM, biological control, water management, soil health, renewable-energy irrigation, and climate-risk modules.
- Farm business management, record keeping, profitability, investment planning, budgeting, savings, credit awareness, and debt management.
- Cooperative governance, member services, aggregation, collective marketing, value-chain development, and market-readiness training.
- Farmer field schools, demonstration plots, field days, exchange visits, peer learning, mentoring, and field-based coaching.
- Digital-advisory content and staff training for e-extension tools, beneficiary systems, dashboards, weather and pest alerts, and farmer follow-up.
- Food-safety, HACCP, inspection, sampling, traceability, standards, SPS, certification, and reporting training.
- Gender-responsive outreach, women-only sessions where appropriate, youth inclusion, and environmental and social good-practice awareness.
- Transfer of technical and administrative control of digital advisory services to the Ministry of Agriculture.

## Team Requirements

The service provider may be a joint venture, association or consortium of firms, or a specialised firm for a defined module and track. It must demonstrate at least ten years of relevant business experience, a successful record in climate-smart training, agricultural extension, food-safety systems, capacity building, and development-finance institution assignments, plus the capacity to manage multidisciplinary teams and environmental and social risks.

The minimum team comprises a Team Leader or Project Manager with more than ten years' relevant leadership experience; a CSA and Extension Specialist; a Digital Advisory or E-Extension Specialist; a Food Safety and Regulatory Specialist; training and facilitation specialists; a Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning Specialist; a Market Specialist; an Environmental and Social Specialist; and field coordinators and regional facilitators. Additional short-term expertise may cover irrigation, soil, IPM, cooperative development, veterinary or food safety, marketing, and communications. Training materials and field delivery require Arabic, while reporting and technical coordination require Arabic and English.

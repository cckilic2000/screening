## Executive Summary

The World Bank is inviting consulting firms to evaluate the Ukraine Relief, Recovery, Reconstruction and Reform Trust Fund. The remote evaluation will examine the fund's relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, coherence, results, and sustainability, including its contribution to mobilising and leveraging finance. Outputs will guide implementation, donor engagement, resource mobilisation, and annual planning and budgeting. Firms need substantial trust-fund, MDB, donor-programme, FCV, recovery, governance, and institutional-reform experience.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The evaluation will use document review, targeted virtual interviews, and analysis of available programme, financial, monitoring, and results information. It will cover the URTF portfolio from establishment through 30 June 2026 and include a limited sample of activities and financing instruments. The official notice excludes field missions, primary beneficiary surveys, financial audits, compliance reviews, and project-by-project performance ratings.

The firm must assess relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, coherence, contribution to results, impact, and sustainability, and identify lessons for future implementation. Particular attention is given to how the URTF mobilises, leverages, or unlocks financing for Ukraine and how findings can improve donor engagement, resource mobilisation, planning, and budgeting.

The expected impact is a stronger evidence base for strategic decisions about the trust fund's future implementation and financing.

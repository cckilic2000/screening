## Executive Summary

ILO Syria is seeking an NGO or INGO implementing partner to enhance business capacity of MSMEs and start-ups under the PROSPECTS Phase II Opportunity Fund. The intervention includes BDS, SIYB-based entrepreneurship support, Financial Education, coaching, mentoring, bank-account support, and referral to financial institutions for loan consideration. Proposals are due by 27-Jul-2026 23:59 (GMT 3.00).

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The TOR objective is to strengthen sustainable and market-oriented Business Development Service provision in Syria beyond short-term subsidized business support. The selected NGO/INGO will support entrepreneurial, managerial, and business development capacities of 1,000 aspiring and existing entrepreneurs, MSMEs, and start-ups in Aleppo, Rural Damascus, Idleb, and Hama, including returnees, IDPs, women, youth, and host communities.

Major activities include identifying and enrolling eligible MSMEs and start-ups, conducting diagnostics and training needs assessments, delivering gender-responsive business management and entrepreneurship support through ILO SIYB tools, delivering Financial Education to 500 MSMEs/start-ups, providing coaching, mentoring and advisory support, helping 500 MSMEs/start-ups open bank accounts or engage formal financial institutions, and referring at least 350 entrepreneurs to partner financial institutions for loan consideration.

## Key Deliverables

- Identify, mobilize, and enroll eligible MSMEs and start-ups.
- Conduct business diagnostics.
- Conduct training needs assessments.
- Conduct market segmentation exercises.
- Deliver gender-responsive business management and entrepreneurship support to 1,000 MSMEs and start-ups using ILO SIYB tools.
- Deliver Financial Education training to 500 selected MSMEs and start-ups.
- Provide post-training coaching, mentoring, and tailored business advisory support.
- Support 500 MSMEs and start-ups to open bank accounts or strengthen engagement with formal financial institutions.
- Assess investment readiness and refer at least 350 eligible entrepreneurs to partner financial institutions for loan consideration.

## Capacity Building Components

## Team Requirements

The project shall be implemented with a non-profit organization/entity. The page requires institutional capacity, presence, and operational experience in Syria, especially Aleppo, Rural Damascus, Idleb, and Hama; previous experience in BDS, entrepreneurship support, MSME recovery, livelihoods, market systems, or economic inclusion; experience in entrepreneurship coaching, mentoring, market linkages, investment readiness, access-to-finance referrals, and bank-account support; and qualified human resources including a Team Leader, MEAL Officer, and technical experts/coaches.

## Executive Summary

JICA has issued a competitive tender for a lump-sum training contract for the 2026-2028 issue-specific training course on strengthening inclusive education practice. The course focuses on improving learning for children with disabilities and career education up to employment. Technical proposals and bids are due by 2026/8/4 at noon, with bid opening on 2026/8/5.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The PDF states that the training course aims to help participants examine how to apply Japan’s inclusive education practices in their own countries. The course covers identification of challenges through country reports, lectures and institutional visits related to Japan’s inclusive education system, discussions, mock lessons, Theory of Change, and action-plan preparation.

The 2026 participant allocation target countries are Belize, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Nicaragua, Panama, Colombia, Ecuador, Paraguay, and Uruguay. Target participants are ministry and local-office officials, technical officers, specialists, and core teachers involved in special needs, inclusive, and disability education.

## Key Deliverables

- Country reports and issue list based on country reports.
- Lectures.
- Visits to related institutions.
- Discussions.
- Mock lessons.
- Outputs/成果品.
- Theory of Change.

## Team Requirements

The PDF states that bidders must have the all-ministry unified qualification for provision of services or be confirmed by JICA as having capacity to perform the work. It also states a positive eligibility requirement of 日本国登記法人, a legal entity registered under Japanese law. The contractor is expected to assign a business supervisor, administrative manager, and back-office support / deputy business supervisor.

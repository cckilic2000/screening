## Executive Summary

The EU Delegation to Ukraine is launching an expression of interest for services under the European Integration Without Disinformation project. The contract aims to strengthen resilience to foreign information manipulation and interference by supporting regional and local actors with fact-based information on the EU and Ukraine’s EU integration. The value is 100,000 € - 300,000 €, the place of performance is Ukraine, and the expression-of-interest deadline is 21/07/2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The page states that the contract aims to strengthen resilience to FIMI, including disinformation, by supporting regional and local actors in promoting fact-based information about the European Union and Ukraine’s EU integration. The project combines media monitoring, capacity building, community-led initiatives, and strategic communication to strengthen information integrity and democratic resilience across Ukraine.

Interested economic operators must express interest by email. After the inscription deadline closes, they will be added to the list of economic operators to receive the invitation to tender. The framework contract for 2 year is expected to be signed with a contractor.

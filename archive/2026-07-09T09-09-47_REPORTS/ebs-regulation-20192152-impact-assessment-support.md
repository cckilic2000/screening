## Executive Summary

The European Commission DG ESTAT is tendering impact assessment support for a potential amendment to the EBS Regulation. The contract covers problem definition, policy options, cost-benefit assessment, stakeholder consultations, reporting, monitoring KPIs, and adoption-process support. The estimated value is 160 000,00 EUR and the deadline is 04/09/2026 16:00:59 (UTC+02:00).

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The notice states that the current call aims to contract services supporting the Commission in performing the impact assessment of a potential amendment to the EBS Regulation. The tasks cover problem definition; drivers, objectives, and policy options; cost and benefit assessment; stakeholder consultations; collection, analysis, and reporting; conclusions; and monitoring KPIs.

The deliverables under the contract support Commission conclusions on policy options and drafting of the Commission Staff Working Document accompanying a possible amendment proposal. The duration is 14 months.

## Key Deliverables

- Problem definition.
- Drivers, objectives, and policy-options definition.
- Cost and benefits identification and assessment.
- Stakeholder consultations definition and implementation.
- Collection, analysis, and reporting on information.
- Conclusions and monitoring KPIs for the preferred policy option.
- Support throughout the adoption process.
- Support to drafting the Commission Staff Working Document.

## Executive Summary

ILO has issued a call for an individual external collaborator for skills development and social justice in the Arab States Region. The work is to develop a brief assessing existing skills development systems and provide policy recommendations. The deadline is 16-Jul-2026 10:30 (GMT 2.00).

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The notice states that the external collaborator will develop a brief assessing the contribution of existing skills development systems in the region to social justice. The brief will also provide policy recommendations to strengthen this contribution.

The collaborator will work under the direct supervision of the Head of the Skills Training and Excellence Unit at ILO Headquarters and receive technical support and oversight from the Senior Employment Policy Specialist and Regional Labour Economist at the ILO Regional Office for Arab States.

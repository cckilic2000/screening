## Executive Summary

The European Commission DG CLIMA is tendering a services contract for a study on interactions between climate action and defence. The study will analyse synergies, trade-offs, risks, climate goals, defence readiness, public resilience, and budgetary priorities. The estimated value is 200 000,00 EUR and the deadline is 31/08/2026 16:00:59 (UTC+02:00).

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The notice states that the objective is to analyse how EU climate action and defence policy interact in the context of Russia’s aggression. It will assess defence-sector contributions to climate goals and examine how climate policies can contribute to and affect defence capabilities, the defence industry, public resilience, and budgetary priorities.

The intended output is evidence for aligning rearmament and defence readiness with EU climate neutrality and resilience objectives, and for aligning climate-policy design with defence needs. The contract duration is 12 months.

## Key Deliverables

- Study analysing interactions between EU climate action and defence policy.
- Assessment of defence-sector contribution to EU climate goals.
- Examination of climate-policy impacts on defence capabilities, readiness, defence industry, public resilience, and budgetary priorities.
- Evidence for aligning rearmament and defence readiness with climate neutrality and resilience objectives.

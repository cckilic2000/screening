## Executive Summary

The World Bank procurement plan sets out procurement arrangements for Montenegro’s Waste Management Reform Project. The plan includes governance and institutional development, infrastructure improvements, remediation of a waste disposal site, and project management. It also identifies PIU staffing, TSU fiduciary responsibilities, and use of World Bank procurement methods including QCBS, CQS, RFB, and ICS.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The plan states that the Montenegro Waste Sector Improvement Project consists of four components covering governance and institutional development, infrastructure improvements, remediation, and project management. MESDNRD will serve as lead implementing agency, and a PIU will be established for project management, coordination, oversight, safeguards, monitoring, and reporting.

The Technical Services Unit within the Ministry of Finance will handle procurement and financial management. The plan also states that major procurement activities include design and build of Nikšić Regional Waste Management Center and remediation of the KAP waste disposal site, alongside consultancy firms and individual consultant roles.

## Key Deliverables

- Procurement Plan for the Montenegro Waste Management Reform Project.
- Project Implementation Unit positions including Project Manager, Environmental Specialist, Social Specialist, Civil Engineer, and Project Assistant.
- Design and build of Nikšić Regional Waste Management Center with Knowledge Sharing and Visitors Center.
- Remediation of waste disposal site at KAP.
- Consultancy-firm technical services under QCBS and CQS.
- Early market engagement activities including Business Outreach/Opportunity Event, Request for Information, Supplier Workshop, and Benchmarking and Comparative Market Analysis.

## Capacity Building Components

- The PIU will ensure compliance with fiduciary, environmental, and social standards, monitor progress, and prepare reports.
- The plan states that the project will finance operating costs including training.
- Early market engagement activities are planned for high-value international procurement activities.

## Team Requirements

The plan states that the PIU will be composed of individual consultants: Project Manager, Environmental Specialist, Social Specialist, Civil Engineer, and Project Assistant. It also states that consultancy firms will be engaged to perform planned technical tasks using QCBS and CQS.

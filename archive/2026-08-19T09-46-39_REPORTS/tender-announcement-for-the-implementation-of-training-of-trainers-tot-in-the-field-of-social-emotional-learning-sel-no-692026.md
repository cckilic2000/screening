## Executive Summary

Teacher Creativity Center has issued tender No. 69/2026 for implementation of Training of Trainers in Social Emotional Learning. The assignment sits under a 24-month project implemented with World Vision International and funded by ECHO to support inclusive, safe and dignified education and protection for vulnerable children affected by conflict in the West Bank. The work covers four ToT groups for 100 teachers, training materials, a detailed training plan, participant assessment, supervisory field visits and final reporting. Technical and financial offers are due by 26/08/2026 at 14:00.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The source page describes Teacher Creativity Center as a Palestinian non-governmental and non-profit association focused on social outcomes of quality education in Palestine and the Middle East and North Africa. Since 1 June 2026, the association has been implementing a 24-month project in partnership with World Vision International and funded by ECHO under the title "Ensuring access to inclusive, safe and dignified education and protection for the most vulnerable children affected by conflict in the West Bank."

The tender seeks a specialized trainer to facilitate four ToT courses in Social Emotional Learning for educational cadres from targeted schools. The training consists of four groups, each receiving 24 hours over three training days, and is designed to enable 100 teachers to understand SEL theory, SEL-related skills, and ToT facilitation skills for later transfer to other teachers.

The scope includes preparing creative training material and a detailed training plan, delivering the four training groups, developing and implementing pre- and post-assessments, developing a skills-measurement tool, conducting supervisory field visits during knowledge transfer and initiative implementation, and submitting detailed reports and all training and visit files.

## Key Deliverables

- Training material including training objectives and creative, non-traditional training mechanisms, revised after review by the association's specialists.
- Detailed training plan based on the training description, including main and sub-training titles and a timetable for the three days and eight daily hours of each training.
- Implementation of four training groups, each three training days and 24 hours.
- Pre- and post-assessment to measure changes in trainee knowledge and skills, with analysis and delivery of collected and analysed information to the association.
- Skills-measurement tool for use during field visits, reviewed with the project team.
- Supervisory field visits to targeted schools during knowledge transfer and initiative implementation.
- Detailed training report after completion, plus a field-visit report after visits are completed.
- Analysis of the skills-measurement tool results in the report.
- Delivery of all papers and files related to the trainings and field visits.

## Capacity Building Components

- Training of Trainers in Social Emotional Learning for 100 teachers from targeted schools.
- Four training groups, each receiving 24 training hours over three training days.
- Training on SEL theory and related intrapersonal, interpersonal and cognitive skills.
- Training on identifying, monitoring and responding to social and emotional challenges affecting learners' social and academic performance.
- Training on using sports and arts to provide learners with SEL skills.
- ToT facilitation skills enabling teachers to organize later trainings for other teachers.
- Supervisory field visits to observe application of skills and provide feedback and recommendations.
- Knowledge-transfer phase where trained teachers transfer acquired knowledge and skills to other educational cadres in the same schools.

## Team Requirements

The source page states that Teacher Creativity Center invites qualified trainers with qualifications and experience in the stated fields to submit technical and financial offers. The centre seeks a specialized trainer in Social Emotional Learning; preference is given to applicants combining academic qualifications and specialized practical experience in Social Emotional Learning, Training of Trainers and capacity building for educational cadres. The technical offer must include evidence of qualifications and previous experience for applicants and participants in the trainings or field visits, including CVs, and no trainer, participant or team member may be added after award. Applicants must provide examples of similar trainings, relevant experience, CVs for consultants/trainers with team roles where applicable, a recent sample of similar reports if relevant, three organizational references, and evidence of Arabic and English reporting skills.

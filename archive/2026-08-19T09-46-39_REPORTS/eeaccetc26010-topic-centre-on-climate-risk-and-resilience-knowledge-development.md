## Executive Summary

The European Environment Agency has issued an open call for tenders for a Topic Centre on Climate Risk and Resilience: Knowledge Development. The source page lists an estimated value of 1900000 EUR, a maximum duration of 53 months, and a tender deadline of 17/09/2026 14:00 Europe/Copenhagen. The work focuses on data, policy-relevant insights, knowledge exchange, climate risk assessments and resilience knowledge products. The source geography is Europe and EU Member States.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The EEA tender is intended to support progress toward a climate-resilient Europe by providing data, information and policy-relevant insights. The work will inform adaptation measures, risk-management strategies and societal preparedness, and will support EU Member States in preparing climate risk assessments and adaptation policies. The new Topic Centre will focus mainly on knowledge and networking services, with some data services and collaboration with another Topic Centre on climate and resilience.

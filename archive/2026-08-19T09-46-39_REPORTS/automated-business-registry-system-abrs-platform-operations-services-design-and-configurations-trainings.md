## Executive Summary

DAI/IPSD II has issued RFP DAI-IPSDII-2026-032 for ABRS Platform Operations, Services Design, and Configurations Trainings. The project benefits the Palestinian Ministry of National Economy, is funded by the World Bank and European Union, and is implemented by DAI Global UK. The deadline for proposals is August 30, 2026 @4:00 PM Ramallah Time (GMT+3). The RFP asks service providers to deliver structured technical training, supervised practical implementation, online mentoring, knowledge transfer, reports and certificates over approximately six months.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The RFP is under IPSD II, which aims to improve economic opportunities for tech and tech-enabled startups, innovative SMEs and individuals in the digital economy in the West Bank. Subcomponent 1.3 focuses on improving government-to-business services through implementation and enhancement of the Automated Business Registry System. The assignment is split into services design/configuration training and platform operations training, both intended to enable MoNE teams to independently configure, operate, maintain, troubleshoot and enhance ABRS services and infrastructure.

Scope A covers translating administrative procedures into digital workflows, configuring service forms, validation, certificates, APIs, integrations, reports, dashboards, landing page and service catalog administration. Scope B covers ABRS architecture, microservices, platform operations, Linux-based infrastructure, networking, certificates, databases, containerized environments, monitoring, logging, maintenance, backup, recovery and troubleshooting. Both scopes use a learn-practice-apply model with face-to-face training followed by online mentoring and supervised practical implementation.

## Key Deliverables

- Detailed training plan including methodology, schedule and session/practical exercise structure.
- Training materials including presentations, practical exercise guides and supporting reference materials.
- Operational and configuration guidance materials.
- Delivery of training sessions and supervised practical exercises.
- Online mentoring and follow-up sessions.
- Knowledge transfer and handover support.
- Training completion report summarizing activities, topics, attendance and evaluation results.
- Evaluation mechanism to assess participant progress, practical understanding and performance.
- Certificates of completion with participant assessment and grades where applicable.

## Capacity Building Components

- Structured training for MoNE IT team on ABRS services design and configuration.
- Structured training for MoNE IT technical team and IPSD II technical support team on ABRS platform operations.
- Hybrid capacity-building approach combining instructor-led sessions and supervised practical implementation.
- Guided practical exercises on workflows, forms, APIs, reports, dashboards, deployment, monitoring, backups and troubleshooting.
- Online mentoring and follow-up sessions.
- Knowledge transfer and handover support enabling MoNE staff to continue using and internally transferring acquired knowledge.

## Team Requirements

Only Service Providers can submit proposals. The service provider must demonstrate experience delivering technical training programs, designing and delivering capacity-building programs for technical teams, and relevant experience with digital platforms, workflow-based systems, service-oriented architecture, enterprise systems or business process automation solutions. Trainers must have hands-on experience with UNCTAD e-Registration or similar digital registration systems, ABRS implementation experience is an advantage, and Arabic proficiency is considered an advantage.

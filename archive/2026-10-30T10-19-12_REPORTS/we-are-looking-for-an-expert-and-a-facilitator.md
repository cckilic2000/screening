## Executive Summary

CNUM is recruiting a Lot 1 expert/trainer for the EU-funded COMMUNITY+ project in northern Moldova. The role supports two local consultation platforms and two community advocacy/participation plans through eight field days and preparatory/finalization work. The assignment totals 14 working days in November–December 2026 and requires Romanian and Russian. Applications with a CV, two comparable examples and a gross daily fee in MDL are due on 9 November 2026 at 18:00 Chișinău time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment advances residents’ participation and representation following administrative reform and amalgamation. In Vasileuți and Unguri, the expert will support functioning consultation platforms and practical advocacy/participation plans, with Danu and Țețcani representatives participating in peer exchange. The programme anticipates up to 80 unique participants, of whom approximately 20 will become platform members. The expert is responsible for content and methodology, coordinates with the separately recruited facilitator responsible for group process, and reports to the CNUM project manager.

## Key Deliverables

- Adapted methodology, session agendas, training materials and practical instruments before the first field day.
- Eight field days: community forums, platform-formation workshops, peer-learning exchanges and advocacy/participation planning.
- Two documented local consultation platforms, each with approximately ten members, defined roles/rules and a representation map.
- Two community advocacy and participation plans, each identifying three to five priorities, responsible persons, participation and feedback mechanisms.
- Final expert report with findings, lessons and recommendations for sustainability and replication in Danu and Țețcani.

## Capacity Building Components

- Adult-group civic-participation training and practical workshops.
- Capacity development for residents and community leaders to influence local decisions and represent interests after amalgamation.
- Peer learning involving Danu and Țețcani on field days three and seven.
- Practical tools for platform membership, roles, rules, representation and advocacy planning.
- Coordinated preparation and follow-up with the facilitator; no separate ToT component is specified.

## Team Requirements

- Lot 1 is an expert/trainer role requiring three to five years of civic engagement, community development, participatory governance or local-advocacy experience.
- Experience with local public authorities or civil-society organizations and adult-group training/facilitation, preferably in rural communities, is required.
- Romanian and Russian are required; English is an advantage. Relevant administration, political science, social-science or law studies and knowledge of amalgamation reform are advantages.
- Applicants provide a CV with two examples of similar activity and a gross daily fee in MDL; remuneration follows actual days worked and accepted deliverables, with financial offers at 0% VAT.
- One candidate may be selected for only one lot. The expert and facilitator must be different people. Consortium arrangements and the separate Lot 2 qualification requirements: COULDN'T BE FOUND.

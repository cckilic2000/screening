## Executive Summary

IFC is selecting a firm to deliver a structured coaching programme for 15 logistics-related companies in Albania. The work includes programme design, participant selection, individual and group coaching, practical business tools and results monitoring through a six-month follow-up. Delivery is expected from November 2026 to December 2027, supported by a separate local implementing partner engaged by IFC. EOIs are due on 12 November 2026 at 11:59 PM EST.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment translates MoveUp training into practical improvements in participating companies’ operations and management. During preparation, the provider will develop the methodology and materials, present two online introductory meetings, assess applications and form groups of no more than three companies, with up to two participants per company. Coaching includes initial and final approximately one-hour individual sessions per company and five 1.5-hour online group sessions per group, with applied tasks between sessions. The provider will track baseline/progress indicators and support a six-month follow-up survey, while IFC’s separately engaged local partner provides coordination, outreach, communications and logistics.

## Key Deliverables

- Coaching Program Design Report and Engagement Plan, including methodology, preparation completion, introductory-meeting materials, application form, topic list, selection criteria, final company/participant list, thematic needs, group composition/rationale, coaches, calendar and M&E indicators.
- Delivery of two online Introductory Meetings and participation in Q&A/expectation setting.
- Initial and final individual coaching sessions for each company, and five 1.5-hour online group sessions per group.
- Practical tools, templates, checklists/reference materials, between-session actions and company follow-up action plans.
- Coaching Implementation Report covering delivered sessions, customized content, progress notes, results, feedback/evaluations and issues/dropouts.
- Post-Implementation and Final Report with six-month survey data, response rate, results against baselines, lessons and recommendations for future replication.
- Inputs for company case stories, subject to company consent.

## Capacity Building Components

- Structured individual and group business coaching with qualified coaches.
- Peer learning and “hot-seat” exploration of company-specific logistics challenges.
- Tailored support on transport/distribution, warehousing, inventory/demand planning, process optimization, supply-chain performance and digitalization/light automation preparation.
- Tools and between-session practical work to apply learning in daily operations.
- Final individual recommendations and follow-up action plans.
- Company baseline/progress tracking and six-month post-completion learning/results assessment; no separate ToT package is specified.

## Team Requirements

- Consulting firms are invited, and firms may associate to enhance qualifications, identifying a lead if bidding as a group.
- The provider must demonstrate logistics/supply-chain/warehouse/transport/distribution coaching, mentoring or advisory experience, structured individual/group programme delivery and comparable assignment references; SME and donor-funded experience is preferred.
- A named team of coaches with CVs must hold recognized coaching qualifications/certification and practical logistics/supply-chain experience, with complementary operational, leadership, digital-transformation and change-management expertise.
- The team must have sufficient capacity for multiple parallel groups; delivery must not depend on a single individual. A numerical minimum team size: COULDN'T BE FOUND.
- Programme-management capability must cover scheduling, performance indicators, baseline and six-month surveys, session records, feedback, privacy and quality assurance.
- English fluency is required; Albanian is an asset. The provider supplies the online delivery platform and licenses and coordinates with IFC and its separately appointed local implementing partner.

## Relevant Sector Analysis

- The assignment assesses organizational capacity and service models across 40 Ukrainian veteran spaces.
- It examines accessibility, gender-responsive support and the user journey of veterans, families and caregivers.
- Financial and institutional sustainability, management, staffing and coordination are explicit assessment domains.
- The work evaluates links with local authorities, social/health services, employment services, civil society and other community actors.
- A practical self-assessment framework and improvement recommendations are intended for veteran spaces, local authorities, the Ministry of Veterans Affairs and partners.
- The approach combines quantitative/qualitative field research, institutional document review and practical analytical tools.

## Executive Summary

UNDP Ukraine is procuring an assessment of 40 veteran spaces under its community-based reintegration work supported by Germany. The assignment combines multi-regional research, user-journey analysis and institutional/financial sustainability assessment with a practical capacity-assessment toolkit. It lasts up to 22 weeks and requires a five-person expert team with Ukrainian-language capability. Proposals are due on 20 November 2026, with different closing-time displays in the notice and negotiation PDF.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assessment will examine the functioning of municipal, social-service, civil-society and integrated veteran-space models, including how veterans and family members access services, receive referrals and navigate barriers. It will compare staffing, management, accessibility, financial sustainability and community partnerships across a 40-space sample focused on eight oblasts. Findings must be translated into a usable Veteran Space Capacity Assessment Framework with a self-assessment component and practical recommendations for organizational improvement. The contractor will provide edited, designed and translated analytical products and present the final findings to UNDP and stakeholders.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception Report, approved mixed-method methodology, sampling approach, assessment tools, work plan and quality/data-protection arrangements, within three weeks.
- Fieldwork Completion Report, cleaned/anonymized quantitative data, organized qualitative evidence and supporting documents, within 12 weeks.
- User Journey Maps for the required veteran/family categories, with barriers, referral pathways and improvement recommendations.
- Comparative analysis of organizational models, institutional capacity, financial/operational viability and service coordination.
- Practical Veteran Space Capacity Assessment Framework with domains, criteria, indicators, guiding questions, evidence sources, recommendations and a self-assessment component.
- Draft Analytical Report integrating the research and practical recommendations, within 18 weeks.
- Final Analytical Report of at least 40 main-body pages excluding annexes, Executive Summary and complete final research package; finalization products within 20 weeks.
- Ukrainian and English print-ready versions, professional translation/proofreading, graphic design, editable source files and all final materials.
- Final presentation of at least 20 slides and a 40–60-minute presentation session, within 22 weeks; final deliverable payment follows completion of both finalization and presentation outputs.

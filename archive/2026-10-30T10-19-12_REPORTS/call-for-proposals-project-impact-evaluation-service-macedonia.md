## Executive Summary

LBI-GMR invites firm/institution proposals for an independent impact evaluation of EU Support for Rule of Law in North Macedonia. The assignment assesses effectiveness and contribution to institutional and systemic change using Contribution Analysis and existing monitoring evidence. The maximum budget is €15,000.00, and the final report is due on 21 January 2027. Proposals must be emailed by 6 November 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The evaluation covers a project supporting justice institutions, law-enforcement agencies, anti-corruption bodies and human-rights institutions. The contractor will test the project’s contribution story, analyze monitoring data and stakeholder evidence, and distinguish verified results from perceptions, emerging outcomes and longer-term effects. LBI-GMR provides project outputs, monitoring records and stakeholder interview transcripts, while the contractor conducts additional questionnaire-based data collection and supports qualitative analysis. The final products must communicate the project’s contribution through a rigorous, impartial and accessible account, supported where appropriate by graphics, visualizations and illustrative cases.

## Key Deliverables

- Concept note with work plan, evaluation methodology and proposed report structure.
- Draft questionnaires and/or other proposed data-collection instruments.
- Draft data analysis incorporating evidence supplied by the project.
- Draft evaluation report, due 11 January 2027.
- Final evaluation report with an evaluative judgement suitable for the project’s OPSYS impact indicator, due 21 January 2027.
- Executive summary of no more than 15 pages and an evaluation presentation.
- Editable reports, graphics and supporting visual products; deliverables 1–4 are refined following feedback meetings.

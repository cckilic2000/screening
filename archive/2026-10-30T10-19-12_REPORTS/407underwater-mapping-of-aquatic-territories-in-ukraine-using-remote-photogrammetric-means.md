## Executive Summary

UNDP Ukraine is procuring underwater photogrammetry services at two sites near Cherkasy and Odesa. The assignment requires 15 working days of field activity, at least six hectares of mapping and hands-on engagement with state counterparts. Bidders must demonstrate organizational underwater-photogrammetry experience and deploy qualified ROV/UUV specialists/trainers. The public notice closes on 10 November 2026; its New York-time display and the negotiation PDF’s UTC closing time differ.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The Mine Action Project will test underwater photogrammetry as a means of producing detailed mapping that can support identification of underwater explosive remnants of war. The selected organization will use its own equipment alongside SESU, SSTS and National Police counterparts, complete surveys at two designated locations and supply open-format data suitable for standard GIS tools. A completion report must summarize the work, data quality, applicability in different water bodies and potential use in Ukraine. UNDP will review and accept the deliverables before making the single completion payment.

## Key Deliverables

- Fifteen working days of underwater scanning/mapping at two sites, covering at least six hectares.
- Open-format underwater photogrammetric orthomosaics and all final survey data, georeferenced imagery, GIS datasets and supporting documentation.
- A completion report of at least ten pages in English describing work undertaken, locations, results quality, applicability in different water bodies and suggested use in Ukraine.
- Complete final data package delivered within 14 calendar days of the last field day through a secure method approved by UNDP.
- Corrected or completed deficient survey work and revised data/report deliverables when required for UNDP acceptance.

## Executive Summary

ILO STREAM is seeking an individual external collaborator to finalize a Social Protection Across Borders training package connecting South Asia and the GCC. The work covers curriculum review, a modular framework, participant materials, a Facilitator Toolkit, pilot support and Training-of-Trainer resources. The deadline is 12 November 2026 at 11:00 GMT+2. Budget, duration and detailed academic/experience requirements are not disclosed in the accessible public notice.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The expert will transform existing course materials into a standardized, modular adult-learning curriculum and supporting resources. Participant handouts and a comprehensive Facilitator Toolkit must accompany the curriculum, while pilot-run support and Training-of-Trainer materials are intended to enable independent delivery by other trainers. The public UNGM page lists the regional beneficiary countries and links procedural supplier guides. Its detailed tender documentation is accessed through the authenticated ILO e-sourcing process described in those guides.

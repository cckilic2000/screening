## Relevant Sector Analysis

- The programme finances modernization of irrigation and drainage infrastructure and services.
- Planned works include approximately 440 km of infrastructure serving 82,000 hectares, ten regulation structures/groundwater sources and dam-safety improvements.
- Water-information, permitting, SCADA, accounting and monitoring systems form the technology component.
- Performance-based financing supports ten District Water Management Units and their operational and financial efficiency.
- Technical assistance addresses irrigation design norms, institutional service delivery, investment packaging and programme management.
- Transaction advisory support includes feasibility and structuring work for a regional agro-logistics hub and private-sector engagement.

## Executive Summary

The Kyrgyz Republic has announced procurement under the National Irrigation Investment Program, supported by US$172 million equivalent in financing. The programme combines irrigation and dam investments with institutional service improvements, training, digital water systems and transaction advice. Procurement is open to eligible firms and individuals under World Bank Procurement Regulations. The general notice provides no submission deadline or contract-specific consulting qualification requirements.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The programme aims to improve irrigation efficiency, access and reliability through physical infrastructure, climate-smart technologies and stronger service institutions. Its first component covers infrastructure modernization and on-farm pilots; its second covers national water management, performance-based financing for district units, and private-sector transaction support; its third provides technical assistance and programme-management support. Institutional activities include design-norm updates, tariff-setting and financial-management processes, data systems, training and investment preparation. Separate procurement notices will identify the actual contracts as they become available.

## Executive Summary

UN Women is procuring technical assistance to strengthen the evidence and gender responsiveness of Moldova’s entrepreneurship and competitiveness policies. The assignment will inform PACC 2028–2032, review up to three ODA support instruments and deliver a practical toolkit, diagnostic, policy options and monitoring framework. A minimum four-expert team must combine entrepreneurship policy, legal, gender-equality and data/M&E expertise with Romanian and English proficiency. The deadline is 13 November 2026, and the TOR specifies an assignment of up to nine months.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The contractor will map the policy and institutional architecture, review statistical/administrative and stakeholder evidence, and screen relevant policy, legal and regulatory provisions for gender responsiveness. A proportionate review of up to three agreed ODA programmes will consider access, participation, available results data and user experience, and identify feasible procedural improvements. These findings will feed an integrated diagnostic, evidence-based policy options, a PACC 2028–2032 policy-evidence matrix and a minimum indicator/data framework. MEDD, ODA and other relevant institutions will participate in technical review, and the final checklist/toolkit must be in Romanian and usable within existing institutional processes.

## Key Deliverables

- D1 Inception Report: detailed work plan, methodology, analytical framework, stakeholder/institutional mapping, evidence inventory, data needs, consultation plan and team mobilization — December 2026.
- D5 Assessment of up to three selected ODA programmes/support instruments, with prioritized practical adjustments — January 2027.
- D6 Sex-Disaggregated Data and Reporting Recommendations, including minimum dataset, indicators and form/reporting revisions — February 2027.
- D7 Draft Romanian Gender-Responsive Checklist and Toolkit with adaptable templates, validated in one technical working session with ODA — March 2027.
- D2 Institutional and Policy Mapping Report — April 2027.
- D3 Gender and Entrepreneurship Evidence Review Report — April 2027.
- D4 Policy, Legal and Regulatory Screening Report — May 2027.
- D8 Integrated Gender and Entrepreneurship Diagnostic Report — June 2027.
- D9 Evidence-Based Policy Options Paper for PACC 2028–2032 — July 2027.
- D10 PACC 2028–2032 Gender Entrepreneurship Policy Evidence Matrix — July 2027.
- D11 Gender-Responsive Indicator and Data Framework — July 2027.
- D12 Consolidated Final Report and finalized Romanian Checklist and Toolkit incorporating MEDD/ODA feedback — August 2027.
- Reports in editable Word and PDF; written UN Women feedback and contractor resubmission are each scheduled within five working days.

## Capacity Building Components

- Strengthening MEDD/ODA capacity to identify gender-related constraints and use evidence in entrepreneurship policy and programme design.

- One technical working session with ODA to validate the draft toolkit.
- Improved sex-disaggregated data fields, reporting processes and feasible indicators within existing institutional systems.
- Structured coordination, technical review and knowledge transfer through policy diagnostics, evidence matrices and final tools; a separate ToT programme is not specified.

## Team Requirements

- Registered legal entity in Moldova or abroad; the TOR includes companies, consulting firms, NGOs and research institutions. The eligibility grid separately uses the term “legally registered commercial entity”.
- At least three years of organizational experience in at least two relevant domains and at least one comparable assignment in the last five years. Experience with a UN/EU/multilateral/bilateral development client or national ministry/agency is required; three recommendation letters or equivalent evidence and three years of financial statements demonstrating an average-current-assets/current-liabilities ratio over the last three years of at least one are required.
- Expert 1 / Team Leader: relevant master’s/advanced degree; at least five years in economic/entrepreneurship policy analysis/design/reform and five years of project management; a documented SME-policy/support assignment and central-government policy dialogue; EU SME-policy knowledge.
- Expert 2 / Legal Expert: relevant law degree, master’s preferred; at least five years in economic/commercial/regulatory law for master’s holders; gender-sensitive legal analysis, CEDAW and relevant EU gender-equality legal frameworks.
- Expert 3 / Gender Equality and Women’s Economic Empowerment Specialist: relevant master’s degree; at least three years of gender mainstreaming in economic policy, enterprise programmes or labour-market work; sex-disaggregated indicators, intersectionality and government/private-sector training experience.
- Expert 4 / Data Analytics and M&E Specialist: relevant university degree; at least two years of data analytics, M&E/results measurement; databases, administrative/survey data, quality checks and results frameworks.
- All four experts require Romanian and English; Russian is an asset. All must be actively involved. A JV/consortium/association is allowed with a designated lead, documented roles and the prescribed information form; minimum criteria apply to members unless the RFP specifies cumulative compliance.

## Executive Summary

The Regional Challenge Fund, through WB6-CIF and KfW financing, is procuring consulting services to design, develop, and implement a student online portal for CACTTUS Education in Kosovo. The estimated cost is 39,000.00 EUR excluding VAT, with a seven-month implementation period and a 9 September 2026 deadline. The assignment includes portal development, institutional data workflows, integrations, training, manuals, and post-launch technical support.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment aims to create a centralized online platform serving the school community, including the public, parents, students, teachers, administrative staff, and in-company instructors. The scope covers design workshops, portal architecture, student management, administrative controls, event calendar, alert functions, analytics, multilingual and accessible interfaces, security, hosting, integration, and technical support. Beneficiaries identified in the source are CACTTUS Education LLC and partners.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception Report.
- Approved system architecture and design mockups.
- Fully functional online portal platform.
- Integrated modules for Student Management, Administration, Calendar, Alerts, and Analytics.
- Verified SSO and system integrations.
- Configured hosting environment with SLA in place.
- Portal trainings, manuals, and documentation.
- Installed and active SSL certificate.
- 60-day post-launch technical support period.
- Final Report.

## Capacity Building Components

- Stakeholder workshops to gather technical and functional requirements.
- Portal trainings for users.
- Manuals and documentation for portal operation.

## Team Requirements

The service provider must be an experienced IT company with at least five years of proven experience in planning, designing, and implementing IT software and user-friendly webpages, and must evidence at least two similar contracts. Required key experts are a Project Manager/Team Leader, UI/UX Designer, Software Developer, and Quality Assurance expert with the qualifications and experience stated in the ToR.

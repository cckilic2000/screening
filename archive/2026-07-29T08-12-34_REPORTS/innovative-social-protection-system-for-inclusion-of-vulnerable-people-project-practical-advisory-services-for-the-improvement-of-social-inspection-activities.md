## Executive Summary

Uzbekistan’s National Agency for Social Protection has issued an REOI for practical advisory services to improve social inspection activities under the World Bank-financed INSON project. The assignment is six months, uses QCBS, and EOIs are due by Aug 14, 2026 at 23:45. The work includes oversight-system reform, digital and risk-based approaches, data analytics and AI solutions, training of Social Inspection personnel, and pilot implementation support.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consulting services include development and implementation of an enhanced system of state oversight, monitoring, and evaluation in social protection in Uzbekistan. The assignment will support NASP through review of international best practices, risk-based and digital oversight, data analytics and AI-based solutions, training, and pilot implementation. Expected impact is stronger social protection oversight aligned with ongoing reforms.

## Key Deliverables

- Enhanced system of state oversight, monitoring, and evaluation in social protection.
- Review of international best practices.
- Risk-based and digital oversight approaches.
- Data analytics and artificial intelligence-based solutions.
- Training of Social Inspection personnel.
- Support for pilot implementation.

## Capacity Building Components

- Training of Social Inspection personnel.
- Development of training and methodological materials including manuals, presentations, and guidance tools.
- Evaluation of training effectiveness through pre- and post-training assessments.

## Team Requirements

The firm should have at least 10 years of consulting experience in public administration, social policy, social protection, or related fields; international-organization technical assistance experience; oversight/inspection reform experience; regulatory and methodological document development; institutional analysis; pilot methodology experience; social protection/social services experience; training and capacity-building experience; and digitalization/AI oversight experience. Consultants may associate as JV or sub-consultancy.

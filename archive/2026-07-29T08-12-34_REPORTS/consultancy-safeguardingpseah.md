## Executive Summary

Right To Play is procuring safeguarding/PSEAH consultancy support in Lebanon, with applications due on 07th of August 2026. The assignment is expected to last up to 60 days over August-December 2026 and will support five partner organizations. The work includes safeguarding framework revision, focal point support, training design and delivery, reporting mechanisms, digital safeguarding procedures, MEL tools, and final reporting.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultancy is intended to strengthen existing or create new safeguarding capacity and procedures among Right To Play partner organizations in Lebanon. The scope covers programme management standards, safeguarding risk assessment, safeguarding recruitment checks, focal point capacity, training curriculum and delivery, reporting and disclosure systems, investigations, digital safeguarding and data protection, and follow-up support. The expected impact is stronger partner safeguarding practice in programmes serving children and adolescents.

## Key Deliverables

- Revised implementing partner Safeguarding Framework and related procedures.
- Support to appointment and capacity of partners’ Safeguarding Focal Points and senior leadership.
- Training curriculum, training materials, preparation, and delivery of in-person/remote trainings.
- Localized reporting, disclosure, response, and investigation procedures.
- Digital safeguarding and data protection procedures for programme, MEL, and communications activities.
- Final report on implementation of the safeguarding framework.
- Ongoing support based on partner needs until end of 2026.
- Safeguarding assessment tool, training priorities, child-friendly reporting mechanism, strengthened digital safeguarding standards, and safeguarding MEL tools.

## Capacity Building Components

- Capacity building for five partner organizations.
- Training curriculum and delivery for partner staff in different departments.
- Support to Safeguarding Focal Points and senior leadership.
- Ongoing partner support until end of 2026.

## Team Requirements

Applicants may be consultants or firms. Essential requirements include a degree in social sciences, social work, human rights, preferably child safeguarding/protection/rights or equivalent experience; safeguarding systems experience for aid/humanitarian organizations; understanding of international safeguarding best practice; experience with referral pathways and confidential incident reporting in Lebanon; clear technical communication; safeguarding/protection/child protection/gender experience in development and emergency settings; and excellent written and fluent English and Arabic.

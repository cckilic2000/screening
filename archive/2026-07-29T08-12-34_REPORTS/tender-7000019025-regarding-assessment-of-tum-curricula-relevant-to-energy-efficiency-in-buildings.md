## Executive Summary

GIZ has issued tender 7000019025 for assessment of TUM curricula relevant to energy efficiency in buildings in Moldova. Applications are due electronically by 13.08.2026 at 17:00, and financial bids must be in Moldovan lei, VAT 0. The assignment includes curriculum mapping, course analysis, benchmarking, stakeholder consultation, recommendations, and ToR preparation for follow-on curriculum-development services.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment aims to determine the extent to which energy-efficient and high-performance building design is integrated into relevant bachelor’s and master’s programmes. The scope covers mapping programmes and course units, establishing an assessment methodology, analysing learning outcomes and teaching formats, benchmarking gaps, consulting TUM and external stakeholders, and preparing prioritized recommendations. The expected output includes ToR for later curriculum development or revision.

## Key Deliverables

- Mapped relevant study programmes and course units with assessment methodology.
- Analysis of course content, learning outcomes, teaching formats, practical activities, and assessment methods.
- Gap identification through benchmarking against international good practice, higher education quality standards, and Moldova construction-sector needs.
- Consultations with TUM representatives, faculty members, students, and external stakeholders.
- Feasible and prioritized recommendations for revising courses, developing new courses/modules, and strengthening practical teaching.
- Terms of Reference for subsequent curriculum-development or revision services.

## Capacity Building Components

- Recommendations for strengthening practical teaching.
- Curriculum-development/revision ToR.
- Assessment of knowledge, competencies, and practical training gaps.

## Team Requirements

The tender is open to private companies, public institutions, NGOs, professional associations, or consortia established and operating under Moldovan law. A successful bidder must be a legal entity registered in Moldova for at least 12 months before the deadline and must ensure no actual or potential conflict of interest with TUM. The technical offer must present understanding, methodology, work plan, expert team, and relevant experience.

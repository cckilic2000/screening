## Executive Summary

EUACI has published a tender for assessment of HR policies and practices of Ukraine’s State Agency for Restoration and Development of Infrastructure under EU4Reconstruction Stream 2. The maximum budget is EUR 20,000 inclusive of taxes and all costs, and proposals are due by 7 August 2026 at 18:00 Kyiv time. The assignment will diagnose HR policies, recruitment, onboarding, leadership, learning, retention, compensation, and engagement, and produce recommendations, an implementation roadmap, an action plan, and a self-diagnosis tool.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultancy will assess SARDI’s personnel policy, HR processes, and human resource capacity to identify strengths, gaps, and priority areas for HR management development. The work includes inception and methodology development, desk review, analysis of the 2023 institutional merger, at least 15 senior-management/key-department interviews, anonymous staff survey, HR policy and process assessment, compensation and reward analysis, recommendations, reporting, and validation workshop. Beneficiaries are SARDI and its ecosystem, including central and regional/subordinate entities involved in reconstruction.

## Key Deliverables

- Detailed work plan and assessment methodology with preliminary Assessment Report structure.
- Data-collection tools, including request for data, semi-structured interview guide, and anonymous online staff survey questionnaire.
- Draft Assessment Report with executive summary, findings, conclusions, gaps, risks, priority areas, recommendations, roadmap, action plan, and self-diagnosis tool.
- Validation workshop materials package including agenda and presentation of results and recommendations.
- Final Assessment Report revised after validation, with action plan, executive summary presentation, and summary of feedback.
- Bi-weekly progress reports throughout the assignment.

## Capacity Building Components

- Assessment of learning and professional development systems.
- Recommendations for staff professional development system improvement.
- Self-diagnosis tool for HR policies and practices.
- Knowledge transfer through validation workshop and action plan.

## Team Requirements

The contractor may be an individual expert, team of experts, company, or NGO with at least two HR, organizational capacity, and/or institutional assessment projects for public sector institutions. The team must include two key experts: a Team Lead/Senior HR Development Expert with a master’s degree, at least seven years of relevant experience, and at least three related assignments; and an HR Consultant with a master’s degree, at least five years of relevant experience, and at least three related assignments. English fluency is required for the team lead; Ukrainian is an asset for the team lead and required for the HR Consultant.

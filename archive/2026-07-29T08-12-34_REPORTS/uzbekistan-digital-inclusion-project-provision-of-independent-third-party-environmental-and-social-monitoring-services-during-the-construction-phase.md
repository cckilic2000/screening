## Executive Summary

The Uzbekistan Digital Inclusion Project is seeking a consulting firm for independent third-party environmental and social monitoring during construction. The assignment covers ITES centers in Bukhara, Syrdarya, Qarshi, and Andijan and is expected to last about 38 months. EOIs are due by Aug 12, 2026 at 18:00, and selection will use CQS.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The objective is to provide independent monitoring, compliance verification, auditing, and advisory services to the PIU throughout the construction period. The scope includes review of ESCP, ESIAs/ESMPs, contractor C-ESMPs, labor management, stakeholder engagement, grievance redress, national legislation, contractor E&S performance, environmental monitoring, and social risk management. The expected impact is stronger independent oversight of E&S compliance during construction of digital inclusion infrastructure.

## Key Deliverables

- Independent monitoring and verification of ESCP, ESIAs/ESMPs, C-ESMPs, LMP, SEP, GRM, and national legislation compliance.
- Independent verification of civil works contractors’ E&S performance.
- Verification of Construction Supervision Consultant E&S oversight effectiveness.
- Environmental monitoring and compliance verification for pollution prevention, waste, hazardous materials, air quality, noise, OHS, and emergency preparedness.
- Social risk management and compliance monitoring including labor, community health and safety, workers’ welfare, SEA/SH, and GBV prevention.

## Team Requirements

The firm must have not less than ten years of continuous experience implementing E&S assignments for internationally financed infrastructure projects, proven independent E&S auditor/third-party monitoring experience on at least three large-scale infrastructure projects, and annual turnover of at least USD 200,000 for any of the last two years. JV requirements specify that the lead partner must meet general experience and financial capacity independently, while specific experience may be met jointly under stated conditions.

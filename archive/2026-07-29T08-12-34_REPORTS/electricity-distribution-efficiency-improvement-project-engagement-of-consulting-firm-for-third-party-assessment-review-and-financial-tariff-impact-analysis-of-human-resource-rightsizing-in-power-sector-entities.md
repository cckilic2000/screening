The Ministry of Energy is implementing EDEIP with World Bank financial assistance to support affordable electricity, energy security, and sustainability. The PIU intends to hire consultancy services for third-party assessment review and financial/tariff impact analysis of HR rightsizing across power sector entities. Expected outputs include assessment and analysis to support staffing rationalization and financial/tariff impact understanding; the detailed ToR is referenced on power.gov.pk but not included in the fetched page text.

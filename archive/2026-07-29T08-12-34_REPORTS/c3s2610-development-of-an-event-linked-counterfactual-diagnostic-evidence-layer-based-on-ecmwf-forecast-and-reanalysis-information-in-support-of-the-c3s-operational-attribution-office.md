## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment aims to develop an evidence layer that uses ECMWF forecast and reanalysis products to derive event-specific counterfactual diagnostics. These diagnostics will support assessment of how observed extreme-event intensity, probability, or other characteristics may have differed without human-induced climate change.

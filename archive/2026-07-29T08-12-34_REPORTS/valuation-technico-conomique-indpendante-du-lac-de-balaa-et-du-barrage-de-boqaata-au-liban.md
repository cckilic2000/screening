## Executive Summary

AFD has a consultation for an independent technical and economic evaluation of Lac de Balaa and the Boqaata dam in Lebanon. The deadline is 15/09/2026 12:00 Paris time, with reference EAA-2026-0295. The assignment includes critical documentation review, field progress assessment, comparison of alternatives, feasibility of a recommended alternative, and a roadmap for remaining studies, analyses, and works.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment concerns an independent technical and economic evaluation of Lac de Balaa in the North Lebanon Governorate and the Boqaata dam in Mount Lebanon Governorate. A recognized international engineering office will conduct a critical analysis of available technical documentation, evaluate field progress, and provide an independent reasoned view on whether to resume and finalize the projects. The office will define objectives and implementation conditions for alternatives and produce feasibility for a recommended alternative plus a roadmap for remaining work.

## Key Deliverables

- Critical analysis of available technical documentation.
- Assessment of the state of progress of works in the field.
- Independent reasoned opinion on whether to resume and finalize the projects.
- Comparison of possible alternatives.
- Objectives and implementation conditions for alternatives.
- Feasibility of a recommended alternative.
- Roadmap for remaining studies, analyses, and works for the recommended alternative.

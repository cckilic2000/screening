## Executive Summary

Georgia’s Ministry of Environmental Protection and Agriculture has issued an REOI for institutional strengthening of sustainable agricultural land management under the World Bank-financed GRAIL project. Expressions of interest are due by Sep 01, 2026 at 17:00. The assignment will develop strategy, business processes, ICT and data architecture, SOPs, a five-year public finance model, and a capacity-building framework with ToT curriculum and technical user guides.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment is designed to assess existing institutional, legal, operational, and technical arrangements for land management and convert mandates into clear workflows and business processes. The intended beneficiaries are the Land Management Agency and the MLMD of MEPA. Expected outputs include institutional strategy, process redesign, ICT and data architecture, SLM operational plans, land monitoring feasibility work, public finance modelling, and internal capacity building.

## Key Deliverables

- Institutional strategy for LMA and relevant MEPA structures.
- Roadmap and action plan for sustainable agricultural land management.
- Business process mapping and reengineering outputs.
- ICT strategy and enterprise architecture.
- Data architecture, metadata rules or instructions, and operational data flows.
- SOPs and operational plans for windbreaks, pasture inventory, sustainable pasture management, and related mandates.
- Assessment of remote sensing and LCLU monitoring feasibility.
- Comprehensive five-year public finance model.
- Capacity building framework with ToT curriculum and technical user guides.

## Capacity Building Components

- Comprehensive capacity assessment for LMA and MLMD.
- Capacity building framework.
- Training-of-trainers curriculum.
- Technical user guides and knowledge transfer for independent management of the strategy and roadmap.

## Team Requirements

The consultant must be a firm or consortium with at least 10 years of institutional reform, land management policy, and public sector governance experience; SLM expertise; BPR and conceptual systems/data architecture capability; remote sensing and LCLU monitoring expertise; geospatial/database/BI systems experience; capacity-building and ToT experience; and preferably World Bank, EU, or UN-funded project experience plus Georgia or South Caucasus land-management context familiarity. Associations through JV or sub-consultancy are permitted.

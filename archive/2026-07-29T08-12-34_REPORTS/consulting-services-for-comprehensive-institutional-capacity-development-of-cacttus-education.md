## Executive Summary

The Regional Challenge Fund is procuring a nineteen-month institutional capacity development assignment for CACTTUS Education in Kosovo. The budget is 70,000.00 EUR excluding VAT and proposals are due on 09 September 2026 at 14:00h CET. The work covers corporate governance, advisory board design, ownership restructuring, performance-based compensation, marketing and recruitment strategy, training, and post-strategy support.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment aims to strengthen CACTTUS Education’s business model, governance structures, human resources systems, and market positioning. The scope is organized around three components: Corporate Governance System and Professional Advisory Board, Performance-Based Compensation Scheme, and Marketing and Recruitment Strategy. Expected outputs include assessments, recommendations, governance documents, implementation plans, training, financial diagnostics, compensation-system design, recruitment and marketing strategies, process design, support reports, and a final report.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception Report covering all three components.
- CACTTUS Education Corporate Governance Assessment with recommendations.
- Professional Advisory Board governance document and principles.
- Proposal for CACTTUS Education Ownership Restructuring.
- Implementation Plan for Corporate Restructuring and Corporate Governance principles.
- Training of Owners, Board, Advisory and Senior Management on key governance principles and proposed changes.
- Report on financial diagnostics of operations and business.
- Report on the design and development of the Performance-Based Compensation System.
- Report on Year 1 monitoring of PBCS implementation.
- Marketing strategy and communication plan.
- Recruitment strategy.
- Process design for marketing, recruitment, and collaboration.
- 6-month post-strategy support report.
- Final Report summarising all components, proposed schemes, and conclusions.

## Capacity Building Components

- Training of owners, board, advisory and senior management on governance principles and proposed changes.
- Bi-monthly post-strategy support sessions for the CACTTUS Education team.
- Knowledge transfer through implementation plans, reports, and guidance materials.

## Team Requirements

Firm-level requirements include at least seven years of establishment with HR management and organizational development consulting experience, at least two similar assignments in the last five years designing customized performance-based compensation schemes, and education-sector organizational development experience as an asset. Required key experts are a Senior Corporate Governance and Management Consultant/Project Manager, Finance and Accounting Management Consultant, Senior HR Management and Organisational Development Consultant, and Marketing and Sales Strategy Consultant.

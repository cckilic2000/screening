## Executive Summary

IFC seeks a qualified consulting firm to conduct a Private Sector Mapping & Diagnostic of Healthcare Facilities in the West Bank. The assignment will map hospitals, pharmaceutical entities, and medical suppliers, develop registries, validate data, benchmark providers, and identify service gaps and structural investment barriers. The fetched page does not publish a deadline, budget, duration, or detailed expert criteria.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The objective is to create a verified and analytically robust picture of the private health sector in the West Bank. The scope includes a facility registry, service registry, data collection and validation, benchmarking with local, regional and global peers, gap analysis, and recommendations for follow-on opportunities. The stated beneficiaries are private sector health entities and the broader effort to support select healthcare entities.

## Key Deliverables

- Comprehensive facility registry of private sector health entities.
- Service registry for identified providers covering service provision, quality, and financial performance.
- Data collection and validation support.
- Benchmarking interaction with selected private sector entities.
- Identification of service gaps, geographic disparities, and structural barriers.
- Synthesis of findings and recommendations for follow-on opportunities.

## Executive Summary

AD ESM in North Macedonia has issued an REOI for project preparation of the Bitola gas-fired CHP plant and district heating network expansion. The assignment is expected to start in early 2027 and be completed within 12 months, with EOIs due by Aug 07, 2026 at 23:45. The scope includes feasibility studies, basic and technical designs, environmental and social instruments, capacity building, financial planning, budgeting, tariff/cost recovery, and implementation arrangements.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The services include technical and economic preparation for a gas-fired CHP plant, gas pipeline connection, associated facilities, and district heating network expansion. The consultant will also prepare environmental and social risk management instruments, including ESIA/ESMP, RPF/RAP, LMP, and SEP in line with World Bank requirements. Institutional support includes project management, procurement, contract management, E&S risk management, financial planning, budgeting, cost recovery, tariff considerations, and operationalization of implementation arrangements.

## Key Deliverables

- Feasibility study and basic design for Bitola gas-fired CHP plant and gas pipeline connection.
- Assessment of optimal capacity, technology options, fuel supply assumptions, heat offtake, and system integration.
- Feasibility study and technical designs for Bitola district heating network expansion.
- Demand assessment, network routing, phasing, and investment prioritization.
- Environmental and social assessments and instruments including ESIA/ESMP, RPF/RAP, LMP, and SEP.
- Capacity building and training activities for ESM.
- Institutional strengthening for financial planning, budgeting, cost recovery, tariff considerations, and project implementation arrangements.

## Capacity Building Components

- Capacity building and training to enhance ESM’s project preparation and implementation capacity.
- Training/support on project management, procurement planning, contract management, and E&S risk management.
- Learning exchanges are included under institutional and capacity building support.

## Team Requirements

The consulting firm must have at least ten years of engineering and consulting experience, at least 50% of key experts employed by the firm, average annual turnover of at least EUR 300.000 equivalent in the last five years, at least two ESIA assignments for thermal power/related energy infrastructure each with contract value at least EUR 300,000, and at least one EU/Western Balkans feasibility study for CHP or CCHP plant of at least 100MW. Key experts are not evaluated at shortlisting. Associations are permitted with detailed JV/subconsultancy rules.

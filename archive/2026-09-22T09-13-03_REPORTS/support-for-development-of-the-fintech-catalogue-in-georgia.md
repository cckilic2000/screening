## Executive Summary

IFC has issued EOI 0002023721 for a fintech catalogue in Georgia. The selected firm will survey and map fintech companies, analyse the sector, and prepare profiles and draft catalogue reports. The TOR requires expertise in fintech technologies, digital financial services and analytical reporting. EOIs close on 28 September 2026 at 23:59 EDT.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consulting firm will identify and classify fintech companies, design a survey instrument, compile and process responses, build sector analytics and ecosystem visualisations, and prepare standardised company profiles. It will integrate analysis, maps and profiles into draft catalogue reports with charts and summaries for IFC and the relevant fintech associations. The TOR names Georgia throughout the practical tasks, although a few generic sentences refer to “both countries”.

## Key Deliverables

- Fintech ecosystem database of companies operating in Georgia.
- Fintech ecosystem map and segment classification.
- Draft statistical and analytical outputs.
- Draft standardised company profiles.
- Draft fintech catalogue report integrating analysis, maps and profiles.

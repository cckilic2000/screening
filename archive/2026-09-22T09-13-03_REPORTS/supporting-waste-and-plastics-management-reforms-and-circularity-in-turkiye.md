## Executive Summary

The World Bank has opened EOI 0002023707 for waste and plastics-management reform advice in Türkiye. The assignment has two components: a national solid-waste roadmap and support to scale up the deposit-management system. The draft TOR calls for finance and PPP analysis, policy reforms, municipal investment prioritisation and training. EOIs close on 13 October 2026 at 23:59 EDT.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

Component 1 includes an inception phase, national waste-sector assessment, analysis of three to five priority municipalities, infrastructure-investment prioritisation, private-sector and PPP review, and a consolidated roadmap aligned with Turkish strategies and EU benchmarks. Component 2 assesses the deposit-management system, advises on technical integration and financing, prepares a scale-up implementation plan and provides TUCA with training, workshops and international knowledge exchange. Reports are required in English and Turkish with companion presentations.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report at four weeks.
- Draft waste-sector analysis at eight weeks.
- Draft deposit-management-system technical and financing report at 16 weeks.
- Draft municipal, infrastructure and PPP assessment at 18 weeks.
- Final DMS assessment and guidance note at 24 weeks.
- Final Waste and Plastics Management Reforms Roadmap at 28 weeks, with English/Turkish presentations.

## Capacity Building Components

- Targeted TUCA staff training on technical, operational and legal DMS issues.
- Stakeholder workshops, study tours and a regional learning event; the draft TOR leaves the counts as X.
- Knowledge exchange and strategic communication advice for DMS scale-up.

## Team Requirements

The firm must cover solid-waste management, infrastructure finance, circular-economy policy, EPR/DMS and private-sector models. The draft TOR names six roles: team leader/senior waste-management specialist (15 years), infrastructure and investment specialist (10), circular-economy policy specialist (10), DMS/EPR specialist (10), private-sector financing/PPP specialist (8), and capacity-building/stakeholder-engagement specialist (8). Turkish is required for the last role; international, national and municipal expertise is expected.

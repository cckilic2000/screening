## Executive Summary

UNOPS has issued RFQ/2026/64732 for design of a CNG supply station at the psychiatric hospital in Skopje. The assignment is part of the EU for Clean Air project and requires a company licensed for second-category design work in North Macedonia. Deliverables include complete multi-discipline design documentation and bills of quantities within 90 days of contract start. Quotations close on 5 October 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The selected company will verify site conditions and coordinate with an existing heating-system design and a separate boiler-room reconstruction project. It will design the CNG storage, decompression, metering, regulation and gas connections, together with architectural, structural, electrical, fire-safety, occupational-safety and environmental components. The design must support permit applications and a later works procurement, with submissions at 60% and 90% design stages.

## Key Deliverables

- Basic multi-discipline CNG station design, drawings, calculations and technical specifications.
- Detailed bills of quantities and estimated construction costs for a subsequent works tender.
- Fire-protection, health-and-safety and environmental assessment elaborates.
- Documentation needed for approvals and permits; 60% and 90% review submissions and final corrected design.

## Capacity Building Components

- COULDN'T BE FOUND

## Team Requirements

Company with at least five years of design-service experience and a valid North Macedonian Licence B; at least five qualifying mechanical projects in five years and USD 50,000 in prior design contracts. The bidder or consortium lead needs two permanently employed engineers. The team must cover architectural, construction, mechanical, electrical, fire-safety, health-and-safety and environmental designers with relevant Authorization B and project experience; some safety/environment roles may be combined if authorised. Joint ventures are permitted.

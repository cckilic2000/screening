## Executive Summary

The EU is procuring 24 months of technical assistance for Albania’s Chapter 22 and Cohesion Policy preparations. The contract is estimated at 1 750 000,00 EUR excluding VAT. It will support closing benchmarks and strengthen Albanian institutions’ administrative and strategic capacities. Requests to participate close on 26 October 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The programme supports relevant Albanian authorities in meeting Chapter 22 requirements and developing the capacity to manage EU Cohesion Policy after accession. The published scope includes closing benchmarks and action-plan measures, with a forward-looking focus on institutional, administrative and strategic systems. The participation document specifies firm-level capacity and reference requirements, but a detailed implementation TOR and named deliverables are not yet public.

## Key Deliverables

- COULDN'T BE FOUND

## Capacity Building Components

- Strengthening institutional, administrative and strategic capacities for future EU Cohesion Policy management is an explicit objective; training formats are not yet specified.

## Team Requirements

Eligible natural or legal persons from EU Member States or other IPA III eligible countries may bid alone or as a consortium; international/regional organisations are also eligible. Candidates need average annual turnover of at least EUR 1.4 million over three closed financial years, a current ratio of at least 1 for each consortium member, an average of at least three specialist personnel over the current and prior two years, and two relevant EU Cohesion Policy contracts in the preceding four years with at least EUR 500,000 in completed services each. Four to eight candidates will be shortlisted. Named key experts are not yet published.

## Strategic Notes For FS

## Risks / Concerns

- Restricted two-stage procedure and 4–8-candidate shortlist.
- EUR 1.4 million turnover and two EUR 500,000 EU Cohesion Policy references are required.

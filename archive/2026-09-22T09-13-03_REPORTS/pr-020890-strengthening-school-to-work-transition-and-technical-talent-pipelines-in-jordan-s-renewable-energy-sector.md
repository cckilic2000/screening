## Executive Summary

EBRD PR 020890 targets the transition from education to renewable-energy employment in Jordan. The selected consultant will design a company-certified apprenticeship programme and improve mentoring, career progression and workforce planning. The event summary estimates an 18-month assignment starting in November 2026 and a EUR 100,000 cost. The public GEP listing closes on 20 October 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

EBRD will support a renewable-energy developer in Jordan as an anchor employer and demand-led skills provider. The assignment is to introduce a structured apprenticeship programme for early-career professionals, while upgrading mentorship, performance management, career pathways and workforce planning. The public summary calls for labour-market-led skills and inclusion expertise; the full procurement dossier is available only to registered SMART suppliers.

## Key Deliverables

- Structured company-certified apprenticeship programme for early-career renewable-energy professionals.
- Strengthened mentorship, performance-management, career-progression and workforce-planning arrangements.

## Team Requirements

The public event summary names a Team Leader / Work-Based Learning Expert, Skills Development Specialist, and HR Transformation & Gender Inclusion Specialist.

## Executive Summary

UNDP Jordan has issued RFP UNDP-JOR-00722 for a low/no-cost energy-efficiency manual and implementation toolkit. The work supports critical-facility resilience and national demand flexibility. The selected company must coordinate its tools and recommendations with related project outputs. Proposals are due through UNDP Quantum on 1 October 2026 at 05:00 New York time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The project seeks practical demand flexibility solutions in response to energy-market and supply-chain disruptions. The selected company is to prepare a manual and implementation tools for selected facilities and align institutional responsibilities, reporting arrangements and rollout methods with a national coordination mechanism. Where relevant, it will coordinate with the team working on a potential automated demand-side management and demand-response pilot.

## Key Deliverables

- Low/no-cost energy-efficiency manual for selected facilities.
- Implementation toolkit aligned with the national demand-flexibility mechanism and action plan.
- Recommendations on load management, operating schedules, peak-demand reduction and monitoring, where relevant to the pilot.

## Executive Summary

FEMALE and Terre des Hommes Germany seek a feasibility study for a proposed feminist action and protection project in Lebanon and the wider MENA region. The work includes a desk study, fieldwork and a final report with recommendations on the project proposal. Offers are due on 2 October 2026. The notice states a remuneration range above USD 6,000 and a two-month contract.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The proposed intervention aims to improve women’s and girls’ access to safe, integrated protection and support services while strengthening coordination among feminist civil-society actors. The feasibility consultant will review existing evidence, conduct fieldwork, assess the local partner and target groups, apply OECD-DAC criteria, and recommend changes to the project design. The final report is limited to 30 pages excluding annexes.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report following the preparatory meeting and desk study.
- Fieldwork following the agreed methodology.
- Draft feasibility report for comments by TDH Germany and FEMALE.
- Final English feasibility report, up to 30 pages excluding annexes, with methodology, context, partner and stakeholder analyses, OECD-DAC assessment, recommendations and supporting annexes.

## Capacity Building Components

- The proposed project concerns capacity development of feminist civil-society organisations; the consultancy itself specifies research and recommendations rather than training delivery.

## Team Requirements

Applicants must show prior research and feasibility-assessment experience on internationally funded projects; participatory and child-sensitive methods; Middle East experience, preferably Lebanon/MENA; knowledge of NGOs and gender-based violence; strong English, with Arabic preferred. Offers must include a staffing plan, two NGO client references, a portfolio and CVs/profiles of key consultants. The notice does not specify a mandatory consortium or named roles.

## Strategic Notes For FS

- Lebanon and MENA fall within the target geography.
- The feasibility report can shape a proposed multi-country protection programme.

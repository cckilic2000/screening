## Executive Summary

DG REGIO is procuring a 36-month technical-assistance service for food-safety and animal-health management in the Turkish Cypriot community. The estimated value is 2 750 000,00 EUR excluding VAT. Candidates must meet financial, staffing and prior-contract thresholds in the published participation documents. Requests to participate close on 3 November 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The published notice states the goal of supporting, guiding, upgrading and accelerating implementation of EU-aligned food-safety and animal-health standards in the Turkish Cypriot community. The additional-information document sets the eligibility and shortlisting criteria, including specialist personnel and completed contracts in both domains. A detailed technical TOR and named deliverables are not in the currently published document bundle.

## Team Requirements

Eligible legal persons may bid alone or in a consortium if established in an EU Member State or another country/territory authorised under Regulation 389/2006; international organisations are also eligible. Candidates need average annual turnover of at least EUR 1.8 million over the last three closed years, a current ratio of at least 1, five specialist personnel covering EU food safety, animal health, official controls, hygiene and PRAG project management, and three relevant contracts in the preceding four years, each with completed services of at least EUR 1.1 million. At least one contract must cover each of food safety and animal health. Four to eight candidates will be shortlisted.

## Executive Summary

Eurofound has published an expression-of-interest notice for a planned study of algorithmic management in European workplaces. The future contract would develop nine company case studies covering financial services, ICT and manufacturing. The studies will assess effects on managerial roles, work organisation, conditions and labour relations. Expressions of interest close on 6 October 2026; this notice is not yet the call for tenders.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The planned study will examine how digital systems automate or support managerial functions such as task allocation, work scheduling and performance monitoring. It will develop nine in-depth case studies across selected European companies and assess the consequences for managers, workers and labour relations. The notice does not publish company locations, detailed methods, expert profiles or a project budget.

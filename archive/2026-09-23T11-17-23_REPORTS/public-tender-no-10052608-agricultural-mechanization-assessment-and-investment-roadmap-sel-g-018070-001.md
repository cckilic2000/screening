## Executive Summary

GIZ is commissioning an integrated Agricultural Mechanization Assessment and Investment Roadmap for SEL target areas in Lebanon. The work spans producers, machinery-service providers, private-sector actors, and the national enabling environment, with specific attention to finance, business models, market services, digital solutions, capacity needs, and policy and PPP opportunities. The resulting evidence base is intended to prioritize investments and practical recommendations that improve sustainable and affordable access to mechanization for agricultural producers and MSMEs.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The Strengthening the Local Economy project seeks to improve the competitiveness of agricultural producers and MSMEs through better institutional frameworks, stronger technical and entrepreneurial services, market access, and sustainable value chains. The assessment covers Minieh-Dinnieh and Akkar in North Lebanon; Zahle, South of Baalbek, and West Beqaa; and Chouf and Aley in Mount Lebanon.

The contractor will assess mechanization capacity and demand-supply gaps; technical, financial, operational, and institutional constraints; rental, custom-hiring, shared-machinery, and cooperative service models; repair and after-sales services; local manufacturing and refurbishment; finance and business models; digital solutions; capacity needs; and policy, institutional, and PPP opportunities. The output is intended to guide investments, service delivery, partnerships, capacity measures, and policy or institutional reforms.

## Key Deliverables

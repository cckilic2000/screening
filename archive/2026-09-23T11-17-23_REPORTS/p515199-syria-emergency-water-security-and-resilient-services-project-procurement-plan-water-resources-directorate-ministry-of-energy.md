## Executive Summary

This World Bank procurement plan sets out civil works, goods, non-consulting services, consulting-firm assignments, and individual-consultant positions for Syria's Emergency Water Security and Resilient Services Project. The largest published packages are water and wastewater rehabilitation works, while consulting packages cover design and supervision, dam safety, climate and water information, and transboundary cooperation. The document is a procurement plan with planned future milestones, not a single tender with one deadline or one team specification.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The works programme includes rehabilitation of the Ain Al-Zarqa water supply, the Upper Orontes treatment and supply system, and the Adra wastewater treatment plant. Goods and non-consulting procurement covers emergency equipment and resilient supplies plus clearance of landmines and explosive remnants of war.

Consulting-firm packages include owner-engineer services for design, procurement, and supervision; engineering design and supervision; wastewater-treatment preparatory studies; and technical assistance for dam safety, a water and climate information and control centre, and transboundary waters cooperation. The plan also lists national individual-consultant roles for project coordination and environmental, social, procurement, financial-management, and monitoring functions.

## Key Deliverables

- Ain Al-Zarqa water-supply rehabilitation works, estimated at US$28,500,000.
- Upper Orontes water-treatment and supply rehabilitation works, estimated at US$13,000,000.
- Adra wastewater-treatment-plant rehabilitation works, estimated at US$55,000,000.
- Emergency equipment and resilient supplies, estimated at US$15,000,000.
- Landmine and ERW site clearance, estimated at US$4,000,000.
- Owner-engineer design, procurement, and supervision services, estimated at US$4,000,000.
- Engineering design and supervision services, estimated at US$8,500,000.
- Wastewater-treatment preparatory studies, estimated at US$7,000,000.
- Three technical-assistance packages for dam safety, water and climate information, and transboundary waters cooperation.

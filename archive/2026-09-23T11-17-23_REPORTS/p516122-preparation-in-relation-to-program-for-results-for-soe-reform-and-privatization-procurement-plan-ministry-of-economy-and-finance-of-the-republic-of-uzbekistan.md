## Executive Summary

The Ministry of Economy and Finance of Uzbekistan has a US$1.5 million World Bank project-preparation grant for advisory work supporting an SOE Reform and Privatisation Program for Results. The plan includes consulting assignments on SOE screening and restructuring, IPO feasibility, fiscal and state-aid analysis, corporate-governance assessment and education, climate-risk and decarbonisation planning, social impacts, accounting and public-service obligations, and a digital SOE register. Firm procurements include both international and national market approaches, while four specialist assignments use individual-consultant selection.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The largest planned assignment is a US$615,000 international QCBS package for pre-assessment of 30 SOEs, capacity building on share-price monitoring, and strategic feasibility advice on five potential IPOs. Three CQS packages cover fiscal and state-aid assessments, corporate-governance gap assessment and education, and climate-risk analysis with a decarbonisation roadmap.

Four additional specialist assignments address preparation of the programme expenditure framework; social-impact, labour, and distributional analysis of SOE divestiture; accounting and separation of non-commercial activities and public-service obligations; and technical specifications for a centralised digital SOE register and performance-management tool. The plan supports Ministry of Economy and Finance, UzAssets, and SAMA preparation activities.

## Key Deliverables

- Technical documentation for the programme expenditure framework.
- Social-impact assessment of SOE divestiture, including labour and distributional analysis.
- Mapping of non-commercial activities and public-service-obligation guidelines.
- Technical specifications for a centralised digital SOE register and performance-management tool.
- Pre-assessment of 30 SOEs, share-price-monitoring capacity building, and strategic feasibility advice on five potential IPOs.
- Fiscal and state-aid assessments.
- OECD corporate-governance gap assessment, board-effectiveness tool, and corporate-governance education programme.
- Climate-risk assessment, scenario modelling, analytical report, and decarbonisation roadmap for the UzAssets SOE portfolio.

## Capacity Building Components

- Capacity building on share-price monitoring.
- Corporate-governance education for corporate secretaries, management boards, and supervisory boards.
- Board-effectiveness tools and institutional corporate-governance assessment.
- Technical specifications and performance-management tools for the SOE register.

## Executive Summary

The Nabni / Building for Peace Facility is establishing an eight-lot consultant roster for programme-quality, learning, strategy, governance, livelihoods, safeguards, infrastructure, inclusion, and organisational-development support across MENA. NRC may select up to five consultants per lot for as-needed remote or field assignments. The roster accepts individual consultants, sole traders, and consultancy firms, provides no guaranteed assignments or financial commitment, and requires submission through NRC's eTB system.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

Roster members will support the Facility Management Unit and Nabni partner organisations with programme design and implementation, quality assurance, learning, strategy development, and organisational strengthening. Applicants may apply for one or more lots covering peacebuilding and conflict sensitivity; governance; civil engineering; livelihoods and economic recovery; gender and youth inclusion; MEL; environmental and social safeguards; and partner capacity strengthening and organisational development.

Assignments may be short or medium term and delivered remotely, in country, or through a mixed model. The notice is open to eligible bidders without a margin of preference, but detailed task-level deliverables will depend on future call-offs.

## Key Deliverables

- Establishment of a consultant roster with up to five consultants per lot.
- As-needed technical assistance to the Facility Management Unit and partner organisations.
- Programme-design and implementation support.
- Quality-assurance, learning, strategy, and organisational-strengthening support.
- Assignment-specific outputs COULDN'T BE FOUND because they will be defined in future call-offs.

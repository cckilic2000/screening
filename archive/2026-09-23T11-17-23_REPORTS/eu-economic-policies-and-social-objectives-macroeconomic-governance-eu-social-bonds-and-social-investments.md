## Executive Summary

The European Parliament intends to launch a negotiated middle/low-value procedure for a study on EU economic policies and social objectives. The study will examine the position of social objectives in the EU macroeconomic-governance framework, social bonds as public-borrowing and private-investment tools, and the EU's support for social investment. The estimated value is EUR 100,000, the maximum duration is 13 months, and the minimum team is three postgraduate experts in relevant areas of macroeconomics, social policy, and finance.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The study is intended to provide the European Parliament's Committee on Employment and Social Affairs with empirically sound, policy-relevant analysis. It will assess how social objectives are incorporated into EU macroeconomic governance and how EU economic policy can support social objectives at Member State and EU levels.

The work also examines the potential of social bonds to complement public spending, both through EU borrowing on financial markets and through mobilisation of private investment in the welfare sector. A third strand assesses how the EU supports social investments, particularly through macroeconomic governance and social-bond policy levers.

## Key Deliverables

- Empirically sound and policy-relevant study for the Committee on Employment and Social Affairs.
- Analysis of social objectives in the EU macroeconomic-governance framework.
- Assessment of social bonds as EU borrowing instruments and as tools for mobilising private welfare-sector investment.
- Assessment of EU support for social investment through the identified policy levers.

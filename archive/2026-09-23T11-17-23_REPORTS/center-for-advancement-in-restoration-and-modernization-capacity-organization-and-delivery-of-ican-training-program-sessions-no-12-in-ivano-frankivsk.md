## Executive Summary

KSE Institute is procuring complete logistical and organisational support for two concurrent I_CAN training sessions in Ivano-Frankivsk. Each event may host up to 75 participants and requires compliant venues, accommodation, catering, participant logistics, technical equipment, registration, badges, and continuous on-site support. The procurement is financed under the World Bank-supported CARMC project, but the requested service does not include curriculum development or substantive training delivery.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The provider must support Event No. 1 from 5–8 October 2026 and Event No. 2 from 6–9 October 2026. The events must operate as two independent training groups in separate acoustically isolated halls at one venue or at two suitable venues in or within 20 kilometres of Ivano-Frankivsk.

The scope covers accessible venues with internal air-raid shelters, furniture, reliable power and internet, accommodation, daily catering, badges and branded materials, travel coordination and eligible reimbursements, and audiovisual equipment. A technical specialist and an organisational representative must remain on site throughout the events and coordinate technical operation, registration, documentation, logistics, subcontractors, and emergency procedures.

## Key Deliverables

- Full organisation and logistical support for two overlapping four-day training events.
- One compliant venue with two isolated halls or two compliant venues for concurrent groups.
- Accommodation arrangements for non-local participants and, when requested, lecturers and organisers.
- Daily catering and refreshment services.
- Participant badges, branded materials, registration materials, and consumables.
- Participant travel arrangements and documented reimbursement administration.
- Audio, presentation, internet, backup-power, and other technical support.
- On-site technical specialist and organisational representative for the entire event period.

## Capacity Building Components

- Logistical and technical enablement of two I_CAN training sessions.
- Provision of group-work spaces, presentation equipment, registration, and learning-event materials.
- No curriculum development, facilitation, coaching, Training of Trainers, or substantive knowledge-transfer task for the contractor COULDN'T BE FOUND.

## Team Requirements

The provider must demonstrate at least one contract since 1 January 2023 covering comprehensive logistics for conferences, trainings, or similar events and must not be in bankruptcy proceedings. During delivery, the provider must supply one technical specialist and one organisational representative on site throughout the events. Joint ventures are permitted, with members jointly and severally liable; firms and individual entrepreneurs may participate subject to the stated eligibility and sanctions rules.

## Strategic Notes For FS

- The procurement supports a World Bank-financed capacity-development project in Ukraine.
- The scope is event logistics, not substantive advisory or training content.
- Two large training groups must operate concurrently under strict safety and continuity requirements.
- The source specifies no financial-sector, enterprise-development, or policy-reform content for these sessions.

## Risks / Concerns

- The first event begins only five calendar days after the bid deadline.
- Two concurrent events require venue capacity, acoustic separation, shelters, accommodation, catering, and technical support for up to 150 participants.
- Bid documents and correspondence must be in Ukrainian, and prices must be in Ukrainian hryvnia.
- Award is based on the lowest evaluated price among technically compliant offers.
- The expected tender value is expressly not stated.

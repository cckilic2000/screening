## Executive Summary

FAO is prequalifying organisations for a 12-month package of climate-resilience, sustainable-water, biodiversity, and agrifood-system activities across Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, and Uzbekistan. The assignment includes community and needs assessments, SHARP+ fieldwork, pilot demonstration sites, training and ToT programmes, institutional capacity building, data analysis, stakeholder engagement, and knowledge products. The expected contracting mechanism is a Letter of Agreement with one or more non-governmental organisations.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment supports project outputs on climate services for decision support and integrated climate-resilient management of land, water, biodiversity, and ecosystems. The service provider will identify target communities and priority areas, conduct SHARP+ resilience assessments, and use the evidence to inform adaptation actions.

The provider will also establish and support pilot demonstration sites; prepare awareness, communication, and knowledge products; organise national and regional training workshops and ToT programmes; strengthen the capacities of government institutions, local organisations, and communities; and support data analysis, reporting, monitoring, evaluation, knowledge exchange, and cross-country learning.

## Key Deliverables

- Baseline and needs assessments identifying target communities and priority intervention areas.
- SHARP+ field assessments in selected project locations.
- Pilot demonstration sites for climate-resilient and sustainable land, water, biodiversity, and ecosystem management.
- Awareness-raising materials and communication products.
- Knowledge products supporting learning, replication, and dissemination.
- National and regional training workshops and Training of Trainers programmes.
- Technical reports, assessment findings, training materials, data analysis, and project reporting.
- Stakeholder-engagement, knowledge-exchange, and cross-country-learning activities.
- Ongoing technical advisory, monitoring, evaluation, and knowledge-sharing support.

## Capacity Building Components

## Executive Summary

The State Bank of Pakistan, under ADB's Women Inclusive Finance Program, is seeking an eligible consulting firm for a 12-month nationwide gender-focused Voice of Customer survey. The survey will establish a baseline on women's access, experience, satisfaction, treatment, redress, digital-finance usage, trust, financial literacy, and financial health, with particular attention to rural and underserved women. The assignment has a USD 71,200 budget and requires a 2,500-person nationally representative sample, policy recommendations, institutional guidance, and a five-person national expert team.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The firm will design a gender-sensitive survey and stratified, multistage sampling framework covering all four provinces, with at least 40% rural respondents and disaggregation by age, province, location, income, and banking status. It must conduct cognitive pre-testing, a pilot of at least 50 respondents, enumerator training, nationwide fieldwork, real-time data quality monitoring, at least 10% independent back-checks, and complete data cleaning, analysis, and transfer.

The outputs will inform gender-responsive supervision and policy reform, improve oversight of financial institutions' treatment of women, strengthen trust in formal finance, and guide institutionalisation of VoC mechanisms within SBP and banks. The work also covers guidance for a consumer-research Centre of Excellence, advanced/AI-based sentiment analysis on live data, and using survey findings to strengthen financial literacy and awareness.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report and data-management plan within two weeks of commencement.
- Gender-sensitive survey instrument, translations, methodology, sampling framework, and global VoC best-practices report.
- Stakeholder consultations, cognitive pre-testing, and a minimum 50-person pilot.
- Enumerator recruitment and training.
- Nationwide field survey of at least 2,500 respondents with at least 40% rural coverage.
- Monthly progress reports.
- Raw and tabulated datasets plus data analysis and interpretation.
- Draft and final analytical reports and policy recommendations.
- Guidance on institutionalising VoC mechanisms and establishing a consumer-research Centre of Excellence.
- Guidance on global VoC tools, AI-based sentiment analysis, and improving women's financial literacy and awareness.
- Final presentation to CPD and the WIFP Steering Committee.

## Capacity Building Components

- Enumerator recruitment and training.
- Guidance for institutionalising continuous VoC mechanisms within SBP and financial institutions.
- Baseline guidance for a consumer-research Centre of Excellence with a gender focus.
- Recommendations for using findings to improve women's financial literacy, capability, and awareness.
- Guidance on advanced and AI-based VoC and sentiment-analysis tools.

## Team Requirements

The national team comprises a Project Leader, Lead Researcher/Field Coordinator, Consumer Research and Survey Methodology Expert, Data Analyst, and Gender and Inclusion Specialist. Minimum experience requirements are 10 years for the Project Leader; seven years for the Lead Researcher and Consumer Research Expert; and five years for the Gender Specialist and Data Analyst, alongside the specified advanced or relevant degrees. Firms must be from ADB member countries; a joint venture of no more than two members is permitted, and subcontracting is not allowed.

## Executive Summary

The Government of Kazakhstan is seeking IsDB financing for reconstruction and construction of the Kyzylorda–Saksaulsk road corridor. IsDB's EUR 376.05 million financing will cover approximately 134.24 kilometres under an 11-year design-build-maintain model, while a domestic consulting-firm package will combine project management and construction supervision. The project is intended to improve transport connectivity, road safety, climate resilience, rural market access, and Kazakhstan's role in regional transit corridors.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

Component A covers construction, reconstruction, and upgrading of a corridor totaling approximately 462.03 kilometres, of which the IsDB-financed section is approximately 134.24 kilometres. The works include a four-lane Category I-B highway, earthworks, drainage, bridges, interchanges, road-safety features, intelligent transport systems, ancillary works, and seven years of performance-based maintenance after design and construction.

Component B combines a Project Management Consultant and Construction Supervision Consultant. The consultancy covers project management, procurement and contract administration, financial audit, technical and construction supervision, quality assurance, and monitoring of compliance with the Environmental and Social Management Plan.

## Key Deliverables

- Design, construction, reconstruction, and upgrading of the IsDB-financed road section.
- Earthworks, drainage, bridges, interchanges, road-safety features, intelligent transport systems, and ancillary works.
- Seven years of performance-based road maintenance following design and construction.
- Project-management consulting services.
- Procurement and contract-administration support.
- Financial audit.
- Technical and construction supervision.
- Quality assurance and ESMP-compliance monitoring.

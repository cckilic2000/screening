## Executive Summary

MCC is commissioning an external evaluation of a Syria earthquake-response portfolio comprising 20 projects delivered by nine local partners between 2023 and 2026. The approximately USD 2.7 million portfolio covers emergency relief, cash, winterisation, education, livelihoods, psychosocial support, peacebuilding, recovery, and partner capacity strengthening. The evaluator will assess relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, impact, sustainability, localisation, coherence, and contributions to recovery and resilience through field-based mixed methods.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The evaluation covers a multi-sector portfolio serving earthquake-affected communities in Syria. It will use document review, partner self-assessments, interviews, focus groups, data analysis, field visits, case studies, and stakeholder validation to assess portfolio performance against stated evaluation dimensions.

Applicants must be able to operate from within Syria, access targeted governorates, conduct in-person consultation, work fluently in Arabic, and report strongly in English. Relevant experience with evaluation, the Syrian humanitarian and development context, OECD-DAC criteria, humanitarian response, recovery, resilience, localisation, and multi-sector programming is required or preferred.

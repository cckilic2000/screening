## Executive Summary

PITA is seeking a qualified consultant or consulting company to review and update the PICTI/PITA Gender Policy under the BMZ-funded Inclusive Digital Transformation project. The assignment combines a policy gap analysis, a survey of ICT companies and employees, and drafting of a practical Gender and Inclusion Policy covering gender responsiveness, disability inclusion, accessibility, and intersectional equity. The work must be completed within 14 calendar days and culminates in a presentation at the Women's Empowerment Summit.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultant will review the existing policy and project documents, identify outdated or missing provisions, and design and conduct a structured survey of PITA member companies and women and men employees. Where feasible, the assessment will also capture the perspectives of persons with disabilities and organisations of persons with disabilities on accessibility, reasonable accommodation, and workplace inclusion.

Using the review and survey findings, the consultant will draft, revise, and finalise an institutional Gender and Inclusion Policy suitable for dissemination among PITA member companies and ICT stakeholders. The consultant will also present the findings, policy changes, and practical recommendations to the PICTI/PITA team and at the project's summit.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception note, methodology, work plan, and timeline.
- Policy review and initial gap analysis.
- Survey of PITA member companies and ICT-sector employees, with a concise findings analysis.
- Draft updated PICTI/PITA Gender and Inclusion Policy.
- Presentation of the draft policy and findings for PICTI/PITA review and feedback.
- Final Gender and Inclusion Policy in editable and final formats.
- Ten-minute presentation and Q&A at the Women's Empowerment Summit.

## Capacity Building Components

- Presentation of findings and proposed policy updates to the PICTI/PITA team.
- Dissemination of the policy as a reference for member companies and ICT-sector stakeholders.
- Summit presentation with practical recommendations for inclusive workplace practices.

## Team Requirements

Applicants may be a qualified consultant or consulting company. Required qualifications include a relevant academic qualification; at least five years of relevant experience; demonstrated gender/inclusion-policy experience; knowledge of gender equality, non-discrimination, gender-based-violence prevention, disability inclusion, accessibility, and reasonable accommodation; and strong analytical, drafting, consultation, and facilitation skills. Understanding of the Palestinian institutional and private-sector context is required; ICT-sector and donor-funded-project experience are assets.

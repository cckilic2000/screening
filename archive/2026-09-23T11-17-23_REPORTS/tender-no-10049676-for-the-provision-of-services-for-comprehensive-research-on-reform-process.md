## Executive Summary

GIZ is procuring a Moldovan research company to conduct a baseline survey on public attitudes toward territorial-administrative reform and amalgamation for the State Chancellery. The work covers national and regional survey design, fieldwork in Romanian and Russian, data cleaning and weighting, an Amalgamation Attitude Index, analysis, a final report and presentation, and delivery of datasets and technical materials. Bidders must be registered in Moldova and show at least seven years of organisational public-opinion or social-research experience.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The contractor will develop the survey methodology, sampling approach, and questionnaire in coordination with the commissioning organisation. It will implement one nationally representative survey plus booster samples in selected regions or priority areas, using documented fieldwork quality-control procedures.

The company will process, clean, and weight the data; develop an Amalgamation Attitude Index; analyse national and regional findings; and submit the final dataset, codebook, technical documentation, analytical report, and presentation. Relevant experience in governance reform, decentralisation, local development, civic participation, public services, or communication research is advantageous.

## Key Deliverables

- Survey methodology and sampling approach.
- Questionnaire developed with the commissioning organisation.
- One nationally representative survey.
- Regional booster samples in selected regions or priority areas.
- Processed, cleaned, and weighted data.
- Amalgamation Attitude Index.
- National and regional findings analysis.
- Final analytical report and presentation.
- Final dataset, codebook, technical documentation, and related research materials.

## Executive Summary

Acted is procuring a five-month consultancy to support 65 marginalised beneficiaries in the Jordan Valley and Ya'bad/Jenin with viable income-generating activities. The core requirement is four months of weekly, individual, field-based coaching totaling at least 1,131 direct hours, supported by beneficiary diagnostics, business planning, technical advice, marketing, and market linkages. The assignment requires a multidisciplinary firm with strong livelihoods, business-development, agriculture/livestock, product-development, and monitoring capacity.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultancy will begin with programme review, beneficiary diagnostics, and preparation of individual coaching plans. It will then deliver approximately two days of practical group orientation followed by weekly coaching tailored to each beneficiary's business, farm, livestock unit, workshop, home-based enterprise, or other IGA location.

Support covers business models, start-up and operational planning, costing, pricing, cash flow, records, procurement, production, agriculture and livestock, product quality and value addition, branding, marketing, digital promotion, sales, and institutional linkages. By completion, each beneficiary should have improved practices and market access plus a documented sustainability plan, while Acted receives verified coaching records, monthly reporting, and lessons for future programming.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report with methodology, work plan, coaching model, staffing and deployment plan, monitoring framework, reporting arrangements, and risk approach.
- Assessments and individual coaching plans for all 65 beneficiaries.
- Two-day orientation, training materials, attendance records, assessments where appropriate, participant feedback, and training report.
- Four months of weekly coaching totaling at least 1,131 direct coaching hours.
- Four monthly progress reports covering delivery, results, technical support, marketing, linkages, referrals, challenges, and corrective actions.
- Sustainability assessments and plans for all 65 beneficiaries.
- Final report covering implementation, results, business and technical progress, marketing, product development, linkages, lessons, and recommendations.

## Capacity Building Components

- Two-day practical orientation for 65 beneficiaries.
- Weekly individual coaching for four months, at least one hour per beneficiary per week.
- Entrepreneurship, business planning, costing, pricing, cash-flow, records, procurement, inventory, customer service, and risk-management support.
- Marketing, branding, packaging, digital promotion, sales, and customer-retention coaching.
- Agriculture and livestock production, quality, cost, and market-oriented technical support.
- Business mentoring, confidence building, problem solving, goal setting, resilience, and exit planning.

## Team Requirements

The consultancy firm must provide an appropriate multidisciplinary team with, at minimum, a Team Leader/Senior Business Development Specialist, enough Field Business Coaches to meet the weekly coverage requirement, and a Marketing/Product Development Specialist. The evaluation also requires relevant agriculture/livestock expertise; one person may fill more than one role if suitably qualified. The firm or proposed team should demonstrate at least five years of relevant experience, livelihoods/IGA and entrepreneurship/MSME work, experience with vulnerable or marginalised communities, and monitoring, quality-assurance, reporting, and risk-management capacity.

## Strategic Notes For FS

- The assignment requires intensive, measurable business coaching rather than one-off training.
- Financial-management topics include costing, pricing, cash flow, records, and operational financial challenges.
- Product development, value addition, marketing, and buyer linkages are central outputs.
- The model covers household enterprises, agriculture, livestock, services, trade, handicrafts, and home-based businesses.
- Payment is tied to verified outputs and coaching hours.

## Risks / Concerns

- The tender budget is not published; bidders must price in ILS inclusive of all costs and taxes.
- Delivery requires sustained field presence across dispersed Jordan Valley and Ya'bad/Jenin locations.
- Access restrictions, road closures, security constraints, weather, agricultural risks, livestock disease, and beneficiary dropout are identified risks.
- Acted may reduce monthly payments proportionately for unapproved shortfalls in verified coaching hours.
- The assignment demands at least 1,131 direct coaching hours plus travel, preparation, administration, and reporting.

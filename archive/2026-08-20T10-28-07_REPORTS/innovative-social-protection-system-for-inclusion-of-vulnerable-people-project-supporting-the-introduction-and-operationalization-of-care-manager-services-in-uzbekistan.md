## Executive Summary

The National Agency for Social Protection under the President of Uzbekistan is seeking consulting firm expressions of interest for support to introduce the Care Manager function under the INSON Project. The services include concept and standards development, assessment and care planning tools, referral mechanisms, monitoring and KPI frameworks, training of at least 210 Care Managers, a ToT framework, and preparation of 30 national trainers. Expressions of interest are due by 2 September 2026, and the assignment is expected to last 12 months.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The services include support for design and first-phase implementation of the Care Manager function within NASP, based on international experience and good practices. The Consultant will support development of the Care Management concept, standards, assessment and care planning tools, referral and service coordination mechanisms, monitoring and KPI frameworks, training and professional development arrangements, and related methodological and operational documents.

The assignment includes training of at least 210 Care Managers, development and implementation of a Training of Trainers framework, preparation of a national group of 30 trainers selected by NASP, and hands-on implementation support in Tashkent City and the Samarkand, Fergana, Kashkadarya, Navoi, and Khorezm regions.

## Team Requirements

Interested consultants must show at least five years of institutional experience in social protection, social services, Care Management, case management, integrated care, long-term care, medical-social services, person-centered care, or a closely related field. Required experience includes design and implementation support for coordinated service delivery models, operational and methodological instruments, training or ToT, monitoring of service quality, work with public institutions and stakeholders, and sufficient organizational, technical, and managerial capacity. Key Experts will not be evaluated at the shortlisting stage.

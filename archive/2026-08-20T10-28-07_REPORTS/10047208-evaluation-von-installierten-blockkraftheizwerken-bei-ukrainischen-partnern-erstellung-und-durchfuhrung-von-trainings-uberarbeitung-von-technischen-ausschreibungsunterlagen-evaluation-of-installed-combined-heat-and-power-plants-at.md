## Executive Summary

GIZ is tendering reference 10047208 for evaluation of installed combined heat and power plants at Ukrainian partners, training development and delivery, and revision of technical tender documents. The scope includes a questionnaire, evaluation travel to 10 Ukrainian municipalities, operator-manufacturer technical dialogue, a 1-2 day training in Kyiv, a troubleshooting manual, and evaluation/final reports. Offers are due electronically by 02.09.2026 at 12:00, with performance from 15.10.2026 to 30.04.2027.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The contractor is required to prepare a questionnaire for identifying operational problems with delivered CHP plants in Ukrainian municipalities, conduct an evaluation trip to 10 municipalities, and define existing problems by reviewing installation and use on site. The contractor must also support technical dialogue between plant operators and manufacturers.

The capacity building component includes creating a training concept for a 1-2 day training on CHP operation and possible operator errors in the Ukrainian context, and delivering the training offline in Kyiv for all GIZ recipient municipalities, municipal utility companies, and selected partners. The assignment also requires a troubleshooting short manual by CHP manufacturer and evaluation and final reports.

## Key Deliverables

- Questionnaire for identifying operational problems with delivered CHP plants.
- Evaluation trip to 10 municipalities in Ukraine.
- Support to technical dialogue between operators and manufacturers.
- Training concept for a 1-2 day training on CHP operation and possible operator errors.
- Offline training in Kyiv for GIZ recipient municipalities, municipal utility companies, and selected partners.
- Troubleshooting short manual by CHP manufacturers.

## Executive Summary

GIZ is tendering service provider support for Enterprise Architecture and technical specifications for modernization of Moldova’s Real Estate Cadastre Information System. The assignment runs from September 2026 to January 2027 and covers baseline validation, target architecture, transition roadmap, and procurement-ready specifications. The technical bid is weighted 70% and the financial bid 30%, with electronic applications due by 04.09.2026 at 17:00.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment supports RECIS modernization under the “Attractive and Efficient Public Administration” project. The contractor is expected to review and validate the existing IT baseline, develop a target Enterprise Architecture across business, application, data, integration, technology/platform, and security layers, and prepare a transition roadmap.

The scope also includes procurement-ready technical specifications covering functional, non-functional, data, migration, integration, interoperability, implementation, and transition requirements. The selected contractor must provide technical support to tender preparation, including consistency review of technical procurement documentation.

## Key Deliverables

- Review, validation, and structuring of the existing IT systems baseline for Enterprise Architecture purposes.
- Target Enterprise Architecture for the cadastral information system.
- Architecture principles, assumptions, diagrams, and transition roadmap.
- Procurement-ready technical specifications.
- Technical support to tender preparation and consistency review of technical procurement documentation.

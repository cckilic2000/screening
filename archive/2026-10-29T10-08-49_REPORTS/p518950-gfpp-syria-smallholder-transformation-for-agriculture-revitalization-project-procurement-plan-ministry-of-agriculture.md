## Executive Summary

The World Bank has disclosed a procurement plan for Syria’s P518950 agriculture-revitalisation preparation project, implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture. Three planned international consulting-firm packages cover digital/technical preparation (US$600,000.00), ministry/laboratory needs assessment (US$245,000.00) and an environmental/social framework package (US$200,000.00). The document also lists five separate individual-consultant activities. It is a pipeline source with planned dates; it does not publish a current submission deadline or package-specific expert requirements.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The plan governs procurement through the World Bank STEP system and specifies applicable procurement arrangements and international methods. Its populated firm section comprises one QCBS package and two CQS packages, all open-international and pending implementation. The works, goods and non-consulting-services sections have no populated activities in this version.

The technical preparation package covers census documents, digital farmer/animal registries, an agricultural observatory/platform/applications, baseline/MIS work and third-party-monitoring technical specifications. Other firm packages address ministry-unit rehabilitation needs and agricultural laboratory quality infrastructure, and environmental/social preparation. Individual scopes cover value-chain and asset assessment, crisis preparedness, an operational manual and international financial/procurement specialists. These descriptions are forecast package scopes, not full contractual TOR or detailed accepted deliverable specifications.

## Key Deliverables

- SY-MOA-580007-CS-QCBS, consulting firm, estimated US$600,000.00: technical documents for census and digital farmer/animal registries; agricultural observatory centre/platform/applications; baseline survey/MIS; technical specifications for third-party monitoring. Planned EOI: 5 January 2027; contract completion: 11 March 2029.
- SY-MOA-580013-CS-CQS, consulting firm, US$245,000.00: rehabilitation-needs assessment for ministry PCU/Damascus/PIUs/regional directorates/extension units and feasibility/assessment for agricultural laboratory quality infrastructure. Planned EOI: 5 January 2027; completion: 24 March 2028.
- SY-MOA-580024-CS-CQS, consulting firm, US$200,000.00: Environmental and Social Framework preparation package. Planned EOI: 21 January 2027; completion: 10 April 2028.

- SY-MOA-580021-CS-INDV, US$60,000.00: Food Security Crisis Preparedness Plan for the CRW, including scenarios, protocols, triggers and procurement/financial arrangements; planned completion 8 December 2027.
- SY-MOA-580023-CS-INDV, US$60,000.00: STAR project operational manual covering governance, implementation, procurement, financial management, safeguards, monitoring, reporting and grievance procedures; planned completion 8 December 2027.

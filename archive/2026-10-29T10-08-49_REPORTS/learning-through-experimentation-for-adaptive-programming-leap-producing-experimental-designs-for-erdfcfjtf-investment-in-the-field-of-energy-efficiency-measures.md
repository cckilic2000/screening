## Executive Summary

The European Commission’s DG REGIO has issued an open services tender for energy-efficiency experimentation under the future National and Regional Partnership Plans. The EUR 340,000 excluding-VAT contract runs for up to nine months and requires three country designs, joint learning events and a practical LEAP Kit. Bidders need documented experimentation, public-administration capacity-building and multi-country experience, including an energy-efficiency reference. Tenders are due on 7 December 2026 through electronic submission.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The objective is to make experimentation usable from the outset of the 2028–2034 programming period. Three Member States will be selected by the Commission, with the contractor advising on the selection, establishing diagnostic evidence, and bringing programme planners and evaluators together to define robust intervention and comparison groups, outcomes and delivery arrangements. Actual implementation of the experiments and analysis of their eventual results are outside this contract.

The three country workstreams run in parallel. They feed a common toolkit covering design selection, outcome measurement, data requirements, ethics, NRPP clauses, adaptive learning, scaling and institutional readiness, supported by country case studies. Intended users include managing authorities, evaluators and programme planners; energy agencies, utilities, housing associations and territorial bodies may participate in country work.

## Key Deliverables

- D1a: inception report, methodology, timetable, country-selection review process and structures for diagnostics, experimental designs and LEAP Kit; due two weeks after signature.
- D1b: recommendation and written assessment for selecting three participating Member States; due two weeks after signature.
- D2: three country diagnostics addressing investments, legal/administrative conditions, data, comparison units and statistical feasibility; due month 3.
- D3: workshop design notes; due month 4 and distributed at least two weeks before the relevant workshops.
- D4: reports of the three in-country workshops, including agreed experimental-design outlines; within one week after each workshop.
- D5: three NRPP-ready experimental designs, covering hypotheses, theory of change, units, identification/randomisation, implementation and data arrangements, outcomes, ethics and regulatory integration; due month 6.
- D6: joint synthesis event report; month 7.
- D7: LEAP Kit with practical guidance and three country case studies; month 8.
- D8: draft final report with executive summary and annexed experimental designs and toolkit; month 8.
- D9: final report incorporating Commission comments, all presentation files, supporting calculations and documents; month 9. The abstract and publishable executive summary must be in English, German and French.
- Organise the month-2 Brussels launch seminar, three one-to-two-day country workshops by month 5, month-7 Brussels synthesis event, and month-9 online seminar; prepare meeting minutes and participate in regular progress meetings.

## Capacity Building Components

- Jointly develop programme planners’ and evaluators’ ability to design and use experiments, with separate country teams working in parallel.
- Facilitate a Brussels launch seminar on experimental methods, energy-efficiency evidence, NRPP integration and ethics.
- Run one in-person design workshop in each participating Member State and a cross-country synthesis event involving the Evaluation Network.
- Develop practical institutional-readiness, mixed-team working, adaptive-learning and replication guidance through the LEAP Kit.
- Deliver the final online dissemination seminar for managing authorities, programmers, evaluators and other stakeholders.

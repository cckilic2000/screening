## Executive Summary

The Palestinian Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital Economy has published TOR for a five-course capacity-building programme for the Telecommunications Regulatory Authority. The courses cover licensing/enforcement, market analysis/competition, service quality, consumer rights and emergency-sector resilience. A qualified consulting or specialised training firm must provide suitable trainers, plans, participant materials, certificates and evaluation reports, with physical delivery at ministry or firm laboratories. The linked TOR does not publish an assignment budget, duration, announcement date or submission deadline.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The Digital West Bank & Gaza Project supports the establishment and operationalisation of the TRA under its telecommunications legal mandate. This assignment aims to strengthen core regulatory knowledge, institutional processes and evidence-based decisions, using ITU guidance, Global Symposium for Regulators best practices and relevant regional/international experience. The wider project has an IDA grant of USD 20 million; that figure is not this assignment’s contract budget.

Five training tracks address licensing/enforcement, competition, QoS/QoE measurement, consumer protection and emergency/resilience planning. The TOR specifies group sizes and applied/blended delivery modalities, requiring training that translates regulatory concepts into cases, exercises and operating practice. The expected outputs include improved professional capability and documented learning outcomes; the document does not specify a total assignment schedule or course duration in hours.

## Key Deliverables

- Detailed plan and outline for each course, aligned with its objectives, scope, participant count and modality, specifying teaching hours, days and topics.
- Course 1: licensing, compliance and enforcement; 15 trainees; expert-led applied workshop and practical exercises.
- Course 2: telecom market analysis and competition regulation; seven trainees; blended training with regulatory case studies and applied market analysis.
- Course 3: QoS/QoE regulation and measurement; seven trainees; specialised training with remote follow-up, including indicators, drive testing, monitoring and compliance assessment.
- Course 4: protection of telecommunications consumers/users; five trainees; practical workshops on complaints, rights, disclosure, investigations and remedies.
- Course 5: emergency telecommunications planning and sector resilience; 15 trainees; multi-stakeholder workshop with practical emergency/cyber scenarios.
- Printed/electronic learning materials for every trainee, PMU and TRA.
- Course-completion certificates for successful participants.
- Evaluation report for every course describing outcomes and comparing pre- and post-training test results.
- Optional, separately itemised certification examinations, vouchers, registration or associated services where relevant; TRA can choose all, some or none.

## Capacity Building Components

- Design and deliver all five applied regulatory training tracks.
- Use case studies and exercises for licensing/enforcement and market analysis.
- Provide QoS/QoE specialised instruction with remote follow-up.
- Run practical consumer-protection workshops and emergency/cyber resilience scenarios.
- Evaluate learning with pre/post assessments and supply reusable materials to trainees, PMU and TRA.

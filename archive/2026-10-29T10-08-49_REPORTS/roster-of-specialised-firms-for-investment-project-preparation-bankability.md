## Executive Summary

UNDP Jordan is seeking expressions of interest from national and international firms and consortia for a seven-lot investment-preparation and sustainable-finance roster. The scope covers bankability, financial modelling, blended finance, sector investment pipelines and innovative financial instruments. Applicants can target one or more lots and must document relevant institutional experience, financing outcomes and appropriate senior expertise. The live notice gives a 22 November 2026 deadline; the published package contains inconsistent time/draft-date wording that requires attention before submission.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The roster is intended to turn Jordanian development and environmental priorities into technically feasible, appropriately financed investment opportunities. Sector lots address biodiversity, renewable energy/energy efficiency, blue economy, climate adaptation and coral reefs, supported by cross-cutting project-finance/transaction-advisory and sustainable-finance-instrument lots. The preparation cycle covers screening, feasibility, financial structuring, readiness and investor engagement.

Outputs and contractual conditions will be set in later assignment-specific terms of reference. The EOI describes a possible initial bankable or finance-ready project pipeline and distinguishes commercially viable, blended-finance and public-good/conservation investments. Roster inclusion does not guarantee an assignment, framework agreement or minimum workload; capacity development can accompany the preparation phases.

## Key Deliverables

- EOI-stage submission: principal response of no more than ten pages, excluding CVs and supporting annexes, identifying selected lots, lead/member entities, comparable assignments, financing outcomes, methodology, institutional capacity and references; no financial quotation.
- Indicative future phase 1: opportunity mapping, screening/prioritisation reports, project concepts and investment profiles.
- Indicative phase 2: technical, commercial, economic and financial feasibility studies, investment assessments and risk-mitigation plans.
- Indicative phase 3: editable financial models with assumptions and sensitivity analysis; financing plans, blended-finance structures and de-risking mechanisms.
- Indicative phase 4: bankability/finance-readiness assessments, investment memoranda, supporting due-diligence materials and advancement/approval roadmaps.
- Indicative phase 5: financing-partner mapping, investor engagement strategies, presentations, market-sounding reports, transaction packages and financing-mobilisation roadmaps.
- Assignment-specific capacity-building tools, training materials and knowledge-transfer outputs where commissioned. All future deliverables are subject to separate TOR and procurement.

## Capacity Building Components

- Build national capacity in investment preparation, sustainable finance and capital mobilisation.
- Include knowledge transfer and capacity development across preparation phases when required by the assignment.
- Produce training materials and capacity-building tools where commissioned.
- Provide transparent editable financial models and documentation so national counterparts can understand and use the analysis.

## Team Requirements

- National and international firms and consortia may apply for one or more lots; legal registration and capacity to deliver professional advisory services are required.
- The EOI specifies at least three years of relevant active institutional experience and at least three comparable assignments per selected lot completed in the preceding five years.
- Its financial requirements cite audited 2023–2025 statements, average annual turnover of USD 200,000, quick ratio above 1.0 in each year and company capital above USD 100,000. The document says mandatory versus scored requirements remain to be confirmed in final procurement documentation.
- Indicative senior profiles: investment/development-finance team leader; project-finance/investment-banking specialist; financial-modelling/bankability expert; green/climate-finance expert; relevant sector specialists; PPP/legal-regulatory expert; environmental/social safeguards expert; innovative-finance/financial-sector expert; economic/development-impact expert.
- Preferred experience is 15+ years for the team leader and 10+ years for the finance/banking specialist; these are explicitly not mandatory minimum thresholds. CFA, FRM, CPA, ACCA, PPP and sustainable-finance certifications are assets.
- English fluency is required; demonstrate access to Arabic-speaking expertise. Jordan/Arab-region experience is advantageous.
- Consortia identify a lead and partner responsibilities; the signed JV form/letter of intent or agreement provides joint and several liability. Individual consultants’ privately acquired experience cannot be claimed as the firm’s corporate references.
- Submit registration/tax evidence, CVs, audited financials, performance certificates from three clients, required declarations and the applicable checklist.

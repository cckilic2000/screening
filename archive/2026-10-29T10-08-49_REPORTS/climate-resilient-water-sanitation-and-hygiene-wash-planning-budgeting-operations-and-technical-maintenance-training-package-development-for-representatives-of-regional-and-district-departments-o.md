## Executive Summary

UNICEF Uzbekistan is procuring a training-package and delivery assignment for climate-resilient WASH planning, budgeting, operations and maintenance. The official scope combines practical learning materials and financial/maintenance tools with training for 48 officials and practitioners and 320 community participants. The assignment is stated as 50 working days, with the main schedule running from 16 November 2026 to 29 January 2027. Proposals must reach tashkentsupply@unicef.org by 18:00 Tashkent time on 10 November 2026, with technical and protected financial offers submitted separately.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The contractor will adapt existing UNICEF and government WASH resources into a practical three-day curriculum for education-sector and local-government stakeholders. Content covers service assessment, governance, prioritisation, budgeting and financing, operations, preventive/corrective maintenance, climate risks, accessibility, hygiene and emergency continuity. Consultation, piloting and revision are intended to make the materials suitable for national replication.

Delivery comprises a three-day course for 48 officials/practitioners and six one-day sessions for 320 community leaders and members, covering eight districts and 16 schools. The package includes facilitator and participant materials, practical automated tools, evaluation instruments and an editable knowledge base. The wider joint programme aims to strengthen climate-resilient WASH in 45 schools; the training assignment’s stated delivery group is the narrower set above.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception meeting, desk review, methodology, workplan, consultation approach and inception report: six working days; 15% payment.
- Draft training manual covering planning/budgeting and operations/maintenance, plus education and ToT materials: 20 working days; 25% payment.
- Final training manual and complete ToT/facilitation package, revised through UNICEF and stakeholder feedback: ten working days; 40% payment.
- Practical tools for WASH assessment, climate-risk screening, action planning, budgeting, life-cycle costing, maintenance schedules, inspection and maintenance logs, water-quality monitoring, continuity, dashboards and institutional responsibilities.
- Facilitator guide, participant handbook, presentations, session plans, case studies, exercises, role plays, quizzes, pre/post assessments and evaluation forms.
- Three-day course for 48 civil servants/practitioners and six one-day sessions for 320 community participants; training reports with agendas, participant lists, results, photographs, feedback and recommendations.
- Final report, dissemination presentation/knowledge product, final training database and all editable source materials: 14 working days for the final delivery/reporting phase; 20% payment.

## Capacity Building Components

## Executive Summary

UNODC Afghanistan is requesting information from non-profit implementing partners for vocational skills and economic-reintegration services in Badakhshan, Badghis and Takhar. The RFI targets 1,250 recovering individuals and family members through training, apprenticeships, start-up support and market linkages. Eligible organisations need at least five years of relevant programme experience, relevant provincial experience or presence, and a recent annual budget above USD 662,500. The proposed agreement is up to 18 months, with information due by 16:30 Kabul time on 18 November 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The JICA-funded “Strengthening Counter-narcotics Capacities for Vulnerable People” project uses vocational skills and livelihood opportunities to support recovery and reintegration. The intervention covers 500 participants in Badakhshan, 250 in Badghis and 500 in Takhar, with a stated split of 750 males and 500 females. The source includes recovering individuals as well as spouses and family members.

The partner will combine centre-based foundational instruction and practical apprenticeships, coordinate candidate selection with treatment centres, supply appropriate start-up packages, and connect graduates to employers and other livelihood services. Business mentoring covers planning, markets, financial management, pricing, records and customer development. The source sets an employment objective for at least 70% of trainees, while one final indicator gives a differing completion total noted below.

## Key Deliverables

- Implement market-oriented vocational training and apprenticeships for the stated 1,250-person target across three provinces.
- Select eligible participants with drug-treatment providers near completion of residential treatment.
- Provide successful graduates with tools/equipment and other appropriate start-up inputs.
- Create employment, internship, private-sector and income-generation linkages; the RFI targets job opportunities for at least 70% of trainees.
- Provide structured post-training follow-up and support for employment, apprenticeships and self-employment.
- Deliver entrepreneurship/business mentoring on business planning, market opportunities, financial management, pricing, record-keeping and customers.
- RFI submission: organisational/legal/financial information and supporting evidence, eligibility self-assessment referenced as Annex 2, and methodology/workplan referenced as Annex 3. Separate Annexes 2 and 3 are not embedded in the linked PDF.

## Capacity Building Components

- Foundational vocational instruction followed by practical apprenticeship activity.

## Team Requirements

- Applicants must be non-profit CSOs, NGOs or CBOs registered under Afghan law and/or holding a legal permit to operate in Afghanistan; they must directly manage the project.
- At least five years’ vocational-training programme experience, experience with major multilateral/bilateral donors, and presence or past relevant work in at least one target province.
- Experience with people who use drugs and other hard-to-reach/marginalised groups is preferred.
- Annual income/budget for the most recently completed 2024 or 2025 fiscal year must exceed USD 662,500; an account able to receive international deposits is required. This is an applicant threshold, not a tender budget.
- Provide organisational structure, governance information, prior projects, financial statements/audit evidence and CVs for project/programme personnel, a VST coordinator and an M&E officer.

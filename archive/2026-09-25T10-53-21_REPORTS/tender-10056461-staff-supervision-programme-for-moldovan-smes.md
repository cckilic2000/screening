## Executive Summary

GIZ has launched tender 10056461 for a practical staff-supervision programme for Moldovan SMEs under the Youth Employment Promotion project. The content covers onboarding, supervision, delegation, work organization, performance, motivation and retention. Technical proposals are weighted 70% and financial proposals 30%, with pricing in MDL at 0% VAT. The 5 October deadline is short, and eligibility is restricted to Moldova-resident firms established under Moldovan law.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The contractor will design and deliver a practical training programme for SME managers and supervisors. The stated management areas are onboarding, day-to-day supervision, delegation, work organization, performance, motivation and employee retention.

Applicants must submit a technical bid of no more than seven pages excluding CVs and certificates, covering company profile, concept and personnel. Local registration, proof of no tax debt and a self-declaration are required; the full TOR is supplied only by email.

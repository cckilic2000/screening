## Executive Summary

GIZ is procuring a six-month market-systems mapping and value-chain analysis for Lebanon's Strengthening Local Economy project. The assignment covers four Lebanese regions and combines value-chain selection, competitiveness and gap analysis, institutional-capacity review and stakeholder consultation. A multidisciplinary team is expected to conduct 13 workshops or focus groups and translate the evidence into recommendations and a roadmap. The submission deadline is 13 October 2026 before 4:00 PM.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultant will select, preferably, four agricultural value chains and map their market systems across Akkar, North Lebanon, Mount Lebanon and Bekaa. The assessment is to examine competitiveness, opportunities, constraints, support functions, business-development and extension services, stakeholder roles and institutional capacity.

The work includes stakeholder consultations through 13 workshops or focus-group discussions. The intended result is a practical set of recommendations and a roadmap informed by regional evidence and value-chain gaps.

## Key Deliverables

- Market-systems mapping for the selected value chains
- Competitiveness, opportunity and gap analysis
- Assessment of constraints, support functions and institutional capacity
- Thirteen stakeholder workshops or focus-group discussions
- Recommendations and implementation roadmap

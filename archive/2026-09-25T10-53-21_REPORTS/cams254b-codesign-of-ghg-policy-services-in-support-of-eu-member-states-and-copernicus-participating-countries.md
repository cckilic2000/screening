## Executive Summary

ECMWF's CAMS2_54b tender seeks a specialist contractor to co-design greenhouse-gas policy services with national inventory agencies in EU and Copernicus participating countries. The contractor will bridge operational CAMS and satellite-observation products with national UNFCCC reporting needs, organize cross-country engagement, analyse user requirements and recommend policy-facing visualizations. The contract is expected to run from March 2027 through December 2028. Tenders close on 5 November 2026 at 14:00 UK time / 15:00 CET.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The successful tenderer must first develop a strong technical understanding of current and emerging CAMS emissions-monitoring services and satellite Earth-observation methods. It will then establish mechanisms for interacting with national inventory agencies, explain products, document existing workflows and gaps, and facilitate learning across more and less advanced national systems.

The user-engagement results must become a requirements document, fitness-for-purpose and gap analysis, and recommendations for national policy tools and interactive visualizations, including communication of uncertainty. Contract management includes quarterly reviews, annual planning and reporting, KPIs and risk management.

## Key Deliverables

- User-friendly documentation of relevant CAMS emissions-monitoring services in presentation and text formats
- Hybrid meetings with national inventory agencies across Europe
- User-requirements document
- Fitness-for-purpose assessment and gap analysis
- Recommendations for national policy-sector data presentation and visualisation
- Quarterly and annual implementation reports and plans
- Final implementation report
- KPI set, risk register and meeting records

## Capacity Building Components

- Cross-country collaboration and user engagement with national inventory agencies
- Explanation of CAMS products to prospective national users
- Training and support are expressly outside the core scope, although ad hoc technical/scientific support may be requested

## Team Requirements

The team must include a Prime Investigator with more than five years of experience managing activities related to the ITT, at least one additional senior member with more than five years of relevant experience, and a Service Manager. The two senior members must each contribute at least 10% of working time. The contractor and subcontractors must demonstrate relevant national or international project experience; all tender and delivery documents are in English.

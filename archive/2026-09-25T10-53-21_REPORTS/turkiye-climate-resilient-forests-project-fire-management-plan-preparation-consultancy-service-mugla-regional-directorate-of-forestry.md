## Executive Summary

Türkiye's General Directorate of Forestry is procuring a consulting firm to prepare 12 fire-management plans for the Muğla Regional Directorate under the IBRD-financed Climate Resilient Forests Project. The assignment combines GIS-based risk analysis, prevention, preparedness, response-capacity assessment and recovery planning. It runs for 75 calendar days and requires 2 highly experienced forest engineers. The source document does not state the notice publication date, submission deadline or assignment budget.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultant will prepare separate fire-management plans for Aydın, Dalaman, Fethiye, Kavaklıdere, Köyceğiz, Marmaris, Milas, Muğla, Nazilli, Seydikemer, Yatağan, Yılanlı. Each plan must assess forest conditions, fire sensitivity, hazards, preventive measures, intervention resources, personnel and equipment capacity, operational responsibilities, priorities and recovery considerations. Maps must be compatible with the authority's GIS database.

The project applies an integrated-fire-management approach spanning review and analysis, preparedness, risk reduction, response and recovery. The plans are intended to strengthen operational and institutional readiness and include arrangements for education, awareness, coordination and performance review.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report and work plan by day 10
- Progress report with draft plans for 12 directorates by day 30
- Final 12 fire-management plans by day 75
- GIS-compatible maps, tables and plan data
- For each final Turkish plan: five bound printed copies and two electronic copies

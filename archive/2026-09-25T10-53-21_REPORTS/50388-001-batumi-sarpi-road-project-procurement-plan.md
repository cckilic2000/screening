## Executive Summary

The 21 September 2026 ADB procurement plan covers the USD 510 million Batumi–Sarpi Road Project in Georgia. Its main packages are USD 336 million in road construction and USD 10 million in equipment, supported by construction supervision, road-asset-management and road-safety consultancies. The plan has an 18-month horizon and does not provide one common deadline or bidder profile. It is excluded because works and equipment are the primary component.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The project will deliver the Batumi–Sarpi road through a major civil-works package, supported by equipment acquisition and external construction supervision. The procurement plan also includes advisory services for strengthening road-asset-management systems, safety planning and awareness, plus individual experts.

Because each package has its own selection approach and timing, the plan cannot be treated as a single consultancy assignment. Package-level TORs and expected impacts COULDN'T BE FOUND in this source.

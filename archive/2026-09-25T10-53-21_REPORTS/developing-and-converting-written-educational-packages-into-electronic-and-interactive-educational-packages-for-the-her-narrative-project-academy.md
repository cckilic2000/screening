## Executive Summary

The Her Narrative project is seeking a specialized company to convert 34 hours of written training material into interactive e-learning packages. The scope includes instructional design, storyboards, a prototype, responsive HTML5 content, video, animation, graphics, assessments, source files, SCORM-ready documentation and knowledge transfer. Delivery is required within 40 calendar days, followed by a 12-month defect warranty. Sealed technical and financial offers are due on 4 October 2026 before 1:00 PM in Al-Bireh or Gaza.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The provider will analyse the approved content, structure it into courses and Modules/SCOs, develop storyboards and a prototype, then produce interactive learning units, quizzes, video, motion graphics, audio and visual assets. Outputs must be usable across modern browsers and devices and must support later SCORM packaging and publication by the client's technical team.

The academy content covers four components totaling 34 hours and serves female graduates in media, design, multimedia and creative/cultural industries. The firm must complete QA, transfer all editable source files and licensed assets, document export and completion settings, train the client's team and provide a 12-month warranty.

## Key Deliverables

- Implementation plan, course map and Module/SCO map
- Content analysis and interaction design
- Storyboards / Interactive Design Documents and visual-learning style guide
- Prototype for approval
- Interactive e-learning units for all four components
- Quizzes, assessments, activities, videos, animation, infographics, audio, captions and transcripts as applicable
- Editable authoring, design, video, graphic and audio source files and licence register
- Responsive HTML5/Web test-and-acceptance version
- SCORM export/package settings, editing/republication guide and unit index
- Knowledge-transfer session
- Twelve-month warranty and defect correction

## Capacity Building Components

- Conversion of 34 training hours into self-paced digital learning
- Interactive learning and assessment design
- Knowledge-transfer/training session for the client team
- Editable-source handover so the client can update and republish content
- Documentation of SCORM export, completion and assessment settings

## Team Requirements

A multidisciplinary team is required. Proposed roles are Team Leader, Instructional Designer, e-Learning Developer, Graphic/UI Designer, Motion Graphics/Animation Specialist and Video/Audio Specialist; one qualified person may cover multiple roles if responsibilities remain clear. The firm must demonstrate e-learning, written-to-interactive conversion, educational multimedia, SCORM/authoring-tool and source-file handover experience and submit team CVs and similar-project evidence.

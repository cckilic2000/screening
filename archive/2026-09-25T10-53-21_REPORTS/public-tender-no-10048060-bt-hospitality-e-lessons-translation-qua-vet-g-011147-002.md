## Executive Summary

GIZ is seeking a digital-learning provider for multilingual hospitality content under the EU/BMZ co-financed QuA-VET project in Lebanon. The assignment may cover up to 360 lessons in English and 360 in French, rebuilt in Articulate Rise 360 with quizzes, multimedia and quality assurance. The provider must upload the content to the LMS and hand over editable Rise 360 files and SCORM packages. Bids are due by 11 October 2026 at 23:59 Beirut time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The provider will translate Arabic vocational hospitality lessons into English and French and recreate up to 12 courses per language. The source sequence, design and pedagogic structure must be maintained while quizzes and multimedia are translated or adapted.

The scope includes quality assurance, LMS upload and a complete source-file handover through GIZ File Transfer or hard drive. These requirements are intended to leave the partner institutions with reusable and publishable multilingual e-learning assets.

## Key Deliverables

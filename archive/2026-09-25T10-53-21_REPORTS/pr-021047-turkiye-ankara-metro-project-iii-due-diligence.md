## Executive Summary

EBRD is procuring an 18-week, EUR 300,000 due-diligence assignment for Ankara Metro Project III. The proposed 9.36-kilometre line with five stations will be assessed across technical, financial, economic, procurement, environmental, social, climate and gender dimensions. The official summary calls for an extensive senior multidisciplinary team, including Turkish legal and local-project expertise. Proposals are due on 16 October 2026 at 12:00.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultant will support EBRD's assessment and structuring of the Ankara metro investment. The review spans engineering and rail systems, traffic and economics, financial viability, procurement strategy, energy, climate, environmental and social safeguards, resettlement, health and safety, gender, labour, heritage, sustainable mobility and land-value/urban-regeneration considerations.

The study is expected to validate project information, identify gaps and risks, and provide findings and recommendations for decision-making. The public Event Summary points registered suppliers to the procurement dossier for the complete deliverable and reporting schedule.

## Key Deliverables

- Technical and rail-infrastructure due diligence
- Financial and economic analysis
- Procurement due diligence and recommendations
- Environmental, social, resettlement, health-and-safety and labour review
- Climate mitigation/adaptation and energy analysis
- Gender assessment
- Urban regeneration, land-value-capture and sustainable-mobility analysis

## Team Requirements

The official summary identifies a Project Manager, Local Project Manager, Procurement Specialist, Rail Infrastructure Specialist, Energy Specialist, Rail Systems Specialist, Transport Economist/Traffic Modeler, Urban Regeneration/Land Value Capture Specialist, Climate Specialist, Financial Expert, senior social/resettlement and health-and-safety experts, Gender Specialist, labour/supply-chain expert, Senior Environmental Specialist, Cultural Heritage Expert and Urban Transport/Sustainable Mobility Expert.

## Executive Summary

ADB and TBC Bank are recruiting an international consulting firm for an 18-month, USD 120,000 assignment to strengthen the green and climate impact of TBC's lending. The work covers eight climate-risk guidelines, a Climate Risk Appetite Statement, practical credit-risk integration and capacity building for staff. A second task develops ESG Academy curricula and delivers ToT and pilots for at least 60 staff, 100 clients and 20 trainers. EOIs are due on 24 October 2026 at 23:59 Manila time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

Task 1 requires a diagnostic of TBC's credit and ESG processes, a general assessment framework, a portfolio-level guideline and six sector-specific climate-risk guidelines. The consultant will develop a Climate Risk Appetite Statement with indicators, limits, thresholds and monitoring methods, then train at least 100 staff through 18 sessions.

Task 2 develops bilingual ESG Academy curricula for bank staff and clients across agriculture, waste, manufacturing, tourism/HORECA, logistics and sustainable technologies. It includes at least 15 pilot sessions for 60 staff, six client sessions for at least 100 clients, and a 10-session ToT for at least 20 internal trainers, with feedback incorporated into final products.

## Key Deliverables

- Diagnostic and gap analysis of existing credit and ESG practices
- Eight climate-risk assessment guidelines: one general, one portfolio-level and six sector-specific
- Climate Risk Appetite Statement and supporting indicators, limits, thresholds and monitoring framework
- Training materials and 18 sessions for at least 100 TBC staff
- ESG Academy curricula and materials for staff and clients in English and Georgian
- Pilot delivery to at least 60 staff and 100 clients with satisfaction surveys
- Ten-session Training of Trainers for at least 20 TBC trainers
- Finalized training products and documentation incorporating pilot feedback

## Capacity Building Components

- Hands-on learning and capacity building for at least 100 credit and risk staff
- English- and Georgian-language curricula for staff and clients
- Training of Trainers for at least 20 internal trainers
- Pilot sessions for at least 60 staff and 100 clients
- Case-based and learning-by-doing methods
- In-person Georgian-language delivery with hybrid adaptability

## Team Requirements

The firm must have 7–10 years of relevant experience in ESG, green/climate finance, climate risk, banking advisory or capacity building and experience with banks or development/financial institutions. Key experts need at least five years in climate, ESG and sustainable finance; local expert engagement is mandatory. Deliverables must be bilingual, and all in-person training must be conducted in Georgian. Firms must be incorporated in an ADB member country, and personnel must be citizens of ADB member countries and satisfy ADB sanctions rules.

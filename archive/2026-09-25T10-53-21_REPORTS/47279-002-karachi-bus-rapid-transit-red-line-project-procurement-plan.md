## Executive Summary

ADB's 24 September 2026 procurement plan covers the Karachi Bus Rapid Transit Red Line Project. The USD 517.88 million project includes large civil-works, station, systems, equipment and biogas-plant packages, alongside construction-supervision, project-management and emissions-monitoring consultancies. The plan does not constitute a single open consultancy with a common deadline or qualification set. It is excluded because works and supplies are the primary component.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The plan organizes procurement for a major BRT investment in Karachi. High-value packages cover the corridor and buildings, 16 stations, structural works, systems and a biogas plant.

The advisory elements include project management and construction supervision, a firm for post-project emissions monitoring and individual consultants. Expected impacts and package-level deliverables are not consolidated into a single assignment in the plan.

## Executive Summary

GIZ, acting for the German Federal Foreign Office, is procuring training and institutional-strengthening support for Palestinian Civil Defense. The service covers operational planning, needs assessments and surveys, plus stronger management of the PCD Training Department's training cycle and enrolment. The work is centered in the West Bank and supports readiness for wider operational needs, including possible future Gaza deployment. Quotations are due by 6 October 2026 at 23:59.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment will train and coach Civil Defense officers on operational planning and on the design and conduct of needs assessments and surveys. It will also build the Training Department's institutional capability to manage the full training cycle and participant enrolment.

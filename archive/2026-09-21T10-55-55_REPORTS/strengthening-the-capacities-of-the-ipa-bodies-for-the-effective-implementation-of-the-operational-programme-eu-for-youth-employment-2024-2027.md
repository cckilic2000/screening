## Executive Summary

The Government of Albania's CFCU is procuring 36 months of IPA III technical assistance for implementation of the EU for Youth Employment 2024–2027 Operational Programme. The estimated value is 3 540 000,00 EUR excluding VAT, and requests to participate close 22 October 2026. The public documents require at least EUR 1.8 million average annual turnover, six relevant personnel and two comparable contracts. The procedure is restricted and the detailed service TOR is not in the public first-stage document ZIP.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The call covers assistance to the Managing Authority, intermediate bodies for policy and financial management, partners, end recipients and other stakeholders. Work includes institutional and administrative strengthening, capacity-building, preparation of project and procurement documents, monitoring/evaluation/reporting, procurement and contract-management support, and communication/visibility activities. The first-stage Funding & Tenders package contains the contract notice, additional information and participation form. It defines eligibility under IPA III, shortlisting conditions and the 36-month period but does not publish detailed key-expert terms of reference.

## Key Deliverables

- Project and procurement documentation, including technical documentation.
- Programme monitoring, evaluation and reporting support.
- Procurement and contract-management assistance and communication/visibility activities.
- Specific output schedule COULDN'T BE FOUND.

## Capacity Building Components

- Institutional, administrative and operational capacity building for programme bodies and stakeholders.
- Support to policy and financial-management intermediate bodies and end recipients.

## Team Requirements

Eligible legal persons established in an EU member state or IPA III eligible country/territory, and international/regional organizations; consortium bids permitted. The candidate or consortium must have at least EUR 1,800,000 average annual turnover over the last three closed years, six relevant personnel on average, and two comparable service contracts completed in the last four years at no less than EUR 1,200,000 each. Named key-expert profiles are COULDN'T BE FOUND in the first-stage package.

## Strategic Notes For FS

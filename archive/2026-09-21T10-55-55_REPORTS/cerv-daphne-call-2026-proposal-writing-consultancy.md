## Executive Summary

CARE Deutschland invites a proposal-writing consultant for its 2026 CERV DAPHNE application concerning support to women and girls affected by gender-based violence in Germany and Ukraine. The consultancy is approximately 10 working days, with applications due 30 September 2026. A complete draft is due to CARE on 26 October, followed by a short post-submission report. The source does not state a consultancy budget.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

CARE plans to support intermediary organizations and civil-society groups addressing gender-based violence, including women-led refugee organizations in Germany and groups in underfunded Ukrainian areas. The consultant's own assignment is to review CERV rules, write and revise the funding narrative, integrate design-team and specialist inputs, check coherence with the budget/work plan, and document lessons after submission. CARE provides project design, content and budget inputs and submits the application itself. The TOR asks applicants for evidence of past EU proposals, writing samples, CV, work plan and a transparent fee offer.

## Key Deliverables

- Donor-compliant CERV proposal narrative by 26 October 2026.
- Review and incorporation of CARE team and specialist feedback.
- Brief post-submission report on donor expectations, proposal strengths/weaknesses and future recommendations.

## Capacity Building Components

- The proposed CARE project includes capacity building for subgrantees; training delivery is not part of this writer's stated assignment.

## Team Requirements

Individual consultant with university degree and relevant EU proposal-design experience, at least six years' project-development experience, demonstrated EU/CERV proposal writing, and very good German and English. Knowledge of refugee women's issues in Germany, Ukraine's war context and gender-based violence is requested. No consortium or firm team is specified.

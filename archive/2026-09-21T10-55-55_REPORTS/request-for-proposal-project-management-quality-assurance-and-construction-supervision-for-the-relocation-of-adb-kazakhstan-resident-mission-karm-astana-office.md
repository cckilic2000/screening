## Executive Summary

ADB's Kazakhstan Resident Mission invites proposals for project management, quality assurance and construction supervision of its Astana office relocation. The public RFP requires three years of Kazakhstan legal existence or an eligible local JV, plus relevant project or construction management experience. A mandatory meeting and site inspection take place on 28 September, and bids are due 6 October 2026. The budget and contract duration are COULDN'T BE FOUND in the public PDF.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The one-page RFP describes a comprehensive service for the office relocation, including supervision, skilled personnel, tools, testing instruments, transport and incidental works or materials. It requires bids in Kazakhstani tenge through ADB's electronic procurement system. Firms must meet nationality and sanctions conditions, prove relevant legal presence and experience, and show positive net worth in 2024 and 2025 or a bank-certified line of credit of at least US$50,000 equivalent. The full Appendix 1 TOR is available only after Coupa registration and participation request.

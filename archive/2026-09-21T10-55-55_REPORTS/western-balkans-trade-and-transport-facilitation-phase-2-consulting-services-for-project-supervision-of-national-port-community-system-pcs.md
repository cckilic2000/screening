## Executive Summary

Montenegro's Ministry of Transport has issued a World Bank-financed RFP for supervision of the National Port Community System. The consultant will advise on supplier selection and oversee design, integration, testing, commissioning, handover and warranty for the Port of Bar-focused first phase. The expected service period is approximately 640 days, with proposals due 30 October 2026. The detailed TOR is provided by email on request.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The Ministry of Transport is the client and the Ministry of Maritime Affairs is the beneficiary. The consultant will support procurement and contracting of the PCS supplier, then supervise implementation against technical specifications, schedule, World Bank documents and good international practice. The intended result is a secure, documented and sustainable system accepted by the beneficiary. The notice states a USD 15 million World Bank loan for the wider project, but does not identify an assignment budget; the detailed RFP and TOR are available by email request.

## Key Deliverables

- Advisory support during selection and contracting of the PCS supplier.
- Supervision of PCS design, supply, installation, configuration, integration, testing, commissioning, operational acceptance, handover, warranty and technical support.

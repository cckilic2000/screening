## Executive Summary

UNDP Ukraine invites proposals under UNDP-UKR-01829 for a 65-week assignment in five Mykolaiv Oblast communities. The contractor will establish public-health working groups, strengthen evidence-based local planning, train stakeholders and NGOs, and conduct cross-community knowledge exchange. Proposals are due 9 October 2026 through Quantum. The published solicitation does not state a contract budget.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The contractor will identify five target municipalities, document their selection and help local authorities formalize multidisciplinary public-health working groups. It will review community strategies, gather and visualize 2025–2026 public-health data, run a representative survey of 350 people, define indicators and support public-awareness activities. Working-group members and local NGOs will receive training; the assignment closes with a cross-community exchange and a report of lessons and recommendations. UNDP links payment to three accepted deliverable groups at weeks 20, 52 and 65.

## Key Deliverables

- Five selected communities and formally established working groups with governance documents and action plans (within 20 weeks).
- Community strategy recommendations, study visit, two capacity-building activities, survey of 350 people, indicators, monitoring mechanisms, five public-awareness events and five NGO grant-proposal trainings (within 52 weeks).
- Cross-community exchange event and knowledge-exchange report (within 65 weeks).

## Capacity Building Components

- Two activities for working-group members on public-health planning, participatory decisions, data interpretation and coordination.
- One grant-proposal training for NGOs in each target community.
- Cross-community study visit and final knowledge-exchange event.
- Technical assistance to municipal working groups and transfer of monitoring and data-use methods.

## Team Requirements

Registered legal entity, commercial or nonprofit, for at least three years, with two comparable community public-health projects. Minimum experts: project manager/team lead (five years of project management), strategy-development expert, legal expert and at least two health/public-health specialists; the listed experts require fluent Ukrainian. Ukrainian bidders must be registered in government-controlled areas; a JV/consortium may bid with documented roles.

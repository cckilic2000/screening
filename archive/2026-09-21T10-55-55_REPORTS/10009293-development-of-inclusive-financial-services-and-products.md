## Executive Summary

GIZ tender 10009293 seeks advisory services to develop inclusive financial products for MSMEs and startups in Iraq under ProDIGI. The contractor will work with financial service providers on diagnostics, product design, piloting, rollout and staff training, with at least three products targeted. Bids are due 20 October 2026; the notice states a contract period from 26 January 2027 to 31 December 2028. The procurement documents publish fixed cost items but no total contract ceiling.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The contractor will select and assess candidate financial service providers, agree cooperation arrangements and identify barriers affecting MSMEs, especially women-led and green/digital businesses. It will support product design, digitization and inclusive access requirements, develop three prototypes within five to seven months, roll out three products by month eight, and update manuals or product sheets by month ten. The assignment also includes provider training and coaching, monitoring, case studies or communications outputs, and coordination with GIZ and Iraqi financial-sector stakeholders. The TOR names separate expert roles for banking, digital financial services, MSME finance and national support.

## Key Deliverables

- Assessments of selected financial service providers and cooperation agreements.
- Product-design training materials, at least three prototypes, market rollout of at least three products, and updated manuals/product sheets.
- Monitoring system, inception/interim/final reports, and at least three public communications or case-study outputs.

## Capacity Building Components

- Train and coach financial service providers in inclusive product design, digitization, MSME lending and green lending.
- Develop product-design training materials and train front-line staff such as loan officers and branch managers.
- Provide occasional training inputs for sector stakeholders, including regulators and associations.

## Team Requirements

Four key experts: team leader, digital-financial-services expert, MSME-finance expert and technical-banking expert, plus international/regional and national short-term expert pools. The eligibility grid requires qualifying financial-sector reference projects and average annual turnover of at least EUR 1,000,000. Arabic and English proficiency and MENA/Iraq experience are specified across expert roles; a consortium form is included in the documents.

## Strategic Notes For FS

- Direct alignment with MSME finance and financial inclusion.
- Product design includes women-led and green/digital businesses.
- Training and institutional capacity of financial service providers are explicit outputs.

## Risks / Concerns

- No published total contract ceiling; the price schedule contains fixed unalterable budget lines totaling EUR 275,000 but leaves expert remuneration open.
- Arabic/English and regional experience requirements are material.
- Security, travel and local delivery are included in the TOR.

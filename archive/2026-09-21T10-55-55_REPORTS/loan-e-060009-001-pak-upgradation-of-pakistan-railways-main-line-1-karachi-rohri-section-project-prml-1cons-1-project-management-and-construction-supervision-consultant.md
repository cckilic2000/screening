## Executive Summary

Pakistan Railways is recruiting a Project Management and Construction Supervision Consultant for the 480 km Karachi–Rohri Main Line-1 upgrade. The ADB CSRN lists a USD 60,115,232 consulting-services budget, 72-month engagement and 21 October 2026 EOI deadline. The TOR includes FIDIC contract administration, safeguards, testing and commissioning, and a formal capacity-development programme for railway staff. EOIs are submitted through ADB CMS.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultant will act as the Engineer for civil works and railway systems contracts, helping the project unit manage schedules, payments, variations, claims, design interfaces, quality, environmental/social safeguards and operational coordination. The TOR divides the approximate 72 months into inception, construction supervision and defects-notification phases. It requires numerous plans and reports, including inception, mobilization, construction management, monthly and quarterly progress, safeguards and completion reports. Capacity-building obligations cover project management, procurement, railway design/operations, FIDIC administration, PMIS, safeguards, training monitoring and paid internships.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception, mobilization, construction-management and interface-management plans.
- Monthly and quarterly progress reports, safeguards monitoring and compliance reports, testing/commissioning plans and records.
- Works and project-completion reports, defects-notification reports and as-built drawings.
- Capacity Development and Training Plan, Training Execution and Evaluation Reports, and internship/graduate-development programme.

## Capacity Building Components

- Train at least ten Pakistan Railways/PIU professionals per category in each of eight thematic areas, for at least 15 person-days per category.
- At least five hybrid programmes combining national and international exposure.
- Environmental/social capacity plan, project-wide training compliance matrix and contractor training oversight.
- Paid internship/graduate programme of at least 36 person-months with mentoring and job shadowing.

## Team Requirements

International consulting firm or association with national firms. The TOR specifies a large multidisciplinary international and national team across project leadership, rail engineering, contracts, planning, safeguards, quality, signaling, telecommunications, occupational safety and social/gender disciplines. Key international leadership positions specify up to 20 years' relevant experience; EOI assessment emphasizes major rail or comparable transport projects, FIDIC and MDB experience. JV partners and subconsultants must register in ADB CMS.

## Strategic Notes For FS

- Large, explicit institutional capacity-development and knowledge-transfer component.
- Procurement, contract administration, safeguards and project management are major advisory streams.
- The primary assignment remains railway construction supervision.

## Risks / Concerns

- Large specialist rail and FIDIC team required.
- A main project office in Karachi and corridor field presence are required.
- The source assignment has a 72-month commitment and extensive safeguard obligations.

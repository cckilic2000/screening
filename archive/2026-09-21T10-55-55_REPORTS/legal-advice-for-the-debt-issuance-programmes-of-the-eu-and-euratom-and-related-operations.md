## Executive Summary

The European Commission's DG BUDG is procuring legal advice for EU and EURATOM debt issuance under an open procedure. The estimated value is 350 000,00 EUR excluding VAT for a framework of up to 48 months. The tender requires a specialized Luxembourg/Belgian debt-capital-markets team and closes on 19 October 2026. The work includes legal opinions and transaction documentation.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The framework supports borrowing, lending, debt management and asset management, with particular focus on the EU/EURATOM debt issuance programmes. Tasks include advice on relevant legislation, drafting or reviewing offering circulars and service contracts, and handling transaction and contractual questions. Deliverables may be opinions, reports, notes, emails and editable debt-programme documents. The Commission will place specific contracts under the framework; the framework itself does not guarantee orders.

## Key Deliverables

- Legal opinions on relevant frameworks, risks and mitigation measures when requested.
- Debt issuance programme documentation, offering circulars/prospectuses and service contracts in editable form.
- Ad-hoc written legal advice, transaction documents, reports and notes.

## Capacity Building Components

- COULDN'T BE FOUND

## Team Requirements

A legal team covering Luxembourg and Belgian debt-capital-markets law: one dual-qualified equity partner or two equity partners with at least 10 years' continuous experience, one Luxembourg-law senior lawyer with seven years, and one junior lawyer with two years. Relevant bar registration in Luxembourg and in France, Belgium or Germany is required. Joint tenders and subcontracting are permitted subject to the tender rules.

## Strategic Notes For FS

- The mandate is financial-sector legal advisory work.
- The specified legal team and bar registrations are central qualification conditions.

## Risks / Concerns

- Highly specialized Luxembourg and Belgian legal qualifications are mandatory.
- Minimum average yearly turnover is EUR 125,000 and professional indemnity cover EUR 2,000,000.
- The framework does not guarantee specific orders.

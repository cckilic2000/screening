## Executive Summary

Albania's Ministry of Environment invites expressions of interest for World Bank-financed technical assistance on extended producer responsibility. The up-to-12-month assignment covers packaging EPR system design, circular-economy applications and practical knowledge transfer. It requires a team leader, circular-economy expert and financial/economic expert, with EOIs due 5 October 2026. The ministry's public TOR does not state a contract budget.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

Module I addresses household-packaging EPR/PRO governance, stakeholders, collection/sorting/recycling arrangements, reporting, recyclability, financial flows and fee-setting. Module II addresses material flows, circular design, resource efficiency and deposit-return systems, with tourism or zero-waste applications if included in the agreed scope. The consultant will work with the ministry and relevant municipalities, producers, retailers and operators through workshops, practical exercises and case discussions. Formal outputs are an inception note and a capacity-building package with training materials, workshop agendas, facilitation plans, summary notes and recommendations.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception Note with methodology, work plan, stakeholder approach and workshop structure.
- Structured training materials for EPR and circular-economy modules, including presentations, case studies and tools.
- Workshops for both modules with agendas, exercises, summary notes and practical recommendations.

## Capacity Building Components

- Practice-oriented workshops and co-creation with ministry and market stakeholders.
- Training on EPR/PRO design, financial flows, fee setting and operational processes.
- Circular-economy learning on resource efficiency, design for recycling and deposit-return systems.

## Team Requirements

Legally registered firm, JV or consortium with at least five years' relevant corporate/professional experience and practical packaging EPR/PRO experience. Three key experts: EPR and packaging-waste team leader; circular-economy expert; financial/economic expert, each with at least five years' relevant professional experience. English fluency is required for all three; key experts are not evaluated at EOI shortlisting stage.

## Strategic Notes For FS

- Strong institutional and operational capacity-building scope.
- The TOR includes financial flows and EPR fee-setting exercises.
- A consortium may combine operational EPR, circular-economy and financial expertise.

## Risks / Concerns

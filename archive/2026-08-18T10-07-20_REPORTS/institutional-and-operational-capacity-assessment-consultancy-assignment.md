## Detailed Assignment Summary

The tender is issued by the Palestinian Non-Governmental Organizations Network, a coordination framework for the NGO sector in Palestine. PNGO is implementing RAWAFED, a European Union-funded project that seeks to reinforce the role of Palestinian civil society as a central actor in governance, accountability and democratic participation.

The background describes severe constraints affecting Palestinian civil society, including shrinking civic space, civil society infrastructure damaged by war in Gaza, restrictions across Jerusalem and the West Bank, coordination gaps and limited representation in policy and recovery discussions. The assignment title and linked ToR indicate an institutional and operational capacity assessment consultancy for this civil society context.

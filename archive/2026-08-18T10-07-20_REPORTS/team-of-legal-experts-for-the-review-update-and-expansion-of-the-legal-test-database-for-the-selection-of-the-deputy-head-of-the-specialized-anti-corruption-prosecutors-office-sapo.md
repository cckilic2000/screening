## Executive Summary

EUACI has published an ongoing tender for a team of legal experts to review, update and expand the legal test database used in selecting the Deputy Head of SAPO. The service provider must organise 5–10 qualified legal experts to review 4,154 existing questions, develop 200 new questions, conduct peer review and prepare a consolidated final database. The maximum available budget is EUR 15,000, and the assignment must be completed by 1 October 2026. The proposal deadline shown on the page is 24.08.2026 | 17:00.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment supports the Specialized Anti-Corruption Prosecutor’s Office and the Selection Commission established to conduct the competition for Deputy Head of SAPO. The service provider will ensure that the test database is legally accurate, current, unambiguous, methodologically sound and suitable for use in the competition.

The scope includes reviewing all existing questions, revising defective questions, identifying outdated or duplicated items, developing 200 new questions, peer reviewing revised and new questions, resolving discrepancies and preparing a consolidated quality-assured database in Excel format. Confidentiality and secure handling of the database are central requirements.

## Key Deliverables

- Complete review matrix for all 4,154 questions.
- Revised database of existing questions.
- Database of 200 new questions.
- Consolidated final test database.
- Final report summarising work completed, key legal and methodological issues and statistics by thematic block.

## Team Requirements

The service provider may be a legal entity, consortium or organised group of experts represented by a lead contractor that assumes full contractual responsibility. The team must include 5–10 experts, including a Team Lead. Each expert must have at least a master’s and PhD degree in law, at least five years of relevant professional legal experience and excellent Ukrainian; the Team Lead must have at least seven years of relevant professional experience, including coordination of legal experts, legal analysis or drafting. Expertise must cover criminal law, criminal procedure, anti-corruption and prosecution legislation, constitutional law, ECHR and ECtHR case law.

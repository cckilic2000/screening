## Executive Summary

The ILO procurement page on UNGM announces a request for quotation for a Social Security Law Courts Work Stream in Federal Iraq. The assignment will strengthen an existing analytical draft report on social security court cases, extend it to complaints and appeals mechanisms, secure validation and convert findings into training. The Implementing Partner will manage expert resources, logistics, monitoring, quality control, financial administration and reporting. The deadline is 05-Sep-2026 17:00 (GMT 3.00).

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The ILO holds an analytical draft report classifying social security court cases in Federal Iraq and identifying systemic barriers to access. The assignment is not a new study; it strengthens the draft to publishable quality, extends it to worker complaint and appeal mechanisms, validates the findings and converts them into training.

The Implementing Partner coordinates the work stream end to end, while the ILO supervises technically, secures counterpart engagement and accepts deliverables. Judicial engagement depends on the Higher Judicial Council, and administrative counterpart engagement is coordinated with the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs.

## Key Deliverables

- Strengthened analytical draft report to publishable quality.
- Extension of the analysis to mechanisms through which workers pursue complaints and appeals.
- Validated findings.
- Training based on the validated findings.
- Inception arrangements covering the case-extraction template and anonymisation protocol.
- Project management, monitoring, quality control, financial administration and reporting by the Implementing Partner.

## Capacity Building Components

- Conversion of validated findings into training.
- Potential judicial training stream, subject to Higher Judicial Council agreement.
- Equivalent substitute training stream through the judicial training institution or to court administrative staff if judicial agreement is not secured.

The fetched page states that the Implementing Partner contracts the expert resources each component requires. Specific expert qualifications, consortium requirements and eligibility conditions were not found on the fetched page.

## Executive Summary

The European Environment Agency has published an open call for tenders for a Topic Centre on Climate Risk and Resilience: Knowledge Development. The estimated total value is 1900000 EUR, the maximum contract duration is 53 MONTH, and the deadline for receipt of tenders is 17/09/2026 14:00 Europe/Copenhagen. The services will provide data, information, policy-relevant insights, knowledge exchange, resilience indicators and related data services for climate resilience work in Europe. The main CPV classification is research and development services and related consultancy services.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment aims to support progress toward a climate-resilient Europe by providing data, information and policy-relevant insights for adaptation measures, risk management and societal preparedness. The EEA highlights tracking and assessing progress on EU climate resilience as a priority.

The Topic Centre will support EU Member States with national climate risk assessments and adaptation policies. Its focus will be predominantly knowledge and networking services, with some data services, and it is expected to collaborate with another Topic Centre on Climate and Resilience: Reporting, Tools and Online Knowledge support.

## Key Deliverables

- Data, information and policy-relevant insights for climate resilience.
- Knowledge exchange supporting EU Member States in national climate risk assessments and adaptation policies.
- Relevant and actionable knowledge products for Europe.
- Knowledge and networking services.
- Some data services.
- Collaboration with the Topic Centre on Climate and Resilience: Reporting, Tools and Online Knowledge support.

## Capacity Building Components

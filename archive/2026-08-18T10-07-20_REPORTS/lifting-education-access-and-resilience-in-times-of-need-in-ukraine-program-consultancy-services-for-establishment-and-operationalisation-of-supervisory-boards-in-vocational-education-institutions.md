## Executive Summary

The World Bank procurement page announces a firm-selection consultancy for Ukraine’s Ministry of Education and Science. The assignment will support the establishment and operationalisation of at least 300 Supervisory Boards in vocational education institutions nationwide. The scope includes implementation planning, regional training seminars, an online course, legal consultations, monitoring tools, analytical reporting and policy recommendations. The submission deadline is Sep 04, 2026 14:00.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment supports the Lifting Education Access and Resilience in Times of Need in Ukraine Program. The consultant will help operationalise Supervisory Boards as governance platforms linking vocational education institutions, founders, employers and local communities.

The scope covers planning, communication, capacity building, legal and facilitation support, monitoring and feedback tools, and policy-level analytical outputs. The expected impact described on the page is strengthened institutional governance and more effective alignment between vocational education provision and labour market needs.

## Key Deliverables

- Detailed implementation plan, including scale-up plan and timeline.
- Methodology for communication, training and monitoring components.
- Proposed structure of the online course and regional training programme.
- Twenty-five regional in-person training seminars.
- Structured legal consultations and facilitation support to institutions.
- Analytical report identifying implementation challenges, trends and good practices.
- Policy brief with recommendations to the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine.
- Draft Final Completion Report and Final Completion Report.

## Capacity Building Components

- Twenty-five regional in-person training seminars, one per region, with approximately 15 participants per seminar.
- Online course designed with the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine as a standalone micro-credential.
- Training for vocational education institution directors, Supervisory Board secretaries and Supervisory Board members.
- Training content covering governance role, accountability, employer engagement, institutional autonomy and interaction among boards, founders and institutional management.
- At least 100 legal consultations and facilitation sessions for vocational education institutions and supervisory board members.

## Team Requirements

The page invites eligible consulting firms. Consultants may associate with other firms as a joint venture and/or sub-consultancy; joint venture partners are jointly and severally liable if selected. For a foreign company, a representative office, joint venture or consortium with a local firm should exist by the date of contract signing. The page states required firm experience in education sector governance, institutional development, public administration reform or related fields, plus turnover and audited financial statement requirements.

## Risks / Concerns

- Foreign companies should have a representative office, joint venture or consortium with a local firm by contract signing.
- Training venues must consider accessibility and the security situation, including access to certified shelters.
- Reports and deliverables have English and Ukrainian language submission requirements.

- Audited financial statements or verified financial documents are required.

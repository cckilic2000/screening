## Executive Summary

The Council of Europe eProc page announces a call for tenders for IT services to develop an E-Consultation Platform for Albania Central Election Commission. The work is part of the CoE-EU Joint Action Support to Electoral Cycle in Albania, implemented from July 2025 to July 2028. The selected provider will develop the platform, train CEC staff, prepare user guidelines, manuals, video tutorials and training materials, and provide ongoing IT support. The tender closes on 11/09/2026 18:00:00 GMT+03:00.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The Council of Europe is implementing the SECA Joint Action from July 2025 to July 2028. In this context, it seeks a provider to develop the E-Consultation Platform for Albania Central Election Commission.

The scope combines software/IT service delivery with capacity building and knowledge-transfer outputs for CEC staff. The page lists training sessions, user guidelines, manuals, video tutorials, training materials and ongoing IT support during implementation.

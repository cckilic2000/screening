## Executive Summary

ADB CMS has published a firm consulting opportunity for Tajikistan under package CS11 of the Resilient Livelihoods and Empowerment of Rural Women Project. The assignment will support the establishment, registration, organizational development, and sustainable operation of Women Agricultural Groups. The notice lists a USD 241,240 estimated budget, a 32-month engagement period, and a submission deadline of 03 September 2026 at 11:59 PM Manila local time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment objective covers mobilizing rural women, conducting transparent and inclusive beneficiary selection, assessing production potential, and facilitating formation, registration, and operationalization of Women Agricultural Groups. The consultant will maintain records, registration documents, and databases while supporting group governance, internal management systems, and collective production planning.

The assignment also includes coordination among Women Groups, the PIG, AMSO, local authorities, and other stakeholders; advisory and social support during initial group development; monitoring of group performance and sustainability; grievance redress outreach and complaint documentation; gradual formalization into legal entities; and preparation and signing of MoUs with the Ministry of Agriculture.

## Key Deliverables

- Mobilization and awareness raising among rural women.
- Transparent and inclusive beneficiary selection processes.
- Production-potential assessments for prospective Women Groups.
- Formation, registration, and operationalization of Women Groups.
- Women Group records, registration documents, and databases.
- Strengthened group governance, organizational capacity, and internal management systems.
- Collective production planning and income-generating activity support.
- Communication and coordination with PIG, AMSO, local authorities, and stakeholders.
- Monitoring of group performance, functionality, and sustainability.
- Community-level grievance redress outreach, complaint receipt, registration, documentation, and transmission.
- Formalization support and MoUs between the Ministry of Agriculture and newly established Women Agricultural Groups.

## Capacity Building Components

- Organizational development of production-oriented Women Agricultural Groups.
- Strengthening of group governance.
- Strengthening of organizational capacity and internal management systems.
- Advisory and social support during initial group development.
- Support for gradual formalization of mature Women Groups into sustainable legal entities.

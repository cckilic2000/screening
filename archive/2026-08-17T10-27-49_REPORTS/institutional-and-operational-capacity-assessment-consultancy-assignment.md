## Detailed Assignment Summary

The page describes PNGO as a coordination framework for the NGO sector in Palestine, with a role in public policy, coordination, networking, and civil society mobilization. It states that PNGO is implementing RAWAFED, a European Union-funded project designed to reinforce Palestinian civil society as a central actor in governance, accountability, and democratic participation.

The background explains that Palestinian civil society organizations face constraints on civic space, weakened civil society infrastructure in Gaza, shrinking freedoms, restrictions across Jerusalem and the West Bank, coordination gaps, and limited representation in policy and recovery discussions. The page states that RAWAFED is a 3-year project intended to strengthen local CSOs as actors of good governance and development through a more coordinated and resilient Palestinian civil society.

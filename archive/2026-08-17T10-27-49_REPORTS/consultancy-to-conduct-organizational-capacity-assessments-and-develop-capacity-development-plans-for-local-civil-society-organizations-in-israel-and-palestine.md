## Executive Summary

Expertise France has issued TOR for a consultancy to assess 42 local CSOs in Israel and Palestine and develop capacity development plans. The assignment covers organizational systems including governance, financial management, procurement, compliance, HR, safeguarding, and project management. The consultancy is expected to start in mid-September and last approximately six weeks, with applications submitted by 10 September through the Expertise France portal.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment forms part of the Empowering Palestinian and Israeli Civil Society and Media project led by Expertise France with EU support. The consultant will design or adapt organizational assessment tools, conduct OCAs for 42 local organizations, identify strengths, risks and capacity gaps, and produce tailored and portfolio-level capacity development plans. The work is intended to strengthen institutional resilience, operational effectiveness, sustainability, and compliance capacities of organizations operating in Israel and Palestine.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception Report with methodology, workplan, assessment framework/tools, data collection methods, and stakeholder engagement approach.
- Organizational Capacity Assessment Reports for each of the 42 organizations.
- Individual Capacity Development Plans for each organization.
- Portfolio-Level Capacity Development Plan.
- Final Consolidated Analytical Report and Presentation.

## Capacity Building Components

- Organizational capacity assessments for 42 CSOs.
- Tailored capacity development plans.
- Portfolio-level capacity development plan.
- Recommendations for group trainings, peer learning, mentoring, communities of practice, and collective learning approaches.
- Validation sessions with participating organizations.

## Team Requirements

Consultants or consulting firms may apply. Required profile includes advanced university degree; 5-7 years conducting organizational capacity assessments or organizational development for CSOs; experience designing capacity development strategies and action plans; strong analytical, facilitation and report-writing skills; experience in fragile or politically sensitive contexts; donor compliance understanding; and excellent English. Desirable qualifications include Israel/Palestine experience and Arabic and/or Hebrew.

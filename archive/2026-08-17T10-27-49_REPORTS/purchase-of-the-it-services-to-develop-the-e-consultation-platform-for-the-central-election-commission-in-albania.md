## Executive Summary

The Council of Europe has published call for tenders ID 11766 for IT services to develop the E-Consultation Platform for Albania’s Central Election Commission. The work includes platform development, CEC staff training, user guidelines, manuals, video tutorials, training materials, and ongoing IT support. The call closes on 11 September 2026 at 18:00 GMT+3.

The assignment sits under the CoE–EU Joint Action Support to Electoral Cycle in Albania, implemented from July 2025 to July 2028.

## Executive Summary

ILO Iraq has published RFQ IRQ-ILO-SP-26-001 for an implementation agreement covering social security law court cases in Federal Iraq. The work strengthens an analytical draft report, extends it to worker complaint and appeal mechanisms, validates findings, and converts results into training. The deadline is 5 September 2026 at 17:00 GMT+3.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The source page states that ILO already holds an analytical draft report classifying social security court cases in Federal Iraq. The implementing partner will strengthen the draft to publishable quality, extend it to complaint and appeal mechanisms, secure validation, and convert the validated findings into training. The partner will manage the work stream end to end, including expert contracting, convening, event logistics, project management, monitoring, quality control, financial administration, and reporting.

## Key Deliverables

- Strengthened analytical draft report to publishable quality.
- Extension of analysis to mechanisms through which workers pursue complaints and appeals.
- Validation of findings.
- Conversion of validated findings into training.
- Project management, monitoring, quality control, financial administration, and reporting for the work stream.

## Capacity Building Components

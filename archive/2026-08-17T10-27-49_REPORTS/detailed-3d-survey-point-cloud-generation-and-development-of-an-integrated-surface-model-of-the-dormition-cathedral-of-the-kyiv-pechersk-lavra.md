## Executive Summary

UNESCO has issued RFQ-UA-26-40 for a detailed 3D survey and integrated surface model of the Dormition Cathedral in Kyiv. The assignment includes terrestrial laser scanning, UAV photogrammetry, geodetic measurements, data fusion, and generation of point-cloud and textured 3D model outputs. The deadline is 25 August 2026 at 14:00 Kyiv time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment aims to accurately document the Cathedral’s current geometric condition and provide a reliable digital basis for engineering, conservation, restoration, and information-modelling activities. Services include laser scanning of accessible external and internal surfaces, UAV photogrammetry, geodetic measurements, data integration, point-cloud generation, and technical documentation. The notice invites qualified companies and individual entrepreneurs to submit separate technical and financial proposals in English.

## Key Deliverables

- Detailed terrestrial laser scanning of accessible external and internal surfaces.
- UAV photogrammetric surveying.
- Geodetic measurements.
- Integration of laser-scanning and photogrammetric data using Data Fusion.
- Integrated point cloud.
- Measurable textured 3D polygonal model (TIN).
- Horizontal and vertical sections and elevations.
- Photographic documentation and associated technical documentation.

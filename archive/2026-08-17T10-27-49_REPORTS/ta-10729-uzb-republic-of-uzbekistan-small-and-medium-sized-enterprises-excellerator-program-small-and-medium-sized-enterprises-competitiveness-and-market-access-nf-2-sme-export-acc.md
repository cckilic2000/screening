## Executive Summary

ADB CMS has published a firm consulting opportunity for the Uzbekistan SME Excellerator Program under package NF-2. The assignment will operate the SME export-acceleration function, including enterprise selection, advisory support, market-entry strategy, training, business missions, buyer matchmaking, and results tracking. The notice lists a USD 490,000 estimated budget, a 24-month engagement period, and a submission deadline of 03 September 2026 at 11:59 PM Manila local time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The objective is to operate the Program's SME export-acceleration function from enterprise selection through acceleration, training, advisory, and market entry. The operator will take Uzbek SMEs into priority export markets through market-segment analysis, market-entry strategy, a delivery roadmap, and professionally designed business missions.

The TOR covers cohort selection, inclusion targets for women- and youth-led SMEs, regional balance, modular training, coaching, mentoring, train-the-trainers for MIIT trainers, export-readiness audits, branding and certification-readiness support, buyer matchmaking, in-market delivery, follow-up, and sex-disaggregated and regionally balanced reporting.

## Key Deliverables

- SME cohort selection and management.
- Market-segment analysis for priority sectors.
- Market-entry strategy and roadmap for selected SMEs.
- Structured acceleration programme in Tashkent and regional hubs.
- Modular training, coaching, mentoring, and train-the-trainers sessions.
- Tailored advisory and export-readiness audits.
- Marketing, branding, and certification-readiness support.
- Business missions with delegate selection, market preparation, buyer matchmaking, in-market delivery, and follow-up.
- Results, lessons, and pipeline tracking with sex-disaggregated and regionally balanced reporting.

## Capacity Building Components

- Modular training for selected SMEs.
- Coaching and mentoring for selected SMEs.
- Train-the-trainers function for an annual cohort of 25-30 national trainers from MIIT.
- Three training-of-trainers sessions per year.
- Capacity building for national delivery through MIIT trainers.

## Team Requirements

Consultant type is Firm. The source is National. Expertise or positions needed include international Export Market Strategist and Business-Mission & Matchmaking Specialist, plus national Team Leader, SME Training & Capacity-Building Specialist, Monitoring & Inclusion Specialist, and an expert pool, all with Tashkent, Uzbekistan as primary place of assignment.

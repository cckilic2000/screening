## Executive Summary

The World Bank procurement notice seeks consultancy services for the nationwide establishment and operationalisation of Supervisory Boards in Ukrainian vocational education institutions. The consultant will support at least 300 boards through planning, communication, training, legal/facilitation support, monitoring, and policy recommendations. The assignment is expected to run for 12 months and uses Consultant Qualification Selection with a submission deadline of 4 September 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment supports the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine under the LEARN Program. It focuses on large-scale operationalisation of Supervisory Boards, strengthening institutional capacities of directors, secretaries, founders, and board members, and introducing monitoring and analytical tools. The expected impact is improved governance, stronger institutional management, and evidence-based policy development for vocational education reform.

## Key Deliverables

- Draft and Final Inception Report.
- Strategic Communication and Stakeholder Engagement Report.
- Capacity Building Completion Report.
- Supervisory Boards establishment report confirming activation of at least 300 Supervisory Boards.
- Policy Brief with recommendations to MoES.
- Draft and Final Completion Report.
- Monitoring/dashboard-related tools and analytical reporting as stated in the TOR.

## Capacity Building Components

- 25 regional in-person training seminars, one per region, approximately 15 participants each.
- Online course with 30 academic hours equivalent to 1 ECTS credit.
- Training content on governance and Supervisory Board operations.
- Legal consultations and facilitation support to institutions and supervisory board members.
- Certificates for participants upon successful completion.
- Adult learning and micro-credential approach.

## Team Requirements

The Consultant must be a legally registered firm or Joint Venture / Consortium of firms. Requirements include at least 6 years in education sector governance, institutional development, public administration reform, or related fields; 4 relevant assignments in capacity building, governance reform, institutional management support, or vocational education; average annual turnover of at least USD 250,000 or equivalent; and at least one IFI/bilateral donor assignment in the last 5 years. Key experts include Team Leader/Project Manager, Governance and Institutional Development Expert, Training and Capacity Building Expert, and Monitoring and Policy Analysis Expert; non-key experts include Legal Expert, Communication Specialist, IT/Dashboard Specialist, and Trainers.

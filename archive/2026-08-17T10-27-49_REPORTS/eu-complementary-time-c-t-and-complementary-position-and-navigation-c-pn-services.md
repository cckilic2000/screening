## Executive Summary

DG DEFIS has published a TED contract notice for EU Complementary Time and Complementary Position and Navigation services. The assignment will assess how the Commission can contribute to complementary PNT services and resilience against radio frequency interference. The estimated value is 500 000,00 EUR and the tender deadline is 9 October 2026.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The source page states that PNT functions underpin transport, finance, energy, and telecommunications, and that the project will assess Commission support for complementary PNT services in the next MFF. The assessment covers resilience of PNT services to radio frequency interference, mature interoperable C-T architecture, and candidate C-PN services. The page refers bidders to procurement documents for additional details.

## Key Deliverables

- Assess how to strengthen resilience of PNT services to radio frequency interference.
- Assess how to contribute to deployment of a mature, interoperable C-T architecture.
- Assess how to support candidates for a C-PN service using C-T infrastructure.

## Executive Summary

Welthungerhilfe Iraq has published tender IRQ-2026-WHH-RFQ-015 for the final evaluation of the Improved Peaceful Coexistence and Social Cohesion project in Ninewa. The ToR covers social cohesion, livelihoods, vocational training, market linkages, and capacity building of the Women Empowerment Organization. The evaluation must use mixed methods and OECD-DAC criteria and is expected to be completed within 40 days.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment is a final external evaluation of a WHH/WEO project implemented from 1 December 2024 to 31 December 2026 in Hamdaniya, Talafar, and Sinjar. The project aims to strengthen social cohesion, peaceful coexistence, and resilient livelihoods among conflict-affected returnee and host communities, with attention to women, youth, farmers, local authorities, community leaders, religious leaders, and community-based organizations.

The evaluation will assess relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, coherence, impact, and sustainability. It will use document review, quantitative data from the endline survey, key informant interviews, focus group discussions, and additional qualitative or quantitative methods proposed by the evaluator. The assignment also includes outputs such as an inception report, data collection debriefing, sensemaking session, evaluation report, evaluation brief, management response matrix, good practices and success stories, data collection tools, and raw/cleaned datasets.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report and evaluation matrix.
- Data collection debriefing notes/presentation.
- Sensemaking session PowerPoint slides and presentation.
- Draft and final evaluation report including higher-level project rating along selected evaluation criteria.
- Evaluation brief 2-pager.
- Management Response Matrix.
- At least 5 photos of the evaluation, with informed consent.
- Compilation of Good Practices and Success Stories.
- Data collection tools.
- Raw and cleaned datasets.

## Capacity Building Components

- Evaluation of WEO capacity building workshops and consultations.
- Assessment of WEO institutional capacity in livelihoods, social cohesion, finance, logistics, MEAL, HR, communication, and IT.
- Review of three one-day trainings led by WHH professionals on livelihoods, operations, and MEAL.
- Assessment of continuous support, technical guidance, and coordination from WHH staff.
- Evaluation methodology must include participatory and gender-sensitive approaches.

## Team Requirements

The page states that the evaluation may be conducted by an individual evaluator or an evaluation team. It requires an advanced university degree in Monitoring and Evaluation, Development Studies, Economics, Agricultural Sciences, Livelihoods, Rural Development, Social Sciences, or another relevant field; at least 5 years of demonstrated evaluation experience; experience with internationally recognized evaluation standards; previous experience evaluating livelihoods, agriculture, employment, vocational training, economic development, resilience, social cohesion or similar programmes; mixed-methods skills; sampling and evaluation matrix skills; and excellent written and spoken English. Fluency in Arabic is required, and Kurdish is considered an advantage or the team should include members able to conduct interviews in the local language.

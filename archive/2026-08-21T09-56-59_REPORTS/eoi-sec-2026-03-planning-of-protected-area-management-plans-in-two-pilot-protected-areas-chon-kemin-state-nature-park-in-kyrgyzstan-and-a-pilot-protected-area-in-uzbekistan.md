## Executive Summary

FAO has issued a Request for EOI for protected area management planning in Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan. The assignment will support assessment, revision, and implementation planning of Protected Area Management Plans and develop practical technical guidelines. The page explicitly includes capacity development, on-the-job coaching, knowledge transfer, biodiversity conservation, climate change adaptation and mitigation, and sustainable forest management.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

FAO seeks qualified organizations to support the assessment, revision, and implementation planning of Protected Area Management Plans for Chon-Kemin State Nature Park in Kyrgyzstan and a pilot protected area in Uzbekistan. The assignment will be implemented over 12 months through parallel and coordinated workstreams in the two countries. The selected provider will support FAO and national authorities through technical assessments, field analysis, stakeholder engagement, participatory planning, GIS and spatial analysis, zoning, action plans, implementation plans, monitoring frameworks, capacity development, coaching, knowledge transfer, and awareness-raising materials.

## Key Deliverables

- Assessment, revision, and implementation planning of Protected Area Management Plans.
- Practical Technical Guideline(s) for protected area management planning and landscape-level biodiversity connectivity.
- Technical assessments and field-based analyses.
- Zoning, action plans, implementation plans, and monitoring frameworks.
- Targeted awareness-raising and communication materials for global/international and grassroots/local audiences.

## Capacity Building Components

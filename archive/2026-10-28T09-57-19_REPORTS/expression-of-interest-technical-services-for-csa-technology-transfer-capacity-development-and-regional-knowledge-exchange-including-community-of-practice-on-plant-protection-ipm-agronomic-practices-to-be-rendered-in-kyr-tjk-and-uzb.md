## Executive Summary

FAO has issued an expression-of-interest notice for Climate Smart Agriculture services in Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan. The scope includes technology transfer, capacity development and a regional Community of Practice on plant protection, IPM and agronomic/crop-management practices. Eligible suppliers must be legally established in the Russian Federation. The deadline is 4 November 2026 at 17:30 GMT+3, and detailed mandatory requirements are available from FAO by email.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The services fall under project UNUN/SEC/028/UND and connect technical transfer, capacity development and regional exchange in three Central Asian countries. The official description specifies plant protection, integrated pest management, agronomic practices and crop management, including a Community of Practice. It does not publish a detailed TOR, output schedule, training volumes, staffing criteria, budget or duration.

The notice directs requests for the mandatory-requirements document, clarifications and final submission to Makhmud.Shaumarov@fao.org and Marziya.DavlyatovaSariaslan@fao.org. Its public attachment/bulk ZIP contains only general UNGM bidder instructions in English, French and Spanish, not the assignment-specific EOI requirements. Those instructions describe basic UNGM registration; the notice’s specific communication/submission route is email.

## Executive Summary

Geres is commissioning the final evaluation of FRESH, a programme on energy-efficient housing renovation in hot climates. The assignment covers Morocco, Benin, Mongolia and Tajikistan, with field missions in Morocco/Benin and remote review of the other components. The maximum budget is EUR 24,000 including taxes and mission costs, with a justified optional enumerator lot treated separately. Offers must reach Geres before 18 November 2026, and final deliverables are due by 28 February 2027.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The evaluation will assess relevance, coherence, effectiveness, efficiency, impact, sustainability and replication potential, using the programme’s intervention logic and participatory evidence. Particular attention is given to the newer Morocco and Benin components and to how experience from Mongolia and Tajikistan has been adapted. Questions cover accessible renovation solutions, beneficiary needs, local-market development, artisans/enterprises, financing/payment mechanisms, inclusion and climate outcomes.

Methods combine programme and financial/monitoring-document review, interviews with Geres and partners, field visits and stakeholder data collection. The TOR envisages time in Rabat and at least one of Tata or Béni Mellal in Morocco, and Cotonou with site visits in Abomey-Calavi and Parakou in Benin. Remote interviews cover other components. Findings and recommendations are to be discussed with programme stakeholders and AFD, supporting learning, adaptation and future programme development. The programme identifies 2,700 household beneficiaries (at least 20% women-headed), approximately 12,000 people, 110 artisans/building-sector employees, eighty engineering/energy/building professionals and twenty local elected or institutional staff; these are programme beneficiaries, not evaluator recruitment targets.

## Key Deliverables

- Concise inception/methodological scoping note, maximum ten pages or short presentation, with evaluation framework/tools: 18 December 2026.
- Survey/interview evidence database and draft evaluation report: 12 February 2027; field data collection must finish before 8 February.
- Final French evaluation report, maximum forty pages excluding annexes, incorporating review comments: 28 February 2027.
- Two-page synthesis using the supplied format and validated PowerPoint presentation, with the final package.
- Restitution meeting with AFD participation in March 2027.

## Capacity Building Components

- Evaluation of FRESH’s twelve training/competency-strengthening modules for partner teams and financial/building-sector actors.
- The evaluation covers the programme indicator of 1,000 artisans/building employees and/or households trained or sensitized on cooling techniques, on-site accompaniment, partner learning and public/private dissemination in at least six territories; these are programme activities, not additional evaluator training deliverables.
- Assessment of artisan/enterprise capacities, partner learning, replication and institutional uptake.
- Participatory consultation, co-development of recommendations and restitution of lessons.

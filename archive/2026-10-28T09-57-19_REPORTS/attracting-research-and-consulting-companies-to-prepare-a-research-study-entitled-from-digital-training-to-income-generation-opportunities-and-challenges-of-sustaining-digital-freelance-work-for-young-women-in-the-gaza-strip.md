## Executive Summary

Women Affairs Center Gaza is procuring a study on the transition from digital training to sustainable freelance income for young women. The UNDP-funded assignment targets women aged twenty to thirty-five across Gaza, subject to access and security conditions. It requires primary research, an Arabic study with full English translation, bilingual executive summaries, anonymized data and a results presentation. Sealed technical and financial quotations are due at WAC on 1 November 2026 at 14:00.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The research will identify factors affecting the progression from skills training to client access, paid assignments and sustainable income. It will compare women with different professional outcomes, assess barriers and the role of support services, and recommend improvements to digital economic-empowerment programmes. The company determines and justifies sample size, selection and geographic distribution; women with disabilities are to be included where possible.

Tasks include an inception meeting, literature review, approved research design and quantitative/qualitative tools, primary data collection and at least five interviews with women representing different market-entry outcomes. Analysis must explain limitations and distinguish correlation from causality. Informed consent, confidentiality, privacy protection, scientific/language editing and editable handover are explicit conditions. The timetable covers inception in early November, draft analysis during November and final delivery/presentation in early December.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report with research questions, conceptual framework, methodology, sample, tools, workplan and timetable.
- Approved questionnaires, interview/focus-group guides and other research tools.
- Draft study for WAC review and final scientifically/language-edited Arabic study, maximum 12,000 words including references and annexes.
- Complete professional English translation and executive summaries in Arabic and English.
- Dataset stripped of personal identifiers, research tools, analytical outputs and tables.
- Professional 15–20-slide presentation in PPTX and PDF.
- At least one facilitated findings/discussion session, presentation and minutes documenting feedback/recommendations.
- Three progress reports: end of first November week, end of third November week and with final delivery in early December; all approved final files editable.

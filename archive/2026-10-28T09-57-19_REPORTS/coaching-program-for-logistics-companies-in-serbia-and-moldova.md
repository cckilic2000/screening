## Executive Summary

IFC is seeking a firm for a logistics-company coaching programme in Serbia and Moldova. The programme targets twenty companies in Serbia and up to fifteen in Moldova, building on MoveUp training. Delivery combines individual and group coaching, practical actions, performance tracking and post-programme follow-up. Expressions of interest are due in English through WBGeProcure RFx Now by 11 November 2026 at 23:59 EST.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The assignment is part of IFC’s EUR Logistics Services Project and its private-sector capacity pillar. Selected companies will receive tailored support on operational and managerial challenges such as distribution, warehouse management, inventory planning, process optimization and digitalization readiness. The provider will help identify needs, organize thematic groups of no more than eight participants and deliver a structured sequence of sessions.

The model includes an initial individual session of about one hour per company, five group sessions of about two hours at fortnightly intervals, and a final individual session of about one hour. Participants implement agreed actions between sessions, using practical tools and indicators. The provider supplies the online platform/licenses, quality assurance, progress notes and country-based reports, and supports the local implementing partner’s survey six months after coaching.

## Key Deliverables

- Country-based Coaching Program Design Report and Engagement Plan: methodology, introductory-meeting materials, application/selection instruments, company needs, group composition, coach allocation, onboarding, calendar and indicator framework.
- Country-based Coaching Implementation Report: sessions delivered, tools, progress/actions, participant feedback, issues and dropouts.
- Country-based post-implementation/final reporting, including company outcomes, lessons, case-study evidence and follow-up survey findings.
- Practical templates/checklists/reference materials; session progress notes and company-level action/indicator records.
- Support to the six-month post-completion survey and monitoring/evaluation data collection.

## Capacity Building Components

- Individual and peer-group coaching tailored to company needs and concrete logistics/supply-chain challenges.
- Five approximately two-hour group sessions, plus initial/final individual sessions of about one hour per company.
- Applied tasks, tools, templates, checklists and follow-up action plans for daily business use.
- Peer learning, leadership/change-management support and follow-up assessment of adoption.

## Executive Summary

ADB is procuring an international firm for sustainability reporting and ISSB readiness support to TSTX in Uzbekistan. The published consulting services budget is USD 177,000, and the CSRN profile specifies an eight-month engagement. Outputs include SASB metrics, IFRS S1/S2 gap analysis and draft disclosures, double materiality assessment, ESG risk tools and integrated reporting materials. Proposals are due on 18 November 2026 at 16:00 Manila time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The consultant will establish a decision-useful sustainability disclosure framework for TSTX, addressing governance, strategy, risk management, metrics, targets, data controls and reporting responsibilities. The nine work packages cover inception, SASB applicability, ISSB readiness, double materiality, ESG risk, IFRS S1 disclosures, IFRS S2 climate disclosures, integrated reporting and training/handover.

The assignment must use approved outputs from TOR #1 and TOR #2 rather than repeat their climate-strategy, greenhouse-gas inventory, MRV or green-financing work. Editable matrices, registers, manuals and source files must be accepted by ADB and usable internally by TSTX; management validation and a Board briefing form part of the handover.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report, workplan, information-request protocol, coordination and non-duplication arrangements.
- SASB applicability assessment, content index, accounting-metric definitions and evidence/data mapping.
- IFRS S1/S2 readiness and gap matrix with implementation roadmap and assigned responsibilities.
- Stakeholder mapping, engagement evidence and double-materiality assessment/matrix.
- ESG risk and opportunity register, prioritization/heatmap and integration recommendations.
- Draft IFRS S1 disclosures and action plan; IFRS S2 climate-risk, scenario and disclosure package.
- Integrated sustainability reporting framework, reporting manual/calendar, disclosure controls, draft report and editable reporting tools.
- Practical training, validation workshop, Board briefing and complete editable handover package; documented independent quality-review statements with draft deliverables.
- Final Integrated Package and Completion Report at month 8, with final reports, tools, registers, roadmap, editable files and handover. The TOR schedule places inception at M1, SASB at M2, ISSB gaps at M3, DMA/ESG at M4, IFRS S2 at M6, IFRS S1 at M7 and reporting/training/final handover at M8.

## Capacity Building Components

- Practical training on SASB, IFRS S1, IFRS S2, materiality, double materiality, ESG risk assessment, disclosure controls and report preparation.
- Reporting ownership, data lineage, internal controls, calendars, templates and registers for continuing institutional use.
- Validation workshop, management engagement, Board briefing and knowledge transfer through editable tools and handover materials.

## Team Requirements

- International Team Leader/Sustainability Reporting Specialist: 10+ years in sustainability reporting, ESG advisory and institutional strengthening, with ISSB implementation experience.
- ISSB/IFRS S1 and S2 Specialist: 7+ years; SASB/Industry Metrics Specialist: 5+ years; Double Materiality/Stakeholder Specialist: 7+ years; ESG Risk Specialist: 7+ years; Climate Risk/Scenario Specialist: 7+ years.
- National Data/Controls/Reporting Specialist: 5+ years; National Legal/Institutional Specialist: 7+ years in relevant Uzbekistan assignments; Editor/Reporting Designer with demonstrated corporate/sustainability reporting and multilingual publication experience.
- A separate Quality Reviewer must remain outside the delivery team. Roles may be combined only with demonstrated qualifications, capacity, local knowledge and independent review.
- Two international missions to Tashkent are required. The standard contract provides firm, joint-venture and association options; a mandatory consortium requirement was not found.
- CSRN specifies international firm selection. The Data Sheet permits personnel from any country, subject to the stated UN-related restrictions.
- The RFP Data Sheet specifies minimum international input of 6.6 person-months (1.5 each for Team Leader and ISSB specialist; 0.9 each for the other four international roles) and national input of 3.3 person-months (1.1 per national role), conflicting with the TOR’s indicative totals.
- Deliverables are in English. For pricing, Russian translation is specified for the draft sustainability report, reporting manual and capacity-building materials; selected Uzbek/Russian versions otherwise depend on agreement with the client.

## Risks / Concerns

- Duration is inconsistent: the profile and TOR heading state eight months, while TOR §8 states “68 calendar months”.
- Staffing quantities are inconsistent: the TOR lists 221 indicative days but also “approximately 8.3 person-months (116 international, 66 national)”; the portal’s staffing estimates differ. These figures require clarification.
- Deliverables depend on TSTX data and other TOR outputs. Specified input delays extend delivery dates without increasing the lump-sum price.
- The TOR excludes corporate climate strategy, GHG inventory, MRV-system development, green-financing framework, external assurance, certification and formal legal/compliance opinions.
- The proposal must provide an independent reviewer and demonstrate the specialist experience listed in the TOR.
- English reporting and the specified Russian translation costs must be included. Clarifications are due by 9 November 2026.

## Executive Summary

The Clean Hydrogen JU is procuring a four-year Hydrogen Startup and Scaleup Hub with an estimated value of EUR 10 million excluding VAT. The Hub must support at least fifty hydrogen ventures through accelerator, growth and scaleup programmes. Services include technology development, business-case validation, investment readiness, an Innovation Corridor and a digital platform. Tenders are due on 25 January 2027 at 16:00 Brussels time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The contractor will bridge funding and technology barriers for hydrogen startups and scaleups and help them become commercially viable. Three support programmes cover early ventures, operational pilots and pre-commercial scaling, accompanied by a cross-border Innovation Corridor and outreach activities. At least three calls for expressions of interest must be launched within the first three years, with transparent selection, formal feedback and tailored support to at least fifty ventures.

The service includes investor readiness, intellectual-property and due-diligence support, business models, industrial partnerships, offtake arrangements and access to financing. Optional direct financial support is funded within the contract price: up to EUR 100,000 per startup under Task 1 and EUR 200,000 under Tasks 2/3, with Task 3 funding exceptional and justified. The contractor is responsible for administering and monitoring those schemes.

A secure Microsoft Azure-based platform will support calls, applications, selection and the one-stop-shop. Annex 6 requires source-code access, portability, security, operational documentation, deployment/testing and handover sufficient for a third party to operate the system. Progress monitoring covers supported firms, technological advances/patents and funding raised.

## Key Deliverables

- Inception report at month 3, including full task methodology, communication strategy, first-call application documents, selection criteria and evaluation templates.
- Three or more calls for expressions of interest within years 1–3; application guidelines, selection/evaluation reports and feedback to applicants.
- Tailored accelerator, startup-growth and scaleup support for at least fifty hydrogen ventures, with company progress and funding records.
- Innovation Corridor outputs, including case studies, templates, guidance and links to facilities, investors and industrial partners.
- Communication campaigns and annual in-person event, success-story/startup-award activities and publishable event summaries.
- Secure Azure-based digital platform, technical architecture, operating-cost model, source code, test/acceptance materials, deployment/security/operating documentation and exit/handover materials.
- Intermediary reports at months 12, 24 and 36, including confidential/publishable versions and presentation slides; final report and remaining public materials at month 48.
- Knowledge transfer and platform handover during the final two months; monitoring of direct lump-sum support and publication of call outcomes where financial support is used.

## Capacity Building Components

- Tailored business, technology and investment-readiness support; due-diligence preparation, IPR guidance and commercialization assistance.
- Founder exchanges, peer strategic mentoring, knowledge-sharing activities and hydrogen ecosystem capacity building.
- Training materials drawn from programme lessons and expert support across the hydrogen value chain.
- Client-staff training, technical documentation and application knowledge transfer during the final two months.
- Internal training programmes to maintain consistent service quality across the delivery team.

## Team Requirements

- Average annual turnover above EUR 2,500,000 over the last two closed financial years.
- At least three similar projects completed in the previous three years, each at least EUR 500,000; relevant experience must cover startup/scaleup development/ecosystems, financing/investment, investor or VC involvement and hydrogen technologies.
- Capacity in two EU official languages, including English at C2, and English reporting experience demonstrated through completed projects.
- At least one Project Manager with five years’ experience. Experts require a university graduate-level education, at least three years’ professional experience and English at least C1.
- Expert coverage includes hydrogen technology, investor readiness, business cases, startup support, financial/legal matters, online platforms, training, communication and language quality; one expert may cover multiple fields.
- Joint tenders and subcontracting are permitted under the administrative rules; participation is subject to the stated EU procurement-access and Horizon Europe third-country rules. No mandatory consortium is prescribed.

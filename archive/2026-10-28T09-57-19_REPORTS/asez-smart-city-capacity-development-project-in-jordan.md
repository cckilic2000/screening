## Executive Summary

JICA has announced consultant procurement for the ASEZ Smart City Capacity Development Project in Jordan. The assignment covers smart-city planning, ASEZA institutional capacity, three pilots and training. The base estimate ceiling is JPY 196,066,000 excluding tax, separately quoted items and specified fixed allocations; the latter total JPY 45,405,000 excluding tax. Proposals and estimates are due on 24 November 2026 at 12:00.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The project will support ASEZA in establishing the institutional and human capabilities needed to plan and implement smart-city development in Aqaba. It includes baseline work across priority fields, stakeholder/technical working arrangements, a master plan with vision, strategy, roadmap and implementation actions, review of organizational roles and capacity, and a capacity-development programme. Planning is to consider gender and engagement with private actors/startups.

Three pilots will demonstrate practical applications in selected smart-city fields, followed by monitoring, improvement and dissemination. OJT is integrated through delivery, and two Japan-based training rounds are envisaged, each for approximately ten participants over approximately ten days including travel. Local subcontracting is permitted for pilot implementation; fixed allocations cover the pilots and specified Japan-training work. The indicative consultant input is 36.53 person-months, including 3.80 person-months for Japan-training-related work. The TOR states that Japan-training implementation will use a separate contract; the solicitation nevertheless specifies related preparation/accompaniment inputs and fixed allocations.

## Key Deliverables

- Business implementation plan within ten working days of contract conclusion and initial work plan approximately one month after commencement.
- Baseline/smart-city assessment, institutional/organizational capacity assessment and capacity-development plan.
- Smart-city master plan: vision, strategy, priorities/roadmap, action and implementation arrangements, governance/roles, financing/budget considerations and approval support.
- Relevant policy/regulatory recommendations and stakeholder coordination/technical working-group materials.
- Design, implementation, monitoring and improvement of three smart-city pilots, with outputs and lessons documented.
- OJT, training/seminar materials and Japan-training-related planning/support for two rounds.
- Progress/technical and dissemination materials as required, final consultancy completion report in Japanese and project completion report in Japanese/English, with final source outputs.
- English monitoring sheets every six months; draft final consultancy/project reports approximately three months before contract expiry; collected documents/data organized with an inventory and clear usage rights for pre-existing material.

## Capacity Building Components

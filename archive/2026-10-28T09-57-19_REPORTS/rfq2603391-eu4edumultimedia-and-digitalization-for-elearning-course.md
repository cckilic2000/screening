## Executive Summary

UNDP Moldova is procuring multimedia production and digitalization for an EU4EDU career-guidance course. The assignment comprises twelve Romanian-language modules, multimedia assets, assessment, SCORM packaging and LMS integration. It is scheduled over a maximum of six months, with final editable materials and testing documentation due in April 2027. Quotations must be submitted through Quantum by 10 November 2026 at 10:30 New York time.

## Detailed Assignment Summary

The course will strengthen the ability of school leaders, teachers and psychologists to support career guidance. A separately engaged Career Guidance Consultant provides the subject curriculum and raw content; this contractor supplies the instructional architecture, storyboards, graphic design, audiovisual production, interactive digital modules, assessment and technical integration. Each module contains two units, with short instructional and practical activities, and the platform must support learner navigation, progress and completion.

Delivery includes a five-module prototype, a complete twelve-module draft and final validated course, Moodle-compatible SCORM integration, technical testing and handover of editable source materials. The RFQ places responsibility on the contractor for coordination, technical quality, accessibility and incorporation of UNDP feedback. All course deliverables must be in Romanian. The RFQ estimates approximately thirty total learner hours, subject to final instructional design, including individual study and practical application.
